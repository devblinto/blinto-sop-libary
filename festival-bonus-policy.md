# Festival Bonus Policy

## Purpose

Explain Blinto's festival bonus arrangements in clear terms: who is eligible, how bonuses are calculated, when they are paid, and how employees can ask for a correction.

Festival bonuses are separate from ordinary monthly salary and from **paid festival holidays** under the Leave & Time-Off Policy.

## Scope

This policy explains Blinto's proposed festival bonus benefit for employees. The company benefit described here is normally intended for **confirmed employees**. For any worker covered by Bangladesh labour law, statutory eligibility and any stronger contractual entitlement must also be honored, irrespective of the employee's internal label.

Interns and non-employee contractors do not automatically receive Blinto's company festival bonus; any applicable legal entitlement or express agreement takes precedence.

## Festival Bonus at a Glance

| Item | Blinto's proposed company rule |
| --- | --- |
| Number of bonuses | **Two per calendar year** |
| Festivals | **Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha** |
| Standard company eligibility | **Confirmed employees**, subject to statutory and contractual rights |
| Amount per festival | **50% of the employee's contractual gross monthly salary** |
| Payment | **With the salary of the relevant festival month**, subject to any earlier legal payment obligation |
| Payment method | Bank transfer through normal payroll |
| Payroll presentation | Shown separately from ordinary monthly salary |
| Administration | HR verifies eligibility; Finance calculates and processes payment |

This policy was approved by Blinto management on 12 November 2026. Its provisions remain subject to applicable law and binding employment terms.

## Bonus Amount and Calculation

For the additional Blinto company festival bonus, the proposed calculation is:

**Festival bonus for each eligible festival = contractual gross monthly salary × 50%.**

For example:

| Particulars | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Contractual gross monthly salary | ৳40,000 |
| Eid-ul-Fitr bonus (50%) | ৳20,000 |
| Eid-ul-Adha bonus (50%) | ৳20,000 |
| **Total company bonus for both festivals** | **৳40,000** |

Blinto currently states salary in appointment letters as a **single gross amount**, rather than consistently showing a separate basic-salary component. The gross amount is the proposed basis for this **company benefit**; it is **not automatically the legally defined basic wage**.

HR and Finance must check any applicable statutory festival bonus using the legally required eligibility and wage basis. If the company calculation would leave an employee with less than a statutory or stronger contractual entitlement, the higher enforceable entitlement applies. An employee should not lose a legally required amount because Blinto's salary statement does not separately identify basic wages.

## Eligibility and Confirmation

Blinto's proposed additional company benefit normally applies to employees whose employment has been confirmed at the relevant festival bonus eligibility date.

A probationary employee is not automatically entitled to the **additional company benefit solely by being on probation**. However, probation, delayed confirmation letters, or an incorrect HRMS label must not be used to withhold a bonus that is already legally or contractually owed.

Under Bangladesh Labour Rules 2015, **Rule 111(5)** provides two festival bonuses for qualifying covered workers with at least **one year of continuous employment**. Its basic-wage limit and any applicable amendments or industry-specific provisions must be verified and applied by HR and Finance. This statutory rule is separate from Blinto's proposed confirmed-employee rule.

HR checks the actual date of joining, applicable employment status, continuous service, contract, and relevant statutory rights before communicating eligibility. No automatic pro-rata company benefit is promised under this policy unless required by law, contract, or separately approved terms.

## Resignation and Notice Period

**Blinto's proposed company rule:** An employee who has already resigned, or is serving a resignation notice period on the relevant eligibility date, is **not eligible for an additional discretionary company festival bonus** under this policy.

**Important exception:** This exclusion does **not** cancel a festival bonus already earned, due under an employment contract, or required by applicable law. A person is not disqualified from a statutory bonus merely because they have resigned or are serving notice. HR must review the actual entitlement before excluding an employee.

If an eligible amount remains unpaid at separation, it will be considered under the **Final Settlement & Employee Dues Policy**. The employee may ask HR to explain the eligibility date and reason for any disputed exclusion.

The precise company eligibility cutoff (for example, the bonus payroll cutoff or the festival date) requires management approval and must be communicated consistently. Until that is approved, HR must not invent or apply an undisclosed cutoff.

## Payment Timing

Blinto proposes paying the bonus **together with the salary of the festival month**, with the bonus shown as a separate amount.

Where a statute, applicable agreement, or other binding requirement calls for an earlier festival bonus payment, **the earlier lawful deadline applies**. Finance must verify this before the first implementation of the policy. A normal salary-payment date must not be used to delay legally due festival benefits.

## Leave, Absence, and Other Exceptions

- **Approved paid leave** does not, by itself, remove festival bonus eligibility.
- **Long unpaid leave** requires an individual review of actual service, contractual terms, and any applicable law; HR will explain the outcome.
- **Unauthorized absence** may be addressed through the separate Attendance and Corrective Action policies. It does not automatically justify withholding an otherwise earned statutory bonus.
- **Contractual exceptions or stronger benefits** will be reviewed and honored where applicable.
- Employees may ask HR to correct inaccurate dates, status records, or bonus calculations.

## Fair Treatment Across Religious Observances

Blinto currently schedules its two standard company bonuses around Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha. Employees must be treated fairly and without unlawful religious discrimination.

Employees who observe other religious festivals may raise questions about the relevant bonus timing or any legally required accommodation with HR. The company must ensure that its festival bonus practices satisfy applicable law and individual entitlements, rather than assuming every employee observes Eid.

## Responsibilities and Questions

**Employees** may review their bonus statement and raise questions with HR about eligibility, service dates, payment dates, or calculations.

**People Operations / HR** verifies service and employment records, explains eligibility, and reviews disputed cases.

**Finance** calculates and pays eligible amounts, shows the bonus separately from salary, and arranges Finance management approval for bonus payroll disbursement.

Employees who believe a bonus is missing or incorrectly calculated should contact HR. HR reviews the relevant records with Finance and communicates the outcome. Employees retain any statutory rights to raise a wage-related claim.
