# Employment Classification & Types Policy

## Purpose

Help everyone at Blinto understand the type of employment or engagement they have, the terms they should receive in writing, the benefits and protections that may apply, and how their status may change.

Your classification should be clear from the beginning of your relationship with Blinto. If your appointment letter, HRMS record, or actual work arrangement appears inconsistent, you can ask People Operations (HR) to review it.

## Scope

This policy explains Blinto's usual arrangements for regular employees, employees serving probation, paid interns, and freelancers, consultants, or independent contractors. It applies to other arrangements if Blinto introduces them, subject to applicable law and written terms.

**The actual nature of the work and applicable law—not just a title or contract label—determine statutory rights.** No classification or agreement may remove a right that the law protects.

## Employment Types at Blinto

| Blinto engagement type | What it means for you | Main document |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Probationary employee** | You are employed while suitability and performance are assessed over the applicable probation period. | Appointment letter and Probation, Evaluation & Confirmation Policy |
| **Permanent / confirmed employee** | You hold a continuing employment position, subject to lawful terms and applicable statutory status. | Appointment letter and applicable company policies |
| **Paid intern** | You join a defined learning and supervised-work program with payment or stipend terms stated in writing. | Internship offer/agreement and Internship Program SOP |
| **Freelancer / consultant / independent contractor** | You provide specified services under an agreed engagement. This label alone does not determine legal employment status. | Written services or consultancy agreement |

Blinto currently identifies these four engagement types as its normal arrangements. A temporary, fixed-term, part-time, apprenticeship, or other arrangement should not be created informally: HR must confirm the lawful classification and appropriate written terms first.

## Probationary Employment

Blinto's **usual planned probation period is three months**, stated in the appointment letter. The duration, any permitted extension, and the employee's legal status must follow applicable law and the actual work performed.

During probation:

- your Department Head should explain your role, job-specific KPIs, and expected work standards;
- you should receive appropriate feedback and a formal evaluation before the applicable probation period ends;
- HR should communicate the internal confirmation decision and any permitted next steps in writing;
- a probation extension must have a legally valid basis and must not be treated as automatic merely because a review or letter was delayed; and
- you retain leave, pay, workplace protection, and other rights for which you are eligible.

Detailed evaluation, extension, and decision rules belong to the separate **Probation, Evaluation & Confirmation Policy**. Confirmation does **not** trigger a salary review or increment; the first annual salary review date follows the joining-date rule in that policy.

## Permanent / Confirmed Employment

A permanent or confirmed employee works in an ongoing position under the applicable appointment terms and policies. HR should provide written confirmation of the internal decision and update the employment record promptly.

Employees should not assume that a missing confirmation letter means they remain legally probationary indefinitely. HR will verify the actual legal position, service dates, and employment terms when a status is disputed.

Permanent employees receive the applicable statutory entitlements and approved Blinto benefits according to eligibility. Specific leave, attendance, working-time, compensation, resignation, and settlement rules remain in their respective policies.

## Paid Internships

Blinto currently uses **paid internships** as a reported engagement type. The internship offer or agreement should clearly state the program duration, payment or stipend, role, supervisor or mentor, and expected learning outcomes.

The approved [Internship Program](./internship-program.md) describes a usual three-month program, mentoring, structured learning, and evaluation. Completion of an internship **does not guarantee** permanent employment.

If an intern is offered regular employment, Blinto must issue a **separate approved employment offer/appointment** and explain the new employment terms. The ordinary [Hiring](./hiring.md) approval flow applies.

**Policy alignment note:** The currently approved Internship Program v1.0 also refers to possible unpaid internships. Your current decision identifies paid interns only. People Operations must reconcile any inconsistent internship categories with the approved Internship Program, confirm the applicable terms and follow Bangladesh law before entering an engagement. Any internship arrangement must comply with the applicable law and the actual nature of the work.

## Freelancers, Consultants & Independent Contractors

Freelancers and consultants work under the scope, duration, deliverables, payment terms, confidentiality duties, and other conditions in their written engagement agreements.

Blinto should not promise that all employee-only benefits automatically apply to independent contractors. Equally, a person is **not excluded from statutory worker protections merely because their agreement says “consultant” or “freelancer.”** HR must review cases where the real working relationship indicates that employment laws may apply.

Contractors who access company or client information must follow the applicable confidentiality, conduct, and security rules. They should receive clear directions about whom to contact for work-related concerns.

## Your Written Employment Terms

Before starting regular employment, you should receive an appointment letter or applicable written agreement explaining, as relevant:

- your role, employment type, start date, and reporting relationship;
- the duration of any genuine fixed-term arrangement, where applicable;
- salary, pay arrangements, and applicable benefits;
- probation and confirmation terms, where applicable;
- work location, normal schedule, and relevant office policies;
- applicable resignation or notice terms; and
- the company policies and handbook relevant to your role.

For workers covered by section 5 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, an **appointment letter and photographic identity card are employer obligations**. The Labour Rules require prescribed appointment particulars. HR should also explain where you can find and acknowledge Blinto's employee policies through the approved onboarding process.

If any written term is unclear, contact HR before accepting or acting on a material change.

## Leave and Benefits Across Employment Types

Your entitlement depends on your **actual legal eligibility**, written terms, and any more favorable approved company benefit—not solely on whether HRMS displays “probationary” or “confirmed.”

- **Probationary employees** retain any paid casual leave, sick leave, earned leave, and other protections that apply to them by law or approved terms. Advance annual leave is subject to the separate Leave & Time-Off Policy.
- **Permanent employees** receive the statutory and approved company benefits for which they qualify.
- **Interns** receive the terms of the Internship Program and their agreements, together with any rights arising from their actual legal classification.
- **Contractors and consultants** receive their contractual entitlements plus any non-waivable protections that legally apply to their working relationship.

HR should explain any important eligibility difference during onboarding and respond to requests for a correction of a leave or benefit balance.

For details, use the **Leave & Time-Off Policy**, **Office Hours Policy**, and other applicable employment policies; this document does not reproduce their formulas or approval workflows.

## Changes to Employment Status or Terms

Possible changes include:

- probationary employment progressing to confirmed/permanent status after applicable assessment and legal requirements;
- an intern receiving a separate approved offer for regular employment;
- a lawful transition from temporary to permanent status;
- a fixed-term arrangement being renewed with appropriate written terms; and
- a change of role, hours, or employment type confirmed in writing.

A material change to salary, employment type, role, or applicable terms must be communicated by HR through proper written documentation and follow Blinto's authorized approval process. A manager's informal verbal statement is not, by itself, a substitute for the required documentation or approvals.

**A legal change of status may occur by operation of law even if documentation is late.** HR must correct its records when the facts and law require it.

## Employment Records and Clarification

HR maintains Blinto's official employment records. Employees may ask HR to review or correct an inaccurate employment type, probation end date, confirmation status, or benefits classification in HRMS.

You should receive an explanation of the applicable classification and, where needed, corrected written employment information. An unresolved disagreement may be escalated through the appropriate employee grievance or legal channels.

Employment records and personal information should be handled confidentially and only shared with authorized people for legitimate purposes.

## Responsibilities

**Employees and engaged team members** should provide accurate joining information, review their employment terms, follow applicable policies, and raise classification questions promptly.

**Department Heads** explain the role and expectations, provide evidence-based feedback during probation, and notify HR when a material change is proposed.

**People Operations / HR** explains classification and benefit eligibility, issues or coordinates proper written terms, verifies legal status, communicates decisions, and maintains accurate HRMS records.

**Authorized leadership** approves offers and material employment changes according to Blinto's applicable hiring and employment-decision process.

## Exceptions & Employee Rights

If company practice, an agreement, or an HRMS label conflicts with mandatory Bangladesh employment law, the applicable legal protection takes precedence. Any stronger lawful contractual or company benefit remains protected.

HR should seek legal guidance when a classification is uncertain, especially for clerical probation, temporary/contract arrangements, internship status, and contractor relationships that resemble employment.
