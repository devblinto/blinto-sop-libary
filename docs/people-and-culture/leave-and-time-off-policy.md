# Leave & Time-Off Policy

## Purpose

Explain which kinds of leave Blinto provides, how employees request leave, how People Operations makes decisions, and what happens to unused leave. The rules should be fair, understandable, and consistent with applicable Bangladesh law and employment terms.

For the steps to request leave, coordinate with a team, and hand over work, see [Leave Request & Handover](./leave-request-and-handover.md).

## Scope & Core Principles

This policy applies to Blinto employees according to their legal employment classification, contract, and applicable entitlements. Intern arrangements are assessed separately as required by law and approved terms.

- Legal and contractual leave rights cannot be reduced by HRMS settings, manager discretion, or this policy.
- HR is the final approver for ordinary planned Annual Leave (AL/EL) and Casual Leave (CL). Department Leads advise on coverage.
- An available balance does **not** automatically mean a particular date has been approved. Employees must wait for HR's decision on planned leave.
- Genuine sickness, maternity and other protected absences are not treated as ordinary discretionary leave requests.
- HR records reasons for rejected requests and allows employees to ask for a review.
- No employee should lose a statutory entitlement merely because the HRMS balance is wrong or a technical feature is unavailable.

## Leave at a Glance

| Leave type | Blinto baseline / existing benefit | Paid? | Normal treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Annual / Earned Leave (AL/EL)** | **14 working days per calendar year for planning**, or the higher applicable legal/contractual entitlement | Yes | Full-day requests only; accrual and first-year eligibility rules apply; HR approval required |
| **Casual Leave (CL)** | **10 working days per calendar year** | Yes | Full-day or half-day (0.5 day); request at least 3 calendar days ahead when foreseeable; exceptions reviewed |
| **Sick Leave (SL)** | **14 days per calendar year** | Yes, where eligible | Full-day requests only; inform HR/Lead promptly; medical evidence when applicable |
| **Maternity Leave** | Applicable statutory/contractual entitlement, including **120 calendar days where eligible** | As legally/contractually required | Protected category, handled confidentially |
| **Paternity Leave** | **7 working days per qualifying event** | Yes | Existing company benefit; eligibility confirmed by HR |
| **Bereavement Leave** | **3 working days per qualifying event** | Yes | Existing company benefit for qualifying immediate-family loss |
| **Wedding Leave** | **3 working days for employee's own wedding** | Yes | Existing company benefit; request at least 20 calendar days ahead when foreseeable |
| **Festival / public holidays** | Official Blinto annual holiday calendar, meeting applicable statutory minimums | Yes where applicable | Does not normally deduct from working-day AL/CL balance |
| **Leave Without Pay (LWP)** | Case-by-case | No, unless management lawfully decides otherwise | Manual HR review, documented decision |

The 14-day AL amount is a planning baseline, **not a ceiling** on statutory earned leave. Eligibility and balances must be computed individually.

## Leave Year, Counting & HRMS Records

The normal leave year is **January 1–December 31**. HRMS shows the employee's opening balance, accrued entitlement, carried-forward leave, used/booked days, advance leave (if any), and available balance.

Blinto normally deducts only scheduled working days for AL, CL, paternity, bereavement, and wedding leave. Its existing no-sandwich-leave benefit is preserved. A legally defined calendar-day entitlement, including maternity leave, follows the applicable calendar-day rules.

If the legal counting method differs, HR must ensure that the employee receives at least the entitlement required by law or the stronger approved company benefit.

**Permitted request units:** Casual Leave may be requested in **half-day (0.5 working day)** or full-day units. Annual/Earned Leave and Sick Leave are requested in **full-day units only** under the normal HRMS leave-request process. This administrative rule does not remove an employee's statutory medical or leave rights. If an employee needs to leave for part of a day because of illness or an urgent need, HR must review an appropriate lawful attendance exception or other applicable arrangement rather than automatically treating the time as misconduct or requiring an unjustified full-day deduction.

For example, when five scheduled working days fall between two weekends, Blinto normally deducts five working days from AL rather than nine calendar days.

## Medical Prescription for Casual, Annual & Advance Annual Leave

A **permanent or confirmed employee** applying for **Casual Leave (CL), Annual Leave (AL/EL), or Advance Annual Leave** for a medical reason **may be asked by HR to submit a relevant medical prescription or other reasonable supporting medical documentation** to assess the circumstances of the request.

- This is a **case-by-case request**, not a mandatory medical-prescription requirement for every CL, AL, or advance AL application. Employees taking ordinary personal or vacation leave are not expected to provide medical records merely to use their eligible leave.
- HR should explain why supporting evidence is needed and request only information reasonably relevant to the leave decision. Detailed diagnoses or unrelated medical history should not be demanded without a valid reason.
- Employees may explain if a prescription is unavailable and provide another appropriate supporting document where reasonable; HR will review the circumstances fairly.
- Medical information must be handled confidentially and accessed only by authorized personnel.
- Providing a prescription **does not automatically approve a leave request**, and lack of a prescription must not automatically cancel a statutory or contractual leave entitlement. The ordinary HR approval, balance, notice, and exception rules continue to apply.
- **Sick Leave** remains governed by its separate medical-certificate and eligibility rules in this policy. This section does not replace or weaken those requirements.

## Responsible Use of Leave and Leave Categories

Employees should use each type of leave responsibly and for the purpose for which it is provided. **Sick Leave (SL) is intended for genuine illness, medical treatment, or medically necessary recovery**, not for ordinary personal errands, vacations, or other reasons unrelated to illness. Employees should select the appropriate category in HRMS, give accurate information, and provide reasonable supporting evidence when required under this policy and applicable law.

Casual Leave (CL) is for eligible personal or unexpected needs; Annual/Earned Leave (AL/EL) is for eligible planned rest and personal time, subject to their respective rules. A leave request should not be deliberately classified as sick leave merely to preserve an employee's CL or AL balance. HR may seek an explanation and proportionate supporting documents where there is a specific, reasonable basis to question the category. A legitimate illness must not be presumed dishonest merely because an employee has used multiple sick-leave days.

## Annual Leave (AL / Earned Leave)

### Entitlement and Accrual

- Blinto uses **14 working days** as the ordinary annual planning baseline, pro-rated where relevant for employees joining during the year.
- For eligible adult workers covered by section 117 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, earned leave is calculated at **1 day for every 18 qualifying days worked** after satisfying the applicable continuous-service requirement. Legal deemed-work days must also be recognized.
- If the statutory, contractual, or other approved entitlement is higher, the higher entitlement applies.
- HRMS must show employees an understandable available balance, while retaining a separate statutory-accrual and reconciliation ledger.
- For a new joiner who has completed probation but not one year of service, Blinto may permit leave from an already available **company-granted** AL balance. **This first-year company-granted balance is for paid time off only: it is not eligible for Blinto's voluntary cash encashment before one year of employment, including when the employee resigns or otherwise separates before that anniversary.** This does not extinguish any payment already required by law or a binding employment term. The benefit does not replace the statutory service requirement where that requirement applies.

### Advance Annual Leave After One Year

After completing **one year of continuous employment**, an employee may request advance AL even if the employee does not have enough currently accrued AL.

- Advance AL requires HR approval after the Department Lead confirms coverage and handover.
- HRMS records advance AL separately as a **negative/projected balance** rather than pretending it has already been earned.
- The advance balance is reconciled against future earned leave, using **1 day for every 18 qualifying days worked** where that statutory rate applies, and any more favorable approved entitlement.
- Employees who have not completed one year of service **cannot use this advance AL facility**, although they may use an otherwise available company-granted balance after probation.
- Advance AL is not an automatic entitlement, and its approval must be documented.

**If employment ends before advance AL has been earned:** HR and Finance must reconcile the negative balance. Days already taken may be treated as unpaid only where permitted by applicable law, the employment contract, a valid advance-leave agreement and applicable wage-deduction rules. **No automatic final-salary deduction is authorized by this policy.** The employee must receive an explanation and a chance to dispute the calculation. Legal and contractual dues must be paid in full.

### Request Notice, Duration & Business Coverage

- Normally submit an AL request **at least 21 calendar days before** the proposed start date.
- **Annual Leave requests are made in full working days only; half-day AL is not offered under this company policy.**
- Normally request **no more than 7 working days of AL at one time**.
- The employee and Department Lead prepare a reasonable handover and assess foreseeable disruption to client delivery and team operations.
- HR makes the final approval decision and records the reason for any rejection.
- The 21-day notice and 7-working-day standard are **planning controls, not an absolute bar** to an employee's statutory right. HR must review justified exceptions, accumulated earned leave, unavoidable circumstances and other legal/contractual protections individually.
- Repeated refusal must not erase earned leave or circumvent lawful carry-forward requirements.

### Carry-Forward

Unused eligible AL/EL is carried forward. For adult workers in a commercial establishment covered by section 117, the general statutory accumulation threshold is **60 days**, subject to important legal exceptions, including leave requested but refused by the employer. Other legally applicable classifications may have different thresholds.

Accordingly:

- HRMS may use **60 days as the normal commercial-establishment adult-worker threshold only when that classification is confirmed**.
- It must preserve any balance that law or a stronger contract requires above the standard threshold, including qualifying employer-refused leave.
- Oldest eligible leave should ordinarily be used first.
- Existing balances must be audited before implementing any ceiling. **No balance may be silently deleted.**

### Annual Leave Encashment During Employment

Only an employee who has completed **at least one year of continuous employment** may submit **one eligible earned-leave encashment request per calendar year** through HRMS. **Employees with less than one year of employment cannot voluntarily encash company-granted or accrued annual leave, even if HRMS displays an unused balance.**

- Blinto proposes permitting cash-out of **up to 50% of the eligible accrued and unused AL/EL balance** through the applicable annual encashment process.
- Future projected or unearned advance leave cannot be cashed out.
- HR verifies eligibility, balance and legal limits; Finance calculates the payout and payroll treatment.
- **Blinto's proposed annual encashment calculation:** use the employee's **gross monthly salary for the immediately preceding completed payroll month, excluding overtime payments and bonuses**, divided by **30** to obtain the daily encashment rate. Multiply that rate by the number of eligible unused Annual/Earned Leave days approved for encashment.
- **Formula:** `Encashment amount = (Last completed month's gross salary − overtime pay − bonus pay) ÷ 30 × approved encashment days`.
- **Example:** If last month's gross salary was BDT 50,000, including BDT 4,000 overtime pay and BDT 6,000 bonus pay, the proposed calculation for 5 approved days is `(50,000 − 4,000 − 6,000) ÷ 30 × 5 = BDT 6,666.67` before any legally applicable deductions.
- **Legal safeguard:** This company formula is subject to verification against the wage basis and calculation required by applicable Bangladesh law, including section 119 of the Bangladesh Labour Act and the relevant Labour Rules. If the statutory or binding contractual calculation produces a higher payable amount, Finance must use the **higher lawful entitlement**. Do not use the company formula to reduce statutory leave wages. A paid but partially worked month, unpaid absence, unusual salary component, or salary adjustment must be reviewed individually before payment.
- Finance prepares the calculation and HR checks the approved encashment-day balance. Employee requests must not be paid automatically without verifying that the calculation is lawful.
- The request, calculation, decision and remaining balance must be visible in the HRMS audit record.

### Earned Leave at Separation

**Before one year of employment:** If an employee resigns or otherwise separates before completing one year of continuous service, **Blinto does not pay cash for unused first-year company-granted or company-recorded accrued AL under its discretionary leave benefit**, regardless of how many days appear in HRMS. The balance is a time-off benefit, not a cash-encashment entitlement. HR must nevertheless honor any unused leave payment independently required under applicable law or an existing binding employment agreement or commitment.

**After one year of employment:** On resignation, termination, retirement, or other separation, HR and Finance must calculate and pay **all legally or contractually payable unused earned leave**, including eligible carried-forward leave, as part of final settlement.

The calculation must not be limited only to the carried-forward bucket if current-year accrued leave is also payable. The daily wage rate and timing of payment follow applicable law and any more favorable binding terms. Outstanding advance leave is reconciled separately and must not result in an unlawful deduction.

## Casual Leave (CL)

- **10 paid working days per calendar year**, subject to applicable eligibility and stronger rights.
- Unused CL **does not carry forward** into the next calendar year.
- CL can be requested as a **full working day (1.0)** or **half working day (0.5)**. Two approved half-days deduct a total of **1 CL day**.
- Employees normally take **no more than 2 CL working days within one calendar month**, counting half-days as 0.5 each.
- A request above two days in a month or the existing three-consecutive-working-day category boundary is sent to HR for case-specific review. HR may request reasonable documentation where relevant, but a planning limit cannot extinguish statutory leave rights.
- For planned or foreseeable CL, employees must normally submit the request through HRMS **at least 3 calendar days before the first day of leave**. For example, a request to take Friday off should normally be submitted no later than Tuesday.
- The 3-day advance-notice requirement is a **company scheduling rule**, not an automatic reason to reject genuine emergencies or override applicable statutory or contractual leave rights. If a need is unexpected, the employee should inform HR/the Team Lead as soon as reasonably possible and submit or complete the HRMS record promptly. HR reviews late requests fairly and records the reason for any decision.
- Rejected or cancelled requests do not consume leave.

## Sick Leave (SL)

- **14 paid sick-leave days per calendar year**, subject to applicable legal eligibility.
- **Sick Leave is recorded in full-day units only** under Blinto's standard HRMS process. For a part-day medical need, HR reviews an appropriate lawful attendance adjustment or alternative arrangement; the full-day request rule must not automatically cancel a statutory sick-leave entitlement.
- Unused SL does **not carry forward** into the next calendar year.
- Employees should notify HR or the Team/Department Lead as soon as reasonably possible. A genuine medical emergency may be recorded in HRMS afterward.
- For **2 or more consecutive sick-leave days**, employees should provide a medical certificate from an appropriate registered medical practitioner. HR may reasonably request supporting medical evidence earlier where permitted and necessary under applicable law.
- Where statutory sick pay depends on certification, HR must apply that legal requirement; the two-day internal trigger does not waive statutory documentation rules or justify denying a protected entitlement without proper review.
- Medical records must be accessible only to authorized staff and handled confidentially.

### When the Sick Leave Balance Is Exhausted

If an employee has exhausted their available Sick Leave balance but still needs time away for a genuine illness, they should notify HR as soon as reasonably possible and request the leave under an eligible, available balance. Subject to the employee's entitlement, approval requirements, and applicable law, HR normally considers:

1. Available **Casual Leave (CL)**;
2. Available **Annual/Earned Leave (AL/EL)**; or
3. **Leave Without Pay (LWP)** or another lawful, approved arrangement if no eligible paid balance remains.

An approved CL or AL day used after SL is exhausted **is deducted from the leave category actually approved**, not from an exhausted SL balance. HR must communicate the classification and resulting balances clearly. A genuine medical absence is not automatically misconduct.

### Leave Classification and KPI Leave Score

Using CL or AL after the Sick Leave balance is exhausted **may affect the employee's KPI leave score**, but only in accordance with Blinto's separately approved and communicated Performance Tracker/KPI scoring rules. This policy does not introduce a new score formula, automatic penalty, or unverified HRMS feature. The employee must not ask HR to recategorize an accurately recorded CL or AL day as SL, or manually improve the KPI leave score, solely to avoid the scoring effect of having exhausted SL. HR is not authorized to make such a cosmetic adjustment.

If an employee has experienced a **prolonged or recurring genuine illness**, has exhausted the SL balance, and has used other leave categories for medically necessary time away, the employee may submit relevant evidence—such as a medical certificate, treatment or follow-up records, or another reasonable supporting document—together with an explanation of the extended medical need. HR must review whether the original classification, applicable entitlements, and any KPI consequence are accurate and fair. Where appropriate, HR coordinates with the authorized Performance Tracker/KPI decision-maker to consider an exceptional treatment **under the approved scoring and accommodation process**, rather than promising to restore points or fabricate additional SL days.

An employee may always request correction of a **genuine HRMS error, inaccurate leave record, misapplied score, or denied statutory or contractual entitlement**. The restriction on cosmetic adjustment does not prevent such corrections or a good-faith appeal. Medical records must be handled confidentially, and illness, disability, authorized leave, or a reasonable accommodation must not be treated as dishonesty or automatically penalized contrary to applicable law and approved fair-assessment rules.

## Maternity, Paternity & Special Leave

### Maternity Leave

Under **section 46 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended**, a female worker covered by the statutory maternity-benefit provisions must have **worked for Blinto (as the applicable employer) for at least 6 months immediately before the date of delivery** to qualify for statutory paid maternity benefit. This is measured against the **date of delivery**, not automatically against the date maternity leave begins. HR will check the employee's employment records and applicable legal coverage confidentially.

For employees who qualify, the applicable statutory framework provides **120 calendar days** of maternity benefit (normally 60 days before the expected delivery and 60 days after delivery), subject to the law's other eligibility, notice and benefit conditions. Where an employment contract or approved Blinto benefit is more favorable, that stronger entitlement applies.

**An employee with less than 6 months' service is not automatically barred from maternity-related leave, health protection, other available leave or a more favorable contractual benefit.** HR must separately review those rights and any required accommodations under applicable law. HR will explain the process confidentially. Maternity-related statutory rights are not subject to ordinary CL/AL planning caps or team-coverage limits.

### Paternity Leave

Blinto's existing company benefit is **7 paid working days per qualifying event**, subject to the approved eligibility conditions and reasonable notice where foreseeable. Emergency timing is considered fairly.

### Bereavement Leave

Blinto's existing company benefit is **3 paid working days** for qualifying immediate-family bereavement. Inform HR/Lead as soon as reasonably possible.

### Wedding Leave

Blinto's existing company benefit is **3 paid working days** for the employee's own wedding. Employees must normally submit their Wedding Leave request through HRMS **at least 20 calendar days before** the intended start date. HR reviews the request and gives final approval. If the wedding date changes unexpectedly or another exceptional circumstance prevents 20 days’ notice, employees should notify HR promptly and request an exception; approval is not automatic. Meeting the notice requirement does not bypass HR approval.

### Other Medical and Compassionate Circumstances

HR reviews additional medical or compassionate situations individually, preserving applicable statutory or contractual entitlements.

## Weekly Holidays and Festival / Public Holidays

Blinto's normal weekly holidays are **Saturday and Sunday** under the current Office Hours arrangement, unless an employee has a different approved lawful schedule.

Blinto publishes an annual paid holiday calendar. For covered workers, the company calendar must meet **at least 13 statutory paid festival holidays per calendar year** under the Bangladesh Labour Act as amended in 2026, subject to the applicable classification and legal framework.

Employees are not normally required to work on weekly or festival holidays. Any exceptional work requires documented approval, accurate records, and the legally applicable compensatory rest and/or compensation. Follow the [Office Hours Policy](./office-hours-policy.md) and [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md) where relevant.

## Leave Requests, Team Coverage & HR Decisions

All planned CL and AL requests follow [Leave Request & Handover](./leave-request-and-handover.md):

1. Employee requests leave in Blinto HRMS, selecting dates, duration, category and any handover details.
2. HRMS checks the relevant balance, monthly CL guideline, employee eligibility and team calendar. It allows **0.5-day CL** requests, but **only whole-day AL and SL requests**, and flags planned CL submitted fewer than **3 calendar days** before the requested leave date for HR exception review.
3. If another team member has overlapping planned leave, HRMS warns the employee and requests coordination. The warning alone does not reject the request.
4. Department Lead provides coverage and operational-impact advice.
5. **HR makes the final decision** and records approval, rejection with reason, clarification request, or lawful exception.
6. HRMS updates balances, attendance, team calendar and notifications; rejected requests must not deduct balances.

Normally only one employee per team takes overlapping planned CL/AL under the existing scheduling guideline, subject to HR exceptions and stronger rights. Protected sickness, maternity and genuine emergencies must not be automatically refused under this capacity rule.

## Leave During Notice Period

Employees serving notice remain subject to ordinary attendance, handover and leave processes. A positive leave balance does not itself authorize an employee to be absent after HR has rejected a specific leave request.

HR reviews notice-period leave in light of earned entitlements, medical or other protected rights, handover needs, and applicable law. A general prohibition on leave during notice is not created by this policy.

## Unapproved or Unauthorized Absence

If an employee is absent without approved leave or a recorded valid exception:

1. HRMS may show **Unauthorized Absence — Pending Review**, rather than issuing an immediate punishment.
2. HR checks the attendance record, leave request outcome, communications, emergencies and possible system errors.
3. HR asks the employee for an explanation and gives a reasonable opportunity to respond.
4. HR records the conclusion and correct attendance/payroll classification. Days not worked and not otherwise payable may be treated as unpaid **only where legally permitted**.
5. A repeated or serious unexcused violation may be referred to the [Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy](./corrective-action-and-performance-improvement-policy.md), including any statutory disciplinary safeguards.

A rejected leave request does not become approved simply because an employee stays away; equally, a rejection cannot extinguish protected statutory rights. HR must not assume every unrecorded absence is misconduct.

## Probation, Casual Leave & Leave Without Pay

**Blinto's proposed probationary allowance:** During the probation period, an employee may take **up to 3 working days of paid Casual Leave (CL) in total** for a genuine **emergency or sickness**. This is a total allowance for the applicable probation period, not a fresh three days each month. Approved half-day CL, where used, counts as **0.5 day** toward the allowance. The employee should notify HR and the Department Head as soon as reasonably possible and request or regularize the leave through HRMS.

**After the 3-day probationary CL allowance has been used:** A further absence that is not covered by another legally or contractually available paid-leave entitlement will ordinarily be reviewed as **Leave Without Pay (LWP)**. The employee should explain the reason to HR. Management may review the circumstances—including a genuine emergency or illness—and decide whether an additional discretionary paid exception or other lawful arrangement is appropriate. No such exception is automatic, and HR must communicate the decision.

Employees do not receive an additional or duplicate annual-leave allocation through probation. An employee who has completed probation but not one year of service may use an otherwise available company-granted AL balance, but is not eligible for Blinto's proposed **advance AL** facility until completing one year of service.

HR records the leave reason, balance used, applicable paid entitlement and any management decision. Unpaid time and payroll deductions must be handled only where legally permitted. A genuine medical or emergency absence must not automatically be treated as misconduct.

## HRMS Records & Manual Safeguards

People Operations must maintain accurate records of:

- each type of leave and eligibility;
- opening, current, accrued, projected and carried-forward balances;
- qualifying days worked for statutory EL;
- leave requests, decisions, rejection reasons and employee notifications;
- team-calendar conflicts and HR-approved exceptions;
- advance AL balances and later reconciliation;
- sick-leave documents with restricted access;
- leave encashment requests, approved days and payment history;
- employer-refused earned leave that must be protected above ordinary limits;
- leave classification corrections, disputes and audit logs; and
- unpaid leave, separation and final-settlement calculations.

Where HRMS cannot yet perform a required function correctly, HR and Finance must keep a controlled, auditable temporary register. An inaccurate HRMS balance must not determine an employee's rights.

## Responsibilities

**Team Members** follow the request process, provide reasonable notice, keep information accurate, and complete proportionate handovers.

**Team/Department Leads** plan coverage and advise HR on operational impact. They do not make the final approval decision for ordinary CL/AL.

**People Operations / HR** owns the policy, verifies eligibility and balances, makes the final planned-leave decision, reviews exceptions and employee explanations, protects records, and coordinates accurate HRMS configuration.

**Finance** verifies lawful payment, annual-leave encashment, approved LWP treatment, advances and final settlement. HR does not independently calculate or authorize payroll deductions.

**CEO / Authorized Leadership** approves policy amendments and exceptional financial/company-level decisions where required by Blinto's delegation framework. CEO approval is not required for routine CL/AL applications.

## Exceptions & Escalation

Contact People Operations when:

- the HRMS balance appears wrong;
- a statutory or contractual entitlement is unclear;
- urgent leave is needed without normal notice;
- a request exceeds the monthly or consecutive-day planning guideline;
- advance leave, long leave, encashment or carry-forward needs review;
- sick leave or a medical certificate is disputed;
- leave is needed during a notice period;
- an employee disputes a rejection, unauthorized-absence record or unpaid calculation; or
- team coverage may affect client delivery.

People Operations reviews facts promptly and documents the outcome. No employee should be penalized for good-faith reporting of a leave or payroll error.
