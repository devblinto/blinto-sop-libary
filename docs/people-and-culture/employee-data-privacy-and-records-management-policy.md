# Employee Data Privacy & Records Management Policy

## Purpose

Explain how Blinto collects, uses, protects, corrects, shares, and retains personal information about its employees. Employees should know which records Blinto needs, who can view them, and how to ask HR to correct a mistake.

Blinto's goal is a reliable, secure **HRMS-based employee record from onboarding through offboarding**. This is a **planned direction**, not a claim that every document or function is already available in HRMS.

## Scope

This policy applies to employee and former-employee personal information handled by Blinto. Relevant confidentiality protections also apply to interns, job applicants, and other individuals whose personal information Blinto handles for hiring or engagement purposes.

For employees who have statutory worker records, applicable Bangladesh labour-law recordkeeping duties must be met in addition to this policy.

## What Information Blinto May Hold

Depending on the person's role and the purpose, relevant information may include:

- National ID and other identifying details, employee ID, photographs, contact numbers, and emergency contacts;
- joining date, designation, department, employment type, reporting information, appointment letter, and signed agreements;
- educational qualifications and employment-history documents;
- bank details, salary and payroll information;
- attendance, working schedule, leave requests, leave decisions, and related records;
- performance evaluations, recognized achievements, and properly documented disciplinary decisions;
- medical certificates, prescriptions, or other supporting documents submitted for a legitimate leave or employment purpose;
- authorized onboarding, access, employment-change, and offboarding records.

Blinto should request information that is **necessary and proportionate** to a legitimate purpose, rather than collecting documents simply because they might be useful one day.

## Why Blinto Uses Employee Data

Blinto may use relevant employee information for recruitment, hiring, onboarding, salary and benefits, attendance and leave, performance and development, work safety, emergency contact, compliance with lawful obligations, employment changes, offboarding, and authorized workforce reporting.

Personal information must not be used for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate lawful basis and any notice or consent required by law. Employees should be informed of material new uses when required.

## Official Records and HRMS

### Current position

Blinto uses **HRMS** for a limited set of employment and operational records, including employee name, contact number, designation, employee ID, joining date, employment type, attendance, calendar information, work anniversaries, announcements, polls, sign-in method, last sign-in date, leave applications and approval-authority information, and onboarding-checklist information, as applicable.

**Current limitations:** HRMS does not yet provide a complete document repository covering every employee record from onboarding to offboarding. In particular, the current leave-application function **does not provide a medical-certificate upload option**. HRMS also does not currently provide an HR birthday reminder.

A field or module being visible in HRMS does not mean that it stores a complete, verified record or that all workflow capabilities are implemented.

### Intended future position

Blinto intends HRMS to become the **primary controlled system for employee information and documents throughout the employment lifecycle**, including:

- onboarding identity and qualification documents, signed appointment/engagement terms and acknowledgements;
- employment status and changes, leave documents (including medical evidence through a secure restricted submission process), and relevant personnel records;
- approved salary and employment-change records, performance and disciplinary documentation, where appropriate and with restricted visibility;
- offboarding records and related employment documents.

This requires appropriate privacy permissions, lawful collection, secure file handling, employee-record accuracy, and retention controls **before** additional sensitive documents are migrated or uploaded. Not every employee or manager should be able to see every record simply because it is in HRMS.

Until those capabilities are verified, **restricted HR Google Drive folders** remain an approved controlled location for personnel documents, and **Finance maintains necessary payroll records** in its authorized environment. HR must be able to identify the authoritative record for each document type and avoid uncontrolled copies.

Medical evidence must not be posted into general HRMS notes, ordinary team chats, unrestricted folders, or public links simply to work around the missing attachment feature. Employees may submit such evidence to HR through a suitably private, approved channel.

## Who May Access Personal Information

Access is limited to **authorized people with a genuine work-related need**:

- **People Operations / HR:** personnel administration and lawful HR responsibilities;
- **Finance:** payroll, bank-payment and related financial records necessary for its work;
- **CEO or authorized senior management:** specific employment, legal, governance, or operational matters requiring access;
- **Department Heads:** necessary team-level work, attendance, leave-status or operational information, **not unrestricted access** to medical, salary, or entire personnel files.

Medical prescriptions, medical certificates, health information and similarly sensitive records require **stricter access**. A Department Head should normally receive only the outcome or operational information needed to manage work and leave, not an unnecessary diagnosis or prescription.

No employee may browse, download, forward, discuss, or disclose another person's confidential records without authorization. Having a system account is not permission to read all data held in that system.

## Medical Documents and Confidentiality

Where a leave policy permits HR to request relevant medical evidence, employees may provide only what is reasonably required for the specific request. HR should protect the document and limit disclosure. A medical prescription is not a general requirement for ordinary personal or vacation leave.

HR should not require employees to share sensitive diagnoses with their team or place prescriptions in open HRMS comments. An employee may ask HR how to submit supporting material confidentially.

The applicable **Leave & Time-Off Policy** governs leave eligibility and evidence requirements; this policy governs how the resulting documents are protected.

## Employee Access, Corrections, and Updates

Employees can contact **HR** to:

- ask about personal information Blinto holds and how to request access where legally available;
- update their contact number or emergency-contact information;
- challenge an incorrect name, joining date, employment type, designation, or other HRMS record;
- raise an attendance or payroll mismatch; or
- request correction of an inaccurate or incomplete personnel document.

HR may request reasonable verification before updating identity, employment, or payment details. HR reviews the record, coordinates with Finance or the Department Head when necessary, and communicates the outcome.

Attendance corrections follow the **Attendance Policy**. Pay corrections follow the **Compensation & Salary Administration Policy**. HR must not postpone correction merely because an HRMS edit or document-upload function is unavailable; the correction should be recorded in the approved controlled source until HRMS supports it.

Requests for access, correction, restriction, withdrawal of consent, deletion or other data rights will be assessed under the **Personal Data Protection Act, 2026**. Not all requests require immediate deletion: Blinto may need to retain information where a lawful recordkeeping duty or other permitted basis applies.

## Sharing Employee Information

Blinto may share the **minimum information necessary** with authorized banking, payroll or benefit providers; government authorities where legally required; and external professionals or service providers for legitimate protected purposes.

Other disclosures should have the person's authorization or another valid legal basis as required. Recipients must be restricted to appropriate uses and reasonable confidentiality/security expectations.

Personnel records must not be disclosed publicly, sent to unrelated third parties, or copied into unrestricted shared folders or unauthorized group chats.

## Security and Suspected Data Incidents

Blinto's expected safeguards include **role-appropriate access**, restricted HR/Finance folders, existing credential protections, and access review when responsibilities change or employment ends.

Employees who discover an exposed file, misplaced document, unauthorized access, suspicious sharing, or possible loss of employee information must **report it to HR promptly** through an approved private communication channel. HR coordinates the response with the relevant system owner and authorized leadership, including containment and required notifications under applicable law.

The **Credential & Password Management Policy** and **Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning** SOP govern the detailed technology and access processes. This policy does not promise controls or incident-notification technology that has not been implemented.

Any legally required notice to affected people or an authority must follow the applicable law and effective rules; this policy does not invent a fixed notification deadline.

## Retention, Offboarding, and Secure Disposal

Blinto retains employment information for the period **legally required** or for another **documented, lawful, necessary purpose**. Information must not be kept indefinitely without justification.

An employee's resignation or exit does **not** automatically require all records to be deleted. HR reviews records after employment ends, coordinates legally required retention, restricts access, and arranges secure disposal once no lawful retention purpose remains.

**Blinto has not yet adopted a complete internal retention schedule.** HR should coordinate its development with relevant Finance, legal, and system owners; this policy does not assign unverified numbers of years to particular document types.

Where Bangladesh labour law requires appointment records, identity cards, a worker register, a service book, or prescribed records for covered workers, Blinto must follow those obligations. Digital HRMS storage alone must not be assumed to replace an expressly prescribed physical or other statutory record format until legally permitted and verified.

Offboarding-related access removal and asset controls follow **Employee Offboarding** and **Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning**.

## Responsibilities

**Employees** provide reasonably accurate information, keep contact details updated, use approved channels, and report suspected disclosure or errors.

**People Operations / HR** coordinates personnel record accuracy, permitted access, requests and corrections, medical-document confidentiality, and retention reviews.

**Finance** protects payroll and bank details, shares them only when needed for authorized payment or compliance, and supports correction of payroll errors.

**Department Heads** use only necessary team-level information and report inaccuracies or suspected disclosures to HR.

**Authorized leadership and system owners** support appropriate safeguards, access reviews, and legally compliant response to significant incidents.

## Implementation Status and Review Notes

This draft records the following improvements as **proposed, not completed**:

- expanding HRMS from its current limited fields and workflows to a complete onboarding-to-offboarding employee document record;
- providing a secure route for leave-related medical certificate/prescription uploads with restricted HR access;
- resolving inaccurate HRMS fields and scattered documents;
- clearly communicating the HR correction and employee-data request process; and
- establishing a lawful, document-type-specific retention schedule.

The lack of an HR birthday reminder is a requested system enhancement, **not a legal privacy obligation** and not a reason to collect more personal details unnecessarily.
