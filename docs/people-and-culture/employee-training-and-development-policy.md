# Employee Training & Development Policy

## Purpose

Explain how employees may request training, how relevant learning opportunities are considered and approved, how company-funded training is handled, and what employees should do after training.

Blinto values learning that improves job skills, teamwork, service quality, safety, and employees' professional growth. Training should support real development and business needs, not attendance at courses for its own sake.

## Current Position and Policy Status

**Blinto does not currently operate a formal company-wide training and development program, framework, annual learning calendar, or fixed training budget.** This document proposes rules and responsibilities for a future, more consistent approach. It must not be read as a promise that a course, budget, training platform, or development program is already available.

The approved [Employee Onboarding](./employee-onboarding.md), [Internship Program](./internship-program.md), and [Performance Review](./performance-review.md) SOPs already describe their respective orientation, internship mentoring, and review processes. This policy builds on those processes instead of replacing them.

## Who May Be Considered

Training opportunities may be considered for:

- confirmed employees;
- probationary employees;
- paid interns, in line with their approved learning arrangements; and
- Department Heads and team leaders.

Participation depends on the person's responsibilities, development needs, relevant client or project requirements, time availability, and appropriate approval. Being eligible for consideration does **not** automatically guarantee a paid course or training place.

## Types of Learning

Depending on relevance and approval, training may include:

- technical and role-specific skills;
- professional communication, teamwork and other soft skills;
- leadership and management development;
- company software, development tools, design tools and emerging technologies, including AI;
- client- or project-specific knowledge;
- information security, privacy, confidentiality, compliance and workplace safety;
- online courses, webinars, workshops and practical internal knowledge sharing; and
- approved professional certifications and examinations.

The appropriate option may be a free resource, supervised practice, mentoring, a workshop, or formal external training.

## Essential and Required Training

Where relevant to a person's role, Blinto expects completion of:

- onboarding and key-policy orientation under the existing Onboarding SOP;
- essential information-security and confidentiality guidance;
- the training reasonably necessary to perform assigned duties safely and competently;
- workplace health and safety orientation appropriate to the workplace and risks;
- training required by an applicable client contract, regulatory obligation or work assignment; and
- necessary guidance when approved tools, workflows or security requirements change.

These are **proposed common expectations**, not a claim that Blinto currently runs a complete mandatory-training system. Where the law requires particular safety instruction or another legally mandated training measure, Blinto must meet that requirement irrespective of whether a formal development program has been launched.

This policy does not introduce a blanket annual course or certification requirement.

## Identifying Development Needs

Employees may discuss learning needs with their Department Head. A need may also arise during a [Performance Review](./performance-review.md), through coaching, a specific skills gap, a role change, adoption of a new tool, or a new client requirement.

The Department Head and employee should agree on the practical skill or outcome needed. HR may help coordinate development priorities, but no separate company-wide development plan or tracking framework is claimed to exist yet.

Paid interns continue to receive their program-specific mentoring and learning guidance under the [Internship Program](./internship-program.md).

## Requesting and Approving Training

Employees should discuss a proposed training opportunity with their Department Head **before enrolling where it requires work time, a company account, company funds, or a change to commitments**.

The approval path depends on the request:

- **Free training:** The Department Head may give final approval for role-relevant free training, including the necessary work-time arrangement. Any employee-data or security concerns still require review by the responsible owner.
- **Routine internal or role-related training arrangements:** HR may give final approval after Department Head input where HR coordination is needed, unless spending or another higher approval is required.
- **Company-funded external training, courses, certifications or examinations:** The Department Head recommends the request; HR reviews relevance and development need; **the Chief of Staff gives final business approval**; and **Finance verifies and approves the budget or payment** before any company financial commitment.

Where an approval includes a software account, AI service or external platform, the existing Access Provisioning and applicable security policies also apply. Approval to attend a course is not automatically approval to upload confidential data or purchase additional software.

An employee must **not** purchase a course or subscription on the assumption that Blinto will reimburse the cost later without advance approval.

## Training Fees, Certifications and Reimbursement

Blinto **may fund** approved training connected to business and role needs, subject to available funds and the applicable approval process. There is **no fixed annual training allowance or budget per employee** under this draft.

Free and appropriate learning resources are encouraged. Employees ordinarily pay for courses undertaken solely for unrelated personal interests, unless a separate arrangement is specifically approved.

Approved certification examinations or related fees may be covered where justified. Any reimbursement is subject to the applicable company expense policy or approved finance process; this policy does not create a new expense form or promise that one already exists.

Employees retain their own personal certificates and professional qualifications. Records that Blinto legitimately needs to verify approved training completion may be kept by HR with appropriate confidentiality.

## Learning During and Outside Working Hours

Mandatory training required by Blinto should normally be scheduled during paid working hours, with the Department Head arranging reasonable coverage. Approved job-related training may also occur during normal working hours with the appropriate authorization.

Self-selected, unrelated personal learning is generally undertaken outside normal working hours. **Training time that legally counts as working time cannot be reclassified as unpaid personal time merely because it is described as training.**

Training outside normal hours must comply with applicable employment terms, statutory working-time and rest requirements, and the separate [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md). Employees should not be required to attend after-hours training without appropriate arrangements or be denied a legally owed overtime entitlement.

Employees and Department Heads must arrange learning so that essential client commitments, meetings, and operational coverage are maintained.

## Completing Training and Applying the Learning

After approved training, employees should share relevant proof of completion or an earned certificate with HR, discuss useful learning with their Department Head, and apply relevant skills to their work.

Where useful, employees may share relevant knowledge with teammates, but **a presentation or formal report is not required after every course**.

Training participation and demonstrated skill development may inform normal performance or career conversations. Completion alone does not automatically guarantee a promotion, salary change, or employment status change; such decisions follow their own policies and approvals.

**Recordkeeping:** HR should retain reasonable records of approved, completed training in an appropriate restricted location. HRMS may be used **when the necessary function exists and is suitable**. This policy does not claim HRMS currently has a complete training-record module.

## High-Cost Training and Any Repayment Arrangement

Routine approved training normally carries **no employee repayment obligation**.

For unusually expensive training, Blinto may propose a **separate written agreement before the expense is incurred**, explaining the training, costs, and any lawful terms. Such an agreement must be reasonable, clearly communicated, voluntary where required, and reviewed for enforceability under applicable law. No minimum post-training service period or repayment obligation is created simply by this policy.

**No automatic salary or final-settlement deduction is authorized for training expenses.** Bangladesh Labour Act section 125 limits deductions from wages for covered workers; a proposed training-cost repayment claim does not itself make a deduction lawful. Any dispute or recovery must be reviewed separately by HR and Finance, subject to law, the relevant agreement and employee rights.

Employees may request clarification before agreeing to any special training arrangement.

## Fair Access and Employee Responsibilities

Training requests should be considered based on legitimate skills, role, client, safety, and business needs—not favoritism or irrelevant personal characteristics. Budget availability and business priorities may affect timing, and HR should explain the decision where appropriate.

Employees participating in approved learning are expected to attend responsibly, follow the provider's rules and Blinto's confidentiality requirements, use approved tools, complete agreed learning activities where reasonable, and communicate any difficulty to their Department Head.

Employees should not be penalized merely for requesting relevant learning or for raising a good-faith concern about training arrangements.

## Responsibilities

**Employees:** Identify relevant learning needs, discuss requests in advance, seek spending approval, and apply useful learning.

**Department Heads:** Identify team skill gaps, recommend appropriate development, approve free training within their authority, and coordinate operational coverage.

**People Operations / HR:** Review development relevance, coordinate routine training arrangements, communicate applicable rules, and protect training records.

**Chief of Staff:** Provide final business approval for company-funded training under this policy.

**Finance:** Verify available funds and approve and process authorized training payments or reimbursements in accordance with applicable controls.
