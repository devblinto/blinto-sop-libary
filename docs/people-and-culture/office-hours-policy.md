# Office Hours Policy

## Purpose

Explain Blinto's normal working days, office hours, morning routine, breaks, meetings, and end-of-day responsibilities in a way everyone can follow.

This policy explains **when the normal workday operates**. The [Attendance Policy](./attendance-policy.md) explains check-in, late arrival, and attendance records. The [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md) explains work beyond an employee's normal approved schedule and the applicable approval and payment process.

Blinto aims to help employees work effectively during normal hours, arrive prepared, and deliver reliable work. Working late should not be a routine measure of commitment or performance.

## Scope

This policy applies to Blinto employees working under the standard office schedule. Employees with approved individual, remote, or alternative schedules must follow their documented arrangements.

Approved arrangements and applicable contractual or legal requirements take priority where they provide a different rule.

## Standard Office Schedule

Blinto's standard working days are **Monday to Friday**.

**Saturday and Sunday are weekly holidays** under the standard schedule. Employees are not normally scheduled to work on those days.

**Normal office hours: 8:30 AM–5:00 PM.**

| Time | Activity |
| :-- | :-- |
| **8:30–8:45 AM** | Work preparation |
| **8:45–9:00 AM** | Required team breakfast (unless excused) |
| **9:00–9:15 AM** | Daily stand-up |
| **9:15 AM–1:00 PM** | Normal work and client/project responsibilities |
| **1:00–2:15 PM** | Lunch and prayer break |
| **2:15–5:00 PM** | Normal work and end-of-day updates |

Employees should normally plan to arrive by **8:30 AM**. The approved arrival window, official late-arrival threshold, and automatic late allowances are defined only in the [Attendance Policy](./attendance-policy.md).

The **1:00–2:15 PM lunch and prayer break is paid but normally duty-free**, and is not counted as actual hours worked unless duties are performed or applicable law requires a different treatment. **Work preparation and the daily stand-up count as working activities.** Required breakfast participation is paid scheduled time and must be assessed correctly for statutory working-hour purposes. Payroll and overtime calculations must follow applicable law and employment terms.

## Morning Routine

### 8:30–8:45 AM — Work Preparation

Team members should use this time to prepare for the day's work, as applicable to their roles. This may include:

- opening work systems and checking messages;
- reviewing ClickUp tasks and deadlines;
- checking scheduled client commitments;
- identifying blockers; and
- preparing a brief stand-up update.

**Work preparation is a working activity** when performed as part of an employee's assigned work.

Employees who arrive within the approved attendance window but after 8:30 AM should make reasonable preparations and still be ready for the 9:00 AM stand-up. A missed portion of the preparation window must not automatically be counted as a separate late-arrival violation contrary to the Attendance Policy. Employees within the approved arrival window should join the required breakfast routine on time and still prepare as needed for the 9:00 AM stand-up.

### 8:45–9:00 AM — Team Breakfast

Team breakfast takes place from **8:45 AM to 9:00 AM** and is a required part of Blinto's shared morning routine **unless the employee is excused**.

Team members should:

- attend the shared breakfast routine unless excused by the responsible lead or People Operations;
- finish breakfast and clean up by **9:00 AM**;
- respect others using the shared space; and
- be ready for the **9:00 AM** daily stand-up.

An employee who cannot eat the food provided for dietary, religious, medical, or other reasonable personal reasons should inform the responsible lead or People Operations. Employees must not be required to eat particular food. An appropriate exception or alternative may be arranged without unfair treatment.

Breakfast participation must not delay the stand-up. Any concern about missed breakfast should be reviewed reasonably; it is **not automatically another late-arrival incident** under the Attendance Policy. Approved leave, alternate schedules, emergencies, and other valid exceptions must be considered.

Because attendance at the breakfast routine is required, Blinto will treat this **15-minute scheduled period as paid time**. Its treatment as hours worked for statutory limits and overtime must be reviewed in light of the actual duties and applicable law; a label such as "breakfast" does not, by itself, exclude mandatory attendance time from working-time calculations.

## Daily Stand-Up

The daily stand-up starts at **9:00 AM** and should normally end by **9:15 AM**.

Each participant should briefly explain:

1. what they completed previously;
2. what they plan to work on today; and
3. any blockers or decisions they need.

The stand-up is a working activity. It is for coordination, not long discussions. Detailed problem-solving should be handled afterward with the relevant people.

Employees expected to attend should arrive prepared and join on time. If an approved schedule exception or other valid reason prevents attendance, the employee should tell the responsible Team/Department Lead and provide an update using the approved alternative method.

Stand-up punctuality is monitored separately from office arrival under the [Attendance Policy](./attendance-policy.md). A single incident should not lead to unfair or duplicate corrective action.

The responsible Team/Department Lead should help keep the stand-up on schedule and address recurring delays with People Operations when needed.

## Fixed Daily Break Schedule

### 1:00–2:15 PM — Lunch and Prayer Break

The normal lunch and prayer break is **1:00 PM–2:15 PM**.

Employees should:

- use the break for lunch, prayer, and personal rest;
- return ready to work at **2:15 PM**;
- communicate when an approved exception is needed; and
- inform the relevant lead when an operational or client-coverage issue may arise.

**This 1:00–2:15 PM break is paid but normally duty-free.** Employees receive their normal pay during the break and are not ordinarily required to work or remain on duty. The duty-free break is **not counted as hours actually worked** in Blinto's working-time records, subject to applicable law and any more favorable contractual arrangement.

If an employee is required to perform work or provide client or operational coverage during any part of the break, the actual work must be recorded and appropriately treated as working time. The responsible Team/Department Lead should arrange an alternative rest/meal break where required, and any overtime entitlement must be assessed under applicable law and the [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md).

For clarity: **paid time and hours actually worked are different concepts**. People Operations and Finance must verify that the company's records and payroll calculations reflect this distinction without reducing statutory or contractual entitlements.

Short, necessary personal needs outside scheduled breaks should be managed reasonably. They should not be treated automatically as misconduct.

## Meeting-Time Discipline

Meetings should:

- start and finish on time;
- have a clear purpose or agenda;
- include people whose participation is needed;
- allow participants to prepare in advance; and
- avoid unnecessary delays to project work.

Employees should communicate promptly if they cannot attend an important meeting. Team/Department Leads should not routinely schedule meetings during lunch or outside approved working hours.

## International Client Meetings and Adjusted Schedules

Blinto works with international clients. Some client meetings may occur outside the standard office schedule.

Employees may adjust their working hours **only through an approved arrangement**. The responsible Team/Department Lead should identify the business need, and People Operations should record the approved schedule.

People Operations may approve recurring schedule arrangements within its delegated authority. A **permanent change to the standard office schedule requires CEO approval**.

An adjusted schedule must clearly state:

- the employee or team covered;
- the approved working days and times;
- the applicable break and attendance expectations;
- the start date and, if temporary, end date; and
- who approved the change.

A schedule adjustment must not be used to avoid legally or contractually required overtime payment or rest. Actual additional work, including urgent after-hours client commitments, must be reviewed under the [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md) and applicable employment requirements.

## Workday Expectations

Team members should normally carry out their assigned work, collaboration, client commitments, and required meetings within the approved working day.

Team/Department Leads should plan capacity and deadlines so employees do not need to stay late regularly.

An employee should not be assessed as more committed merely because they work after office hours.

## End-of-Day Work Hygiene

Before leaving work, team members should:

- update the status of their assigned ClickUp tasks;
- record any important completed or unfinished work;
- notify the responsible lead about a blocker or urgent client issue; and
- save relevant project files, code, or deliverables in the approved location.

Team/Department Leads are responsible for checking critical deliverables and ensuring that urgent work has a clear owner or handover.

This does not require a separate formal end-of-day report from every employee unless the team has an approved reporting process.

## Schedule Exceptions

Approved leave, remote work, emergencies, and authorized alternative schedules may change an employee's normal working times.

- **Recurring changes:** People Operations may approve arrangements within its delegated authority, after appropriate Department Head coordination.
- **Permanent changes to the company-wide schedule:** Require CEO approval.
- **Approved remote work:** Must follow the agreed work schedule and attendance process.
- **Emergencies:** Employees should inform their lead or People Operations as soon as reasonably possible; exceptions may be reviewed afterward where advance approval was impractical.

A recurring change must be recorded rather than becoming an informal practice.

## Working-Time and Overtime Records

People Operations and the relevant Team/Department Leads should maintain accurate records of approved schedules and exceptions through Blinto's applicable systems.

For payroll and legal purposes, the company must distinguish among:

- time actually worked, including work preparation and required stand-ups;
- the paid, normally duty-free lunch and prayer break (not actual hours worked unless duties are performed or law requires otherwise);
- mandatory paid breakfast participation, with its legal working-time treatment verified;
- paid time that is not necessarily counted as time worked;
- approved alternative schedules; and
- additional hours actually worked.

No overtime calculation should rely only on the 8:30 AM–5:00 PM office window without checking the applicable legal and contractual rules.

## Responsibilities

**Team Members**

- Follow their approved work schedule.
- Arrive prepared for required meetings.
- Use breaks responsibly and return on time.
- Update relevant ClickUp tasks before leaving.
- Notify their lead or People Operations about delays, exceptions, or schedule conflicts.

**Team/Department Leads**

- Start and end team stand-ups on time.
- Plan work and client commitments around approved hours.
- Check critical deliverables and necessary handovers.
- Coordinate required schedule adjustments with People Operations.
- Avoid routine or unnecessary after-hours work.

**People Operations**

- Maintain the company-wide office-hours policy.
- Record and coordinate approved recurring schedules.
- Align schedule exceptions with attendance administration.
- Escalate permanent schedule changes for CEO approval.
- Coordinate working-time and payroll questions with the relevant owners.

**CEO / Authorized Leadership**

- Approve permanent changes to the company-wide standard schedule.
- Review exceptional arrangements when required by the authority structure.

## Exceptions & Escalation

Contact People Operations when:

- the approved schedule is unclear;
- a recurring or permanent schedule change is needed;
- a client meeting conflicts with normal hours or break times;
- the employee cannot attend a required stand-up for an approved reason;
- there is a dispute about working-time records; or
- work beyond normal hours may require overtime review.

## Working-Time Clarification

For the normal 8:30 AM–5:00 PM schedule, the office window is **8 hours 30 minutes**. The paid, normally duty-free lunch/prayer break is **1 hour 15 minutes**. The remaining **7 hours 15 minutes** includes work preparation, required breakfast participation, the daily stand-up, and normal work. Actual hours worked and any overtime must be determined from the employee's duties, attendance records, and applicable legal requirements, not simply from the office window or payroll-paid hours.
