# Company Asset Issuance, Use & Return Policy

## Purpose

Explain how Blinto assigns company equipment and other assets, what is expected of people who use them, and how damage, maintenance, and return are handled.

The policy aims to keep company property available, safe, accountable, and appropriately protected while treating employees fairly.

## Scope

This policy applies to company assets assigned to employees, including permanent and confirmed employees, probationary employees, paid interns, and freelancers or consultants where an engagement requires Blinto equipment.

Covered assets may include:

- laptops, desktop computers, monitors, keyboards, mice, and other accessories;
- company mobile phones, SIM cards, headsets, webcams, and electronic peripherals;
- office access cards, keys, and other issued property;
- company-controlled software licenses, subscriptions, and assigned digital resources; and
- any other company-owned item provided for work.

Software, user accounts, and digital access must also follow the separate **Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning** and **Credential & Password Management** policies. An issued subscription is not permission to share credentials or transfer access.

## Who May Receive Equipment

Company property is allocated according to **actual job or engagement requirements**, not merely employment title or seniority.

The **Department Head recommends** the allocation and confirms the business need. **HR/Admin coordinates issuance**, including the applicable record and communication to the recipient.

Equipment may be issued during onboarding or later when work requirements change. Providing an asset does not transfer ownership of it to the employee, intern, or contractor.

## Receiving an Asset

When Blinto issues equipment, the recipient is expected to:

- receive an explanation of the item's intended work use and any limitations;
- check its apparent condition with HR/Admin and report an existing fault promptly;
- confirm the equipment and any accompanying accessories received; and
- acknowledge responsibility for reasonable care and proper return.

**Blinto's intended control:** HR/Admin maintains the official record of which assets and accessories have been assigned to each recipient, together with their condition and any transfer or return. Acknowledgement of receipt is required under this policy.

Because receipt acknowledgements have not been consistently obtained, the company must **implement and regularize** this control. This policy does not claim a fully completed or automated asset register already exists.

An acknowledgement records possession and condition; it does **not** mean that the recipient agrees to unlimited financial liability or automatic payroll deductions.

## Proper Use of Company Assets

Company equipment is provided **primarily for authorized company work**.

Employees and other authorized recipients must:

- use reasonable care to protect the asset from loss, theft, damage, unsafe handling, and unauthorized access;
- obtain prior permission for personal use;
- not lend or share equipment with family members, friends, or outsiders;
- not install unauthorized software or bypass required security measures;
- not sell, pledge, modify, replace, dispose of, or transfer company property without authorization;
- keep any applicable access cards, keys, devices, and accessories secure; and
- follow the existing company information-security and credential rules.

Unauthorized personal use may be reviewed under the applicable conduct or corrective-action policy. Ordinary work-related wear must be distinguished from deliberate misuse or established negligence.

## Taking Equipment Outside the Office

Company laptops may be taken home or off-site **only when authorized by the Department Head**. Office desktop equipment is normally kept on-site.

For approved emergency work from home, employees may use an authorized company laptop under the separate **Remote Work & Work-from-Home Policy**. Approval to work from home does not automatically authorize taking any other company asset.

When traveling with an issued device, employees should take reasonable steps to prevent theft, avoid leaving it unattended in insecure places, and protect confidential company or client information.

Unusual off-site arrangements or movement of equipment must be coordinated with HR/Admin so the assignment record remains accurate.

## Maintenance and Repair

Blinto normally coordinates necessary maintenance and approved repairs of company-owned equipment.

Employees should report faults, damage, unusual device behavior, or safety concerns promptly to **HR/Admin**. They should not arrange an unauthorized repair or continue using equipment that appears unsafe.

HR/Admin coordinates servicing and updates the applicable equipment record. The Department Head confirms operational impact and whether an alternative device is needed for continuity. Replacement equipment may be provided when available and approved.

**Current improvement needed:** repairs have not been centrally tracked consistently. HR/Admin should establish a reliable record of reported faults, approved servicing, and the asset's return to use. The detailed technical repair procedure, if needed, belongs in a separate operational resource rather than this employee policy.

## Damage, Loss, or Theft

If an asset is lost, stolen, or damaged, the employee or recipient must **inform HR/Admin immediately or as soon as reasonably possible** and explain the circumstances honestly.

HR/Admin reviews the incident, available evidence, asset condition, and any security or operational consequences. The recipient must have a fair opportunity to explain what happened or dispute an allegation.

- **Normal wear and tear** through authorized use is not automatically treated as misconduct or personal financial responsibility.
- **Accidental damage** is considered case by case.
- **Intentional damage or proven negligence** may require appropriate corrective action under the relevant policy.
- **No automatic deduction** may be made from earned salary merely because property is missing or damaged.

Where Bangladesh Labour Act provisions apply to a covered worker, sections **125 and 127** permit only legally qualifying deductions for damage or loss of property entrusted to the worker, linked directly to the worker's neglect or default, with a proper inquiry and limits on the amount. Any proposed recovery must satisfy these requirements and other applicable law.

For others, contractual responsibility and any recovery must be assessed under applicable law. An asset receipt acknowledgement alone is not a blanket authorization to deduct wages.

HR handles the employment review; **Finance processes a recovery only after its lawful basis has been verified**. The employee may raise a concern or dispute with HR.

## Asset Transfers and Changes

An asset may need to be reassigned when a person's role or department changes, equipment is replaced, or the original business need ends.

Recipients must not informally transfer company devices or access items to colleagues. The Department Head and HR/Admin should coordinate reassignment and keep the responsible person's record current.

Digital access changes follow the **Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning** SOP, not an informal hardware handover.

## Returning Company Assets

Company equipment, accessories, access cards, and keys must be returned when:

- an employee resigns or employment is terminated;
- an internship, freelance engagement, or consultancy ends;
- a device is replaced or no longer required;
- an approved role transfer or assignment change requires its return; or
- authorized management reasonably requests its return.

Employees must return the item and associated issued accessories, disclose any known damage or missing items, and cooperate with reasonable return arrangements.

The **Department Head and HR/Admin verify the return** under the official **Employee Offboarding** process. For departing employees, the separate offboarding checklist and any applicable signed handover requirements govern operational closeout; this employee-facing policy does not reproduce those forms.

Employees must not erase company data, remove company-owned files, or attempt to keep access after departure. Authorized account or data handling is coordinated under the security and offboarding SOPs.

## Asset Issues and Final Settlement

An unresolved asset issue should be documented and reviewed separately from the calculation of legally owed salary, unused leave, benefits, or other employee dues.

**Final wages and statutory entitlements must not be arbitrarily withheld or delayed because a device is missing or a clearance step remains open.** Any deduction or recovery must have an independent lawful basis and follow the applicable procedure.

The **Final Settlement & Employee Dues Policy** governs final payments and the **Employee Offboarding** SOP governs the practical return and clearance process.

## Responsibilities

**Employees, interns, and authorized contractors:** Use equipment properly, protect it, report problems promptly, and return it when due.

**Department Heads:** Confirm equipment needs, authorize permitted off-site use, coordinate transfers and returns, and advise HR/Admin about business impact.

**HR/Admin:** Coordinate issuance, employee acknowledgements, incident reporting, authorized repairs, assignment records, and return confirmation.

**Finance:** Handle payments or any legally verified asset-related financial recovery; do not make automatic wage deductions.

**System and security owners:** Manage software accounts, access changes, lost-device or data-security implications, and offboarding access controls under their governing SOPs.

## Questions and Disputes

Employees may contact HR/Admin about incorrect asset assignments, equipment faults, unauthorized-use concerns, disputed damage responsibility, or return records. HR should review the relevant facts fairly and communicate the outcome.
