# Acceptable Use of Company Technology Policy

## Purpose

Explain how everyone working with Blinto should use company technology responsibly, protect company and client information, and avoid disrupting work or exposing systems to avoidable risk.

Company technology exists primarily to support approved work. This policy sets everyday behavior expectations; the detailed rules for passwords, access permissions, devices, and offboarding remain in their own policies.

## Who and What This Covers

This policy applies to Blinto employees, including probationary and confirmed employees; paid interns; and freelancers or consultants who access Blinto or client systems.

It covers company laptops, desktops, phones and other devices; company email and Google Workspace; ClickUp and other project-management tools; GitHub, development and design platforms; company networks, software, subscriptions and client accounts; and company or client services accessed from an authorized personal device.

When a client agreement or an applicable security requirement imposes stricter conditions, follow the stricter condition.

## Work Use and Personal Use

**Company technology is primarily for authorized work.** Personal use of a company device, account or resource requires prior authorization, subject to the limited browsing exception below.

Brief, reasonable personal browsing during a normal break may be allowed if it does not create a security risk, interfere with work, incur unauthorized costs, or breach other rules. This does not authorize installing personal applications, accessing prohibited content, or treating company devices as personal property.

During working hours, personal social media, shopping, videos and unrelated browsing must not interfere with assigned duties, availability, meetings or client commitments.

Streaming, gaming and other entertainment on company equipment are not permitted without approval. Employees must not use company technology for private commercial work or outside engagements without authorization.

## Internet and Online Activity

When using company networks, devices or accounts:

- use internet access primarily for legitimate work;
- do not intentionally visit illegal, fraudulent, malicious or unsafe sites;
- do not use company resources for gambling, scams, harassment, threats or other prohibited activity;
- do not unlawfully download, copy, distribute or use copyrighted material, including software;
- do not bypass security blocks, filtering, access restrictions or protective settings; and
- avoid excessive unrelated browsing that disrupts work or consumes disproportionate resources.

If a legitimate assignment requires access to a site or service that is blocked, ask the appropriate authorized system owner rather than attempting to circumvent the restriction.

## Company Accounts, Email and Work Files

Use the **individual company account assigned to you** wherever the system supports named users. Company email is the normal channel for official work correspondence.

Do not share a personal work login, MFA code, account session, or access link with family members, colleagues or outsiders. Where a system genuinely requires shared access, only the specific arrangement approved under the **Credential & Password Management Policy** may be used.

Store work documents and approved deliverables in the appropriate company-controlled location, not in personal email, personal cloud storage or unauthorized accounts. Do not forward confidential company or client material to a personal email account merely for convenience.

Do not grant yourself or anyone else new access outside the **Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning** process. Access must be authorized and recorded in the authoritative Access Register before it is shared.

## Software, Licenses and Subscriptions

Employees may install or use software and applications for work only when appropriately authorized.

- Do not install unauthorized software, browser extensions or tools on company systems.
- Do not use pirated, unlawfully copied or unlicensed software.
- Do not share paid software seats or licenses beyond the applicable license terms.
- Seek approval before subscribing to a new paid service or creating an account that commits Blinto or a client to an expense.
- Follow stricter client-specific tool and security requirements.
- Report suspicious applications, downloads, prompts or security warnings to the responsible system owner.

Authorized system owners or IT personnel, where assigned, coordinate necessary software updates or technical changes. This policy does not imply Blinto currently operates a dedicated IT help desk or automated software-management platform.

## Generative AI Tools

Approved generative AI tools may be used to support work **only within the relevant company and client permissions**.

Employees must:

- avoid entering client credentials, passwords, API keys, sensitive employee information or other protected data into an **unapproved** AI service;
- obtain appropriate authorization before submitting confidential client source code, nonpublic documents or other restricted material to an AI tool;
- review AI-generated text, code, designs and analyses for accuracy, confidentiality, licensing, security and suitability before using or sharing them;
- remain responsible for their own deliverables, decisions and representations; and
- respect any client agreement that prohibits or restricts AI use.

AI output should never be assumed correct or cleared for public/client release simply because a tool generated it.

A separate **Responsible Use of AI Tools Policy** should define detailed permissions, permitted data, client restrictions, review standards and approved services. No specific AI tool is declared approved merely by being named or commonly used elsewhere.

## Everyday Security Expectations

Protect devices, accounts and company or client information with reasonable care.

Employees must:

- use MFA where required by the existing credential policy;
- protect passwords and authentication codes, and never disclose another person's credentials;
- not disable required device, browser or account security controls;
- avoid connecting unknown USB devices or opening suspicious files;
- use a reasonably secure network for sensitive work;
- store and back up work only through the applicable approved company arrangements; and
- report missing devices, suspected account compromise, unusual security warnings, or possible exposure of company/client information promptly.

Where possible, keep devices from being accessed by unauthorized people when unattended. Technical security configurations and exact backup methods are governed by authorized system owners and the existing security SOPs.

## Personal Devices Used for Work

Using a personal laptop or phone to access Blinto or client systems does not remove company confidentiality and access requirements.

Only authorized accounts and approved methods may be used. Employees must keep company data separate from unrelated personal sharing, protect confidential files from household or public access, and follow the required MFA and account-security controls.

Authorization to use a personal device does **not** grant Blinto unlimited access to personal accounts, unrelated personal files or private communications. Any legitimate examination of device-stored work data must have an appropriate lawful basis, scope and notice.

If personal-device use cannot meet a relevant client or company security requirement, the employee should discuss an approved alternative with the responsible owner.

## Privacy, Logs and Appropriate Review

Blinto may need to review company-system records or access information for legitimate security, compliance, incident response or operational purposes. **This policy does not claim that Blinto currently operates a particular monitoring tool, surveillance system or automated employee activity tracker.**

Any monitoring or review must be lawful, reasonably necessary, proportionate to its stated purpose, and subject to any required employee notice. Access to usage or activity information must be limited to authorized people with a need to know.

Company ownership of a device does not make all personal data on that device public. Employees' personal accounts and unrelated private information must not be accessed without lawful justification.

Personal data collected through any monitoring must be handled under the **Employee Data Privacy & Records Management Policy**, including applicable retention and disclosure safeguards. Information gathered for one purpose must not be repurposed arbitrarily.

## Misuse, Security Incidents and Fair Review

Report lost devices, shared credentials, suspected malware, suspicious software, accidental disclosures or unauthorized access promptly to **HR or the responsible system owner** using an appropriate private work channel.

The system owner coordinates immediate technical containment where necessary. HR reviews potential employment-policy concerns, including the employee's explanation and relevant context.

For credible security or client risks, an authorized owner may temporarily restrict an account, session or device before a final employment decision. This is a protective step, **not an automatic finding of misconduct**.

Employees should have an appropriate opportunity to explain alleged misuse. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to proportionate action under the **Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy**, with all applicable legal safeguards. No disciplinary punishment is authorized simply because a security alert or unverified allegation exists.

## Responsibilities

**Employees, interns and authorized collaborators** use only permitted tools and accounts, protect work information, ask for necessary approvals, and report concerns promptly.

**Department Heads** reinforce appropriate work use, identify tool needs, and escalate suspected misuse or security issues.

**Authorized system and data owners** manage technical permissions, tools and necessary protective actions under their established SOPs.

**People Operations / HR** coordinates employee-policy questions, fair review of alleged misuse, and privacy-related concerns. Contractual or client-security issues are escalated to the appropriate responsible owner.

## Current Problems and Improvements

Blinto has identified several problems this policy is intended to address: unapproved software, unnecessary personal browsing or entertainment, informal account sharing, work files sent via personal email, unclear AI boundaries, and inconsistent device-security practices.

This **draft proposes a common employee standard**. It does not assert that software approvals, monitoring, device controls, incident systems or an approved AI-tool catalog are already fully operational. Any system-level improvements should be implemented and communicated by the relevant owners.

## Applicable Law

This policy is intended to operate consistently with applicable Bangladesh law, including:

- the **Personal Data Protection Act, 2026**, concerning lawful personal-data processing, confidentiality and appropriate security;
- the **Cyber Protection Act, 2026** and subsequent amendments, concerning unlawful access and other applicable cyber offenses; and
- the **Copyright Act, 2023**, including legal rights and restrictions relevant to software and copyrighted works.

These laws do not authorize unrestricted employee surveillance, automatic employment penalties or access to unrelated private accounts. Any specific investigation, monitoring practice or legal duty must be assessed in light of the law then in force, including relevant commencement or implementing provisions.
