# Payroll Processing SOP

## Purpose

Explain how Blinto prepares, checks, approves, pays and corrects monthly employee salaries in a reliable, confidential and legally compliant way.

This document has two sections: **Part A** explains what employees can expect; **Part B** describes the HR and Finance process. The separate Compensation & Salary Administration, Attendance, Leave, Overtime and Final Settlement policies govern the underlying entitlements; this SOP governs how payroll is administered.

## Scope

The monthly employee payroll process covers confirmed and probationary employees, paid interns, Department Heads and management, according to their applicable employment or internship terms. New joiners and departing employees are included for partial-month calculations where applicable.

---

# Part A — Employee Payroll Guidance

## When Employees Are Paid

Blinto's chosen company standard is **monthly salary payment by the end of the current calendar month**, by bank transfer to verified employee bank details.

The payroll team must plan its internal preparation and approvals to meet that date. An internal processing cutoff is necessary; **the exact cutoff date has not yet been confirmed** and must be communicated once management sets it. For overtime only, the [Overtime Policy](../people-and-culture/overtime-policy.md) sets a fixed counting cycle from the **20th of one month to the 19th of the next**, with the **20th as the overtime cutoff**.

If an issue is discovered after payroll closing, HR and Finance will arrange an appropriately authorized correction. A subsequent adjustment is not permission to defer a legally overdue wage payment.

## What Salary Payments May Include

Depending on eligibility and approval, a payment may include:

- approved monthly gross salary or an applicable prorated amount;
- an approved salary revision effective during the month;
- legally payable, verified overtime;
- a festival bonus when due under the governing Festival Bonus Policy or applicable law/terms; and
- other amounts properly authorized and payable under law or the relevant agreement.

Any deduction must also have a valid legal and contractual basis. Possible deductions or adjustments include applicable taxes, legally permitted contributions, verified leave without pay, authorized meal-program contributions **only where legally permitted**, and other independently lawful items.

**An employee's consent or an internal policy entry alone does not make a deduction lawful if the Labour Act restricts that category.** There will be no automatic deductions for damaged assets, training expenses or informal disciplinary fines.

## Attendance, Leave and Salary

HR verifies attendance and leave before finalizing payroll inputs. A missing HRMS check-in does **not**, by itself, establish that an employee did not work.

- Approved paid leave is not treated as leave without pay.
- Verified unpaid leave may affect pay using the applicable calculation rules.
- Attendance errors are handled under the **Attendance Correction & Exception Handling SOP**.
- Approved overtime is calculated under the **Overtime Policy**, not inferred from time online, ClickUp activity or late check-outs alone.
- Where HRMS data is wrong or incomplete, HR reviews other reasonable evidence before treating time as unpaid.

An unresolved technical or administrative HRMS issue must not be used to arbitrarily withhold earned wages.

## Payslips and Payroll Questions

Blinto will provide employees with a **payslip or clear salary breakdown** showing relevant earnings, approved deductions and net pay. This is a required implementation improvement because salary breakdowns have not been delivered consistently.

Employees can contact **HR** privately if they believe an amount, attendance entry, bank detail or deduction is wrong. HR and Finance review the issue and explain the outcome as appropriate. Verified underpayments must be corrected promptly; statutory payment deadlines remain applicable.

Employees should inform HR promptly of changes to bank details through a secure, approved process. Do not post bank details, salary information or payslips in public Telegram groups or broadly accessible ClickUp tasks.

## Final Salary and Other Dues

An employee's final pay, unused leave payments, benefits and other exit entitlements are addressed in the **Final Settlement & Employee Dues Policy** and the offboarding process. Payroll data should support those calculations, but this SOP does not replace the exit-payment rules or statutory payment deadlines.

# Part B — Internal HR and Finance Procedure

## 1. Establish the Payroll Period and Cutoff

Blinto follows a **monthly payroll period**. HR and Finance should establish an internal processing cutoff sufficiently early to verify attendance, changes and bank-transfer readiness while meeting the company commitment to pay by the end of the current month.

## 2. HR Prepares Employee and Employment Inputs

HR compiles or verifies the minimum relevant inputs from approved records:

- employee identity and the correct, securely verified bank-payment details;
- employment or paid-internship status, joining date, departure date and current approved gross salary;
- effective dates of approved annual (including first-year join-month adjusted), promotion-related or other salary revisions;
- HRMS attendance, valid late-arrival exceptions and any corrected check-in/out records;
- approved paid leave, leave without pay and applicable leave-balance corrections;
- approved and verified overtime for the applicable 20th-to-19th overtime counting cycle, with its applicable legal wage basis;
- festival-bonus entitlement when due;
- authorized deductions, including valid meal-program participation where lawful; and
- relevant payroll corrections from earlier periods.

HR uses controlled personnel records as the authoritative source for employment approvals. **Performance Tracker assessment or a manager's verbal commitment is not, by itself, an authorization to change salary.** Changes are applied only after completing the governing Promotion & Salary Review approval process and receiving written confirmation of the amount and effective date.

## 3. Confirm Attendance and Payroll Exceptions

HR reviews material inconsistencies before handing over final payroll inputs. A Department Head verifies actual work time or approved arrangements where necessary, without independently altering official attendance or salary records.

An unresolved missed check-in or incorrect automated warning must be examined under the Attendance Correction SOP; it must not silently be converted into unpaid leave.

Approved leave and valid schedule exceptions must be excluded from attendance-related unpaid deductions. Any case affecting statutory rights, a dispute, or a payroll deadline should be escalated promptly rather than left unresolved until the next routine cycle.

## 4. Finance Calculates Pay

Finance calculates gross and net payable amounts using approved salary records and HR-confirmed inputs. HR and Finance then verify material calculations and changes together.

### Company calculation baseline

Blinto's agreed internal default is a **fixed 30-day divisor** for eligible prorated monthly salary and leave-without-pay calculations, **unless a different method is required by applicable law, a wage order, employment terms or another governing policy**.

For an eligible internal calculation, the illustrative method is:

`applicable daily salary amount = approved applicable monthly salary amount ÷ 30`

`prorated or unpaid-leave adjustment = applicable daily amount × verified eligible days`

This is **not** a universal statutory formula. HR and Finance must confirm whether gross salary or a legally specified wage component is the appropriate base for each calculation. Statutory overtime, maternity benefits, accrued leave encashment, minimum wages, final dues and other mandated calculations must use their legally required bases, even when different from 30-day gross proration.

For partial months, Finance must also check approved effective dates and avoid duplicate or overlapping salary periods.

### Statutory and authorized amounts

Finance applies:

- payroll tax withholding and remittance where legally required;
- any lawful provident-fund, insurance, welfare-fund or other contribution **only where a relevant scheme and deduction requirement actually applies**;
- statutory wage floors and any legally required increments or adjustments;
- approved overtime using the lawfully required rate and wage components for covered workers; and
- other properly verified payments or deductions permitted by applicable law.

**No automatic deduction** is permitted for company asset loss, equipment damage, training fees, generalized penalties, or an unverified attendance issue. An ordinary written repayment agreement is not a substitute for restrictions imposed by the Labour Act.

### Meal-program participation

The separate Compensation Policy describes an optional meal contribution arrangement. HR must verify actual enrollment or authorization, and Finance must verify that any proposed payroll deduction is **independently permitted by applicable wage-deduction law**. If that basis is not established, the cost must not be deducted from protected wages; an appropriate alternative payment mechanism must be reviewed.

## 5. Review, Approval and Separation of Duties

The proposed approval path is:

**HR prepares and verifies employment inputs → Finance calculates and checks payroll → HR reviews employee-specific adjustments → Chief of Staff approves final payroll → Finance executes approved payments.**

Department Heads provide fact-checking only when an operational or attendance question requires it. HR owns employment and attendance records; Finance owns calculation, authorized payment execution and financial evidence.

The process must ensure that one person does not independently prepare, approve **and** execute the same payroll. In a smaller team, compensating independent review is required where full role separation is impractical.

**Chief of Staff final payroll approval is the selected rule for this draft; the current approval authority was identified as unclear and this sequence requires formal adoption.** This SOP does not claim an existing automated approval workflow, bank integration or payroll application.

## 6. Make Bank Payments and Issue Salary Breakdowns

Following approval, Finance confirms payment readiness, verifies the final bank-payment information through the approved secure process, and executes authorized bank transfers.

Finance retains appropriate evidence of the payment date, transferred amount, employee or transaction reference and any failed/rejected transfer. The team should investigate rejected payments promptly and arrange lawful completion.

HR and Finance coordinate delivery of the employee's payslip or salary breakdown through an appropriately restricted channel. The applicable Labour Rules prescribe a wage slip and wage records for covered workers, including earnings, overtime, deductions and net payable wages; the legally required form or equivalent compliant record must be respected.

## 7. Handle Errors, Underpayments and Overpayments

When an employee reports a concern, HR should review the employee's explanation, approved salary terms, attendance, leave, statutory components and other relevant records with Finance.

- **Underpayment:** Correct verified errors promptly and within applicable legal deadlines. If a normal payroll cycle is closed, use an authorized supplementary payment when necessary rather than wait unlawfully.
- **Overpayment:** Explain the discrepancy to the employee and determine whether recovery is legally permitted. Do not make an automatic future payroll deduction merely because Finance found an overpayment.
- **Attendance or leave error:** Use the governing correction process, update the controlled source record, then reconcile the payroll impact.
- **Wrong bank details or failed transfer:** Verify the issue privately, correct the authorized record, and arrange appropriate payment without sharing banking data in public channels.

HR and Finance should maintain a **traceable correction record**, including the period, reason, review, decision, payment impact and resolution. A centrally maintained correction log is a proposed improvement, not a system Blinto currently confirms exists.

## 8. Reconcile Payroll and Retain Records

Finance reconciles approved payable totals against actual bank payments and any outstanding rejected or corrected items. HR confirms that personnel changes and employee-facing payroll records match the finalized approved outcome.

Payroll and wage records must be kept in an appropriately restricted location for the period required by applicable law and any valid records policy. HRMS attendance data may contribute to payroll, but **HRMS is not presumed to perform automated payroll calculations, approvals or bank reconciliation**.

Relevant Bangladesh Labour Rules require prescribed wage records and payslips for covered workers; HR and Finance should verify the applicable current forms, retention requirements and 2022 amendments before implementation.

## 9. Closeout and Escalation

HR and Finance should resolve open questions affecting lawful payment, employee entitlements or record accuracy promptly. Where a dispute cannot be resolved through ordinary HR/Finance review, escalate to the Chief of Staff or an appropriate legally required forum without preventing employees from exercising statutory wage remedies.

Final dues for departing employees are calculated and approved under the separate Final Settlement policy, using verified payroll inputs and applicable Bangladesh law.

## Legal Safeguards — Bangladesh (November 2026)

This SOP is intended to comply with the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended through 2026**, and the **Bangladesh Labour Rules, 2015, as amended**, together with any applicable wage orders, employment agreements and lawful service rules.

- **Wage periods and deadlines:** Sections **122–123** prescribe wage periods not exceeding one month and, for covered workers, payment within seven working days after the wage period. Blinto's internal **end-of-current-month target** is earlier and should remain the operating standard. Section 123 separately addresses payments after termination; consult the Final Settlement policy and current law for the correct exit deadline.
- **Legal responsibility:** Section **121** places responsibility for legally owed wages on the employer and other specified responsible persons; an internal Chief of Staff approval step does not remove that duty.
- **Overtime:** Section **108** provides the relevant double-rate statutory overtime entitlement for covered workers, on the applicable statutory wage basis; company gross salary is not automatically the correct overtime divisor or base.
- **Deductions:** Sections **125–127** restrict permissible wage deductions, including absence deductions and deductions for entrusted-property loss or damage. Evidence, proper procedures, permitted categories and statutory limits must be observed. A general signature, meal enrollment or company agreement does not override these restrictions.
- **Wage registers and wage slips:** Labour Rules **111–112** address wage records, wage slips and payment information, including the prescribed Form 38 for covered workers, subject to applicable amendments. HR and Finance must maintain the legally required records and provide compliant salary information.
- **Tax and other contributions:** Withhold and remit amounts required by applicable tax law or any other legally operative scheme. This SOP does **not** assume that a universal provident fund, pension, social-insurance or payroll contribution applies to every Blinto employee.
