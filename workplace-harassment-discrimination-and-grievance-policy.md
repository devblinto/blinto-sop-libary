# Workplace Harassment, Discrimination & Grievance Policy

## Purpose

Everyone at Blinto should be able to work with dignity, respect, and a reasonable sense of safety. This policy explains unacceptable workplace behavior, how to raise concerns, what happens after a complaint, and the protections available to everyone involved.

Blinto does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, bullying, violence, or abuse of authority. Every concern will be assessed fairly; no person is presumed to have committed misconduct simply because a complaint is made.

## Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to permanent and probationary employees, interns, trainees, temporary workers, contractors, and consultants while working with Blinto. It also covers work-related interactions with clients, vendors, visitors, and other third parties.

It applies in the office; during company events, business travel, and client work; and in remote work, online meetings, emails, calls, and work-related messages. Relevant protections apply regardless of a person's job title or seniority.

Where a person's legal employment classification differs, applicable statutory rights and procedures must still be observed.

## Our Standards

Employees and everyone working with Blinto are expected to:

- treat others respectfully and honor their personal boundaries;
- avoid intimidation, humiliating comments, aggressive behavior, and misuse of authority;
- communicate professionally, including during disagreements;
- report immediate threats to safety promptly; and
- cooperate honestly with a fair, confidential review when asked.

A reasonable, respectful performance discussion, work instruction, or evidence-based feedback is not automatically harassment. However, managerial authority must not be used to threaten, humiliate, coerce, or unfairly target another person.

## Unacceptable Behavior

### Harassment and Bullying

Examples include unwelcome insults, offensive jokes or messages, repeated belittling, targeted humiliation, intimidation, or persistent unwanted personal contact. A single serious incident can be unacceptable; repetition is not always necessary.

### Sexual Harassment

Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, sexual comments or messages, sharing sexual images, unwanted touching, and pressure tied to work opportunities are prohibited. A person's silence, seniority, or prior friendly relationship does not automatically imply consent.

### Discrimination

Employees and others covered by this policy must not be treated unfairly because of legally protected characteristics. Bangladesh law, as amended, specifically addresses discrimination involving such grounds as race, color, sex, gender identity, religion, political opinion, nationality, social origin, descent, and disability. Blinto also expects respectful treatment more broadly.

### Violence and Abuse of Authority

Physical assault, threats of violence, aggressive intimidation, coercion, abuse of a reporting relationship, or pressure to tolerate improper behavior are prohibited.

These examples are illustrative, not an exhaustive list.

## Raising a Concern

You do not need to confront the person involved before seeking help.

Depending on the circumstances, you may report a concern:

1. **Directly to People Operations / HR.**
2. **To your Department Head**, if appropriate and that person is not involved in the concern.
3. **To the Chief of Staff or appropriate senior leadership** when the concern involves HR, a Department Head, a serious conflict of interest, or a disputed complaint.

Blinto also intends to establish a **confidential complaint option in HRMS** and an **official confidential complaint email address**. **These dedicated channels are not yet confirmed as operational.** Until HR communicates their availability and contact details, use the existing direct HR or suitable leadership route. Employees must not be required to report solely to the person about whom they are complaining.

Anonymous information may be submitted for preliminary review where a receiving route is available. Blinto will assess what can responsibly be checked, although anonymity can limit follow-up or fact-finding. Do not put intimate or sensitive complaint details in an open ClickUp task, shared chat group, or other broadly visible location.

For an immediate danger, prioritize personal safety and contact local emergency services or appropriate authorities when necessary, as well as notifying Blinto when safe to do so.

## What Happens After a Complaint

### 1. Acknowledgement

For an ordinary complaint received by HR, Blinto's proposed service standard is to acknowledge receipt **within three working days**. Urgent safety concerns should receive immediate attention. An acknowledgement is not a finding about what happened.

### 2. Explanation of Next Steps

HR will explain, as far as appropriate, who will review the concern, whether a specialist complaints committee is required, and how the employee can share further information.

### 3. Fair Review

Relevant facts and available evidence will be considered impartially. The person raising the concern can explain their experience and provide relevant information. Anyone accused of wrongdoing will also have a fair opportunity to respond, with the specific legal disciplinary safeguards applied if formal punishment is considered.

Serious harassment, discrimination, or violence complaints must be directed to the appropriate formal investigation process. A complainant is not required to accept mediation or directly meet the accused person.

### 4. Progress and Outcome

The people involved will receive reasonable updates when a review takes longer than expected. HR or the appropriate committee will communicate an outcome or case closure to the extent that privacy, safety, and legal requirements allow. Confidential personnel action concerning another employee may not be disclosed in detail.

An employee who believes material evidence was missed or the review was unfair may ask HR or an appropriate independent senior reviewer to reconsider the handling of the concern. This does not replace an external legal remedy.

## Complaints Committee

Bangladesh Labour Act section 332A, introduced by the 2026 amendment, requires employers to establish a **five-member complaints redressal committee** for discrimination, violence, and harassment. The law specifies a woman chairperson, a majority of women members from the establishment, and two members from organizations working on gender and sexual-abuse issues.

**Blinto has not yet confirmed that this committee has been constituted.** The required committee must be established and its accessible reporting arrangements communicated before the company represents the formal committee process as operational. Until then, HR should receive and escalate complaints promptly, while Blinto arranges lawful committee review and does not treat the interim HR channel as a replacement for the legally required body.

Committee reviews must follow the applicable legislation, rules, and legally relevant High Court guidance, including privacy and procedural protections.

## Confidentiality, Safety and Protection

- Complaint information will be limited to people who need it to receive, investigate, decide, or lawfully review the concern. **Absolute secrecy cannot be promised** where disclosure is necessary for a fair review or legal compliance.
- Blinto may take appropriate temporary steps to protect those involved, such as adjusting reporting lines, work arrangements, or access where justified. Temporary measures are not findings of guilt and should not unfairly penalize the person reporting.
- **Retaliation is prohibited** against a person who raises a concern in good faith, assists in an inquiry, or exercises a lawful right. Report any retaliation to HR or an uninvolved senior leader promptly.
- The dignity, privacy, and fair treatment of both the complainant and the person responding to an allegation must be respected.
- A complaint that cannot be substantiated is **not automatically dishonest or malicious**. Knowingly fabricated allegations, if established after a fair process, may be considered separately.

## Ordinary Workplace Grievances

Concerns about working relationships, unfair treatment, communication, or other employment problems that do not require a specialist discrimination/harassment inquiry may be raised with the Department Head or HR.

HR will help clarify the concern, hear relevant perspectives, and propose an appropriate next step. Serious or sensitive allegations must not be downgraded to an informal grievance merely to avoid a proper investigation.

Where an applicable statutory grievance procedure governs a matter, its deadlines and rights take precedence over this internal process.

## Corrective Action and Employee Rights

If a review establishes a violation, Blinto may take proportionate action under the applicable **Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy** and the law. Serious misconduct may require a separate statutory disciplinary process.

For covered disciplinary cases, Bangladesh Labour Act section 24 requires written allegations, **at least seven days to explain**, a hearing opportunity, and the applicable inquiry process. This cannot be replaced by an ordinary three-working-day complaint acknowledgement or internal explanation practice.

Employees retain any right to contact relevant public authorities, pursue statutory grievances, or obtain legal advice. Blinto's internal complaint process does not remove those rights.

## Responsibilities

**Every employee or covered worker:** Treat others respectfully, raise immediate safety concerns, and avoid retaliation or interference with a complaint.

**Department Heads:** Promote respectful teams, receive appropriate grievances, protect privacy, and refer sensitive concerns to HR rather than attempting to resolve conflicts in which they are personally involved.

**People Operations / HR:** Provide an accessible initial reporting route, acknowledge ordinary complaints, coordinate fair reviews, communicate appropriate updates, and refer matters requiring committee or independent review.

**Chief of Staff / Authorized Leadership:** Review serious, disputed, or conflicted matters where appropriate, without displacing the statutory complaints committee.
