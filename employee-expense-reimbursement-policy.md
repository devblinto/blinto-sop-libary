# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

Explain when Blinto reimburses employees for money they spend on authorized company work, how to obtain approval, and how to submit a claim. The goal is to make legitimate business expenses easy to understand, fairly reviewed and properly accounted for.

Reimbursement is repayment of an eligible business expense, not an automatic salary increase, allowance or employee benefit.

## Who Is Eligible?

Confirmed and probationary employees, paid interns, Department Heads and team leaders may request reimbursement for authorized business expenses they personally incur on Blinto's behalf. Eligibility depends on the expense and its approval, not seniority.

## Expenses Blinto May Reimburse

Subject to approval, business purpose and sufficient supporting information, eligible expenses may include:

- official business travel and reasonable transportation;
- approved client meetings, business meals and hospitality;
- approved software subscriptions, work tools and project-related purchases;
- company-approved training, courses, certifications and examination fees;
- authorized office supplies and other necessary work materials;
- official courier and delivery expenses;
- justified emergency business expenses; and
- other necessary business expenses approved by management.

An employee should not assume that a purchase qualifies merely because it might benefit Blinto. Obtain approval before committing company funds or spending personal money with an expectation of reimbursement.

## Expenses Normally Not Reimbursed

Blinto does not normally reimburse:

- regular travel between home and office;
- personal meals, refreshments, shopping or entertainment;
- unauthorized software subscriptions or purchases;
- personal mobile, internet or home-working expenses without approval;
- traffic fines, personal penalties or other private liabilities;
- expenses already paid directly by Blinto or previously reimbursed;
- claims without sufficient supporting information; or
- expenses unrelated to authorized company work.

The optional office meal program is governed by the Compensation & Salary Administration Policy and applicable meal arrangements. It is not a general personal meal reimbursement entitlement.

## Obtain Approval Before Spending

Discuss the expense with your Department Head before spending. Explain the business purpose, expected amount and any relevant project or client requirement.

The Department Head confirms the business need. Finance verifies budget availability and the applicable financial authorization. HR reviews employee-related expenses where relevant.

**Finance may approve routine expenses only within formally delegated authority. The Chief of Staff is the final approval authority for expenses outside that delegation, including significant or exceptional expenses.** Where no applicable delegation has been established, obtain Chief of Staff approval before spending.

No specific monetary approval threshold is established by this policy. Any delegated limits must be formally approved and communicated separately.

Where a separate policy requires a particular approval, that approval must also be obtained. For example, company-funded training and company-wide AI subscriptions require the relevant Chief of Staff approval and Finance budget or payment approval. An expense approval does not replace software, security, client or procurement authorization.

### Emergency expenses

If a genuine urgent business need makes prior approval impractical, contact the responsible Department Head or authorized manager as soon as reasonably possible. Explain the circumstances and submit supporting information afterward. HR and Finance review the claim, and the Chief of Staff approves exceptional reimbursement where justified. Emergency spending is not automatically reimbursable.

## How to Request Reimbursement

Submit your claim through the approved company process. Employee-related claims should be submitted through HR for coordination with Finance; ask HR or Finance for the appropriate restricted submission location if unsure. Do not post receipts containing sensitive information in public Telegram groups or broadly accessible ClickUp tasks.

Provide the date of the expense, actual amount and currency, business purpose, relevant client or project where applicable, receipt or invoice, and evidence of prior approval. If a receipt is unavailable, explain why and provide other reasonable evidence of the actual expense. Finance may request additional information before approving the claim.

**Submit as soon as reasonably possible, normally within seven calendar days of incurring the expense.** HR and Finance may consider justified late requests, including emergencies, delayed receipts or other circumstances outside the employee's control. A late request does not automatically extinguish a lawful or contractual entitlement.

## Review, Approval and Payment

The Department Head confirms that the expense was necessary for work. HR reviews employee-related matters and coordinates relevant records. Finance verifies receipts, amounts, prior approval, budget and whether the expense has already been paid.

Finance approves routine claims within documented delegated authority. The Chief of Staff gives final approval for claims requiring higher authorization, including exceptional expenses. Employees must not approve their own claims; an uninvolved authorized approver must review them instead.

Finance processes authorized reimbursements and retains appropriate payment evidence. Reimbursements may be paid separately from monthly salary and must be identifiable as expense payments in the financial records. Finance should communicate the expected payment arrangement and any material delay. No fixed payment day or automatic reimbursement system is established by this draft.

The same expense cannot be reimbursed twice. Only actual eligible amounts are payable, after accounting for refunds, discounts, direct company payments and applicable advances.

## Travel, Transportation and Client Meetings

Official business travel requires prior approval. Employees should use reasonable, cost-effective transportation where practical and submit the actual approved cost.

Regular commuting is not reimbursable. Exceptional transportation required for approved after-hours work may be reimbursed with specific approval. This does not replace any applicable overtime compensation or working-time protection.

Outstation travel and accommodation require separate approval before booking. Client-meeting meals and hospitality must be approved, reasonable and connected to a legitimate business purpose. Follow the Confidentiality & Conflict of Interest Policy for gifts, hospitality and personal benefits.

No fixed travel allowance, daily meal allowance, mileage rate or accommodation ceiling is established by this policy. Any such arrangement must be separately authorized.

## Software, Equipment, Training and Remote Work

Employees must obtain the relevant tool, subscription, training or equipment approvals before purchase. The Responsible Use of AI Tools, Acceptable Use of Company Technology and Employee Training & Development policies continue to govern their respective approvals and permitted uses.

Company-funded equipment and subscriptions remain subject to company ownership, access, licensing and return requirements. Reimbursement does not transfer company-owned property or subscriptions to an employee for unrestricted personal use. HR/Admin coordinates asset records where applicable.

Approved remote work does not automatically authorize reimbursement of personal internet, electricity, furniture or equipment. Any business expense must be separately approved under the relevant arrangements.

## Company Advances

If Blinto provides an approved advance for an official expense, the recipient must account for the money using actual eligible expenses and supporting documents. Unused amounts must be returned through an approved Finance arrangement. Finance reconciles the advance against approved costs so that the employee is not reimbursed twice.

A disputed advance or missing receipt does not authorize an automatic salary deduction. Any proposed recovery must have a lawful basis, be communicated to the employee and comply with applicable wage-deduction requirements. A written agreement alone does not override statutory restrictions.

## Questions, Disputes and Incorrect Claims

Employees may ask HR or Finance to explain a rejected, delayed or partially approved claim. Finance may request further evidence, and the employee may provide clarification or dispute an incorrect calculation. Unresolved matters may be referred to the Chief of Staff for an authorized review.

Good-faith mistakes or missing receipts should be distinguished from intentional falsification. False or duplicate claims may lead to proportionate corrective action following fair review and the employee's opportunity to respond. No automatic disciplinary fine or salary deduction is introduced by this policy.

On separation, outstanding legitimate claims and advances should be reconciled with Finance separately from earned wages and statutory final dues. An expense dispute must not be used to arbitrarily delay or withhold protected salary or final settlement amounts.

## Confidentiality and Financial Records

HR and Finance should maintain relevant expense approvals, receipts, invoices, payment evidence and advance reconciliations in an approved restricted location. Access is limited to authorized personnel who need the information for work.

Finance determines applicable tax, VAT, withholding and accounting treatment. Genuine business expense repayments should be separately identified from salary and personal allowances; calling a payment a reimbursement does not automatically make it tax-exempt. Employees should provide appropriate receipts or tax invoices where required. Finance must retain records for the legally applicable period and use the applicable tax documentation requirements.

## Responsibilities

**Employees and paid interns:** Obtain approval, spend responsibly, submit accurate claims promptly, preserve evidence and return unused advances.

**Department Heads:** Confirm business necessity and coordinate authorization before expenditure; verify claims and avoid approving their own expenses.

**People Operations / HR:** Explain the policy, coordinate employee-related claims, support fair handling of disputes and protect employee information.

**Finance:** Verify budget, documents and calculations; exercise only authorized delegated approval; process approved payments; reconcile advances and retain financial records.

**Chief of Staff:** Approve expenses and reimbursements outside Finance's delegated authority, including significant and exceptional cases.
