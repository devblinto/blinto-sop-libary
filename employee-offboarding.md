# Employee Offboarding

## Purpose

Explain what employees can expect when leaving Blinto, what they need to hand over, how company property is returned, and how final clearance and payments are handled. We aim to make every departure professional, respectful, secure, and straightforward.

## Scope

This SOP applies when an employee leaves through resignation, an authorized termination, retirement, or completion of an applicable employment arrangement. Interns and other team members follow the relevant engagement terms and applicable law, with these steps used where appropriate.

The **Resignation & Notice Period Policy** covers notice and early release. The **Leave & Time-Off Policy** covers leave during notice. The **Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy** covers corrective processes. This SOP focuses on the **exit and clearance experience**, not those separate rules.

## When Offboarding Begins

- **Resignation:** The process begins when HR receives the employee's resignation through HRMS. HR confirms the applicable last working date and explains the next steps.
- **Termination:** The process begins after an authorized termination decision and the required communication to the employee, subject to lawful immediate protective measures where necessary.
- HR communicates the final/effective date, the responsible Department Head, and the steps relevant to the departure.
- The offboarding arrangements do not by themselves change the employee's lawful notice, pay, or other entitlements.

## What Employees Need to Do

### 1. Complete Work and Knowledge Handover

Before leaving, employees should work with their Department Head to transfer responsibilities to the person designated to receive them. This normally includes:

- updating assigned **ClickUp** tasks and making their current status clear;
- explaining pending projects, deadlines, client commitments, and unresolved problems;
- transferring necessary company work documents and files to approved company-controlled locations; and
- explaining any recurring work or follow-up that will remain after departure.

Employees must not deliberately delete, conceal, or transfer company or client information to personal accounts. They must not share personal passwords to hand over access. The Department Head will coordinate any necessary reassignment or access change through the approved process.

### 2. Sign the Handover Checklist

Blinto will use a **separate Handover Checklist** to confirm the work and responsibilities transferred. The checklist is **not reproduced in this employee-facing SOP**.

- The **departing employee** and the **receiving person**—a newly assigned colleague, replacement employee, or Team/Department Lead—must each review and sign or electronically acknowledge the handover.
- The Department Head verifies that ownership of outstanding work has been assigned appropriately.
- Both parties should record any incomplete, unavailable, or disputed items **before signing**. A signature confirms what was actually reviewed and received; it does not imply that undisclosed or outstanding items were completed.
- If the receiving employee has not yet joined, the Department Head or an authorized interim owner may receive and acknowledge the handover. A replacement employee's signature is not required before they join.
- If a signature cannot reasonably be obtained, HR records the reason and coordinates an alternative documented acknowledgment. An employee may add a written comment if they disagree with an item.

The handover checklist complements Blinto's existing **Employee Offboarding Checklist**. Employees do not need to complete internal IT or HR administrative checklists themselves.

### 3. Return Company Property

Employees must return or account for company-owned items issued to them, where applicable, including laptops, monitors, accessories, identification or access cards, keys, storage devices, records, and other assigned property.

Employees should promptly report any item that is missing or damaged so that HR/Admin can review the circumstances fairly. Normal wear and tear is not automatically employee misconduct or an automatic payroll deduction. Any claim for loss or damage must be supported by applicable law, the relevant agreement, evidence, and a fair review.

### 4. Protect Information and Access

Company and client files must remain in approved company-controlled systems. Employees must not download or forward confidential work materials to personal storage, remove records, or keep access after departure.

Access is normally removed at the appropriate separation time and may be restricted earlier where a legitimate safety or security risk requires proportionate protective action. HR and the relevant system owners coordinate this; employees should not attempt to retain or transfer accounts on their own.

## Final Clearance

Final clearance confirms that the work handover, assigned property, outstanding administrative items, and relevant account access have been addressed.

- The **Department Head** verifies work handover and the receiving owner's acknowledgment.
- **HR/Admin** confirms returned property or records any outstanding asset issue.
- The responsible **IT/system owner** confirms access closure.
- **Finance** reviews approved reimbursements, payable amounts, and any separately documented lawful claims.
- **HR** informs the employee whether clearance is complete and explains any unresolved issue and the next step.

An unresolved checklist item should be documented with a responsible person and a proposed resolution, rather than left unexplained. **Clearance delays do not automatically suspend statutory wages or final dues.**

## Final Settlement and Exit Documents

Finance calculates and processes the employee's final settlement, and HR helps explain the statement. Subject to applicable eligibility and lawful calculations, it may include:

- salary and other earned amounts due up to the applicable final payable date;
- payment for eligible unused Annual Leave under the **Leave & Time-Off Policy**;
- approved outstanding reimbursements;
- applicable statutory or contractual compensation, gratuity, or other benefits; and
- separately identified and lawfully supportable notice-pay claims or other authorized adjustments under the **Resignation & Notice Period Policy**.

Employees may ask HR to explain or correct a disputed item. No item is deducted solely because a manager considers handover incomplete. Where the Bangladesh Labour Act applies, section 30 requires the employer to pay final dues **within 30 working days after employment ends**. Any stronger legally applicable requirement also applies. Blinto must not make timely payment conditional on an optional exit interview or an unreasonable clearance delay.

Employees will receive a **final settlement statement** and an **employment/service certificate where legally applicable**. An **experience letter** may be requested and issued according to Blinto's approved eligibility and verification process. Required legal certificates should not be withheld merely because a discretionary experience-letter request is pending.

## Exit Discussion

For resigning employees, HR may invite the employee to an exit discussion to understand their experience and identify improvements. Employees may share workplace feedback confidentially, subject to the normal limits of confidentiality and any legal reporting duties. An exit discussion is **not mandatory for every departure** and must not delay lawful final payments or required documents.

## Involuntary Termination Approval

Blinto's internal approval flow for involuntary termination is:

**Department Lead recommendation → People Operations/HR review → CEO final approval.**

HR communicates the authorized decision and relevant next steps to the employee. A corrective-action investigation, required notice, opportunity to respond, or other statutory protection must be completed where applicable. Urgent system-access restrictions for genuine security concerns do not, by themselves, establish misconduct or constitute final termination approval.

## Questions, Disagreements, and Exceptions

Employees should contact HR if a handover item is inaccurate, a receiving person is unavailable, property is disputed, a payment appears incorrect, or an exit document is delayed. Employees may submit supporting information and request written clarification.

Blinto will consider genuine emergencies or circumstances outside the employee's control, including sudden or immediate departures where normal handover is not practical. The Department Head and HR will document a reasonable alternative without treating an impossible handover as automatic misconduct.

Employee rights under applicable law, contracts, or more favorable approved policies remain protected. This policy does not authorize an unlawful deduction, withholding of final dues, or termination without the applicable procedure.
