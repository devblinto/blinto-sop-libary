# Employee Onboarding

## Purpose

Help every new Blinto employee begin work with clear expectations, the right tools, an introduction to the team, and practical support. New employees should know what to do before joining, what to expect on their first day, and whom to contact when something is missing or unclear.

## Scope

This SOP applies to new employees joining Blinto. Interns and other engagement types follow their applicable agreement and the [Internship Program](./internship-program.md) or other relevant SOP, with this onboarding approach used where appropriate.

Onboarding is separate from recruitment, probation confirmation, and recurring performance reviews. Those decisions follow their own policies.

## Core Principle

Blinto aims to make joining straightforward and welcoming. Employees are expected to provide accurate information, participate in relevant orientation and training, and ask for help when needed. In return, Blinto will explain the role, make reasonable arrangements for necessary equipment and access, and communicate expectations clearly.

## Employee Onboarding Checklist

Blinto maintains an official [Employee Onboarding Checklist](./employee-onboarding-checklist.md) in the **SOP Library**, and the checklist is also referenced in **Blinto HRMS**. **All employee onboarding must be carried out and tracked according to this checklist.**

HR coordinates the checklist with the Department Head and other responsible teams. Employees should complete the steps that apply to them, provide any required acknowledgment, and inform HR when something is missing or cannot be completed. HR will confirm progress and follow up on outstanding items through the approved onboarding process.

The checklist is the reference for completing onboarding; its full internal task list is not repeated in this employee-facing SOP.

## Before Your First Day

Once the offer is accepted, HR will confirm the joining date, normal work location or joining instructions, reporting Department Head, and any documents needed before work begins.

New employees are expected to:

- provide their **National ID (NID)** and other necessary personal information where applicable; if an employee does not have an NID, speak with HR about lawful alternative identification;
- share relevant educational and professional certificates;
- provide previous employment documents where applicable and reasonably required;
- review and sign their **appointment letter or employment agreement** before joining;
- review and sign the applicable confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement (NDA); and
- complete the required **Blinto HRMS profile** information accurately.

Submit personal documents only through HR's approved secure channel. HR should explain why information is needed and correct inaccuracies; employees should not send sensitive documents into broadly accessible chats or project spaces.

Blinto will provide the appointment documentation and any legally required employee identity card and maintain applicable employment records in accordance with law. Employees should receive and understand the terms applying to their role, including compensation, job title, employment classification, working arrangements and probation conditions.

If required documents, equipment or access are not ready, HR will identify the outstanding items and explain the next steps. Missing company-provided resources should not be treated as employee misconduct.

## Your First Day

### HR orientation

HR introduces Blinto's mission, values, culture, basic workplace expectations and where to find the Employee Handbook and SOP Library. The orientation covers how to:

- understand office hours and attendance expectations;
- request leave and communicate an absence through HRMS;
- use HRMS for basic employee requests and records;
- find the relevant policies, contacts and support channels; and
- follow confidentiality, information-security and workplace-safety expectations.

The detailed rules remain in the linked **Office Hours**, **Attendance** and **Leave & Time-Off** policies; this SOP does not replace them.

### Team and role introduction

Your Department Head or designated lead introduces your teammates and reporting relationship and explains:

- the purpose of your role and its main responsibilities;
- job-specific goals and KPIs, including what successful work looks like;
- current priorities, expected quality and deadlines;
- communication and client-service standards; and
- where to ask questions or seek help.

A designated colleague or buddy may help you become familiar with the team and everyday work practices.

### Equipment and systems

Blinto will arrange the equipment and approved work access needed for the role, such as a laptop or other assigned devices, company email, HRMS, ClickUp, communication tools and department-specific systems.

Only use the access given for your responsibilities. Do not share passwords or move company or client data into personal accounts. Report missing, faulty or inappropriate access to your Department Head and HR promptly.

## During Your First Week

Your Department Head or assigned colleague will help you understand the tools and routines needed to begin work confidently. Depending on your role, introductory training may cover:

- the Employee Handbook and relevant company policies;
- confidentiality, security and responsible handling of company/client information;
- HRMS attendance and leave requests;
- ClickUp tasks, priorities, updates and workplace communication;
- client communication and professional conduct;
- department-specific systems and technical skills; and
- workplace safety and emergency arrangements.

You should receive initial work suited to your role and readiness, understand your immediate priorities and know whom to contact if you are blocked. Do not hesitate to ask for clarification rather than guessing about a critical client, security or compliance task.

## During Your First Month

The Department Head will review how the employee is settling into the role and discuss early work, training needs, priorities, tools and support. Feedback and coaching should continue as needed; employees should not have to wait for a formal probation assessment to hear about a concern or receive help.

This first-month conversation is an onboarding and development check-in, **not an automatic confirmation decision or a separate mandatory formal performance rating**. The approved [Performance Review](./performance-review.md) SOP governs formal assessments.

If job responsibilities, KPIs, systems access or training remain unclear, the employee should raise the issue with the Department Head or HR so it can be addressed.

## Probation and Performance Expectations

HR will explain the employee's applicable probation terms at joining. Blinto normally plans for a **three-month probation** for regular new employees where legally applicable; a different period or protection may apply based on the worker's legal classification, contract and current Bangladesh law.

During probation:

- employees should understand the KPIs, role expectations and standards against which their work will be reviewed;
- Department Heads provide appropriate guidance and timely performance feedback;
- a formal probation evaluation takes place before the applicable probation period ends where required by Blinto's review process; and
- HR communicates the documented outcome, subject to the separate **Probation, Evaluation & Confirmation Policy** and applicable legal rights.

A salary review may be considered after confirmation; **confirmation does not automatically guarantee an increase**. Any salary decision follows Blinto's separate compensation-approval arrangements.

An administrative delay in a review or letter does not itself extend a statutory probation period or take away an employment status acquired under applicable law.

## Employee Acknowledgments

Employees will be asked to acknowledge, through HRMS or the approved HR process, that they have received or been briefed on:

- the Employee Handbook and relevant company policies;
- attendance, office-hours and leave procedures;
- confidentiality and information-security obligations;
- any assigned company equipment; and
- their job description, reporting relationship and role expectations.

An acknowledgment confirms receipt or explanation. It does not waive an employee's legal rights or mean the employee agrees with a term that is contrary to applicable law. Employees may ask HR for clarification or copies of documents.

## Who Helps With Onboarding

**People Operations / HR** coordinates the overall experience, explains company policies and employment matters, helps resolve missing information, and confirms the onboarding process has been addressed.

**Department Head or designated lead** explains role expectations and KPIs, introduces work and teammates, provides feedback and makes sure the employee has practical support.

**HR/Admin** supports workplace arrangements and assigned equipment. **IT or the relevant system owner** provides approved technical access. **An assigned buddy or colleague**, when designated, helps with everyday questions and team routines.

## If Something Is Missing or Unclear

Tell your Department Head or HR promptly if:

- essential equipment or access is unavailable or not working;
- your role, performance targets or reporting line has not been explained;
- you cannot find the right policy or do not understand a requirement;
- required training or support is missing; or
- your employment documents contain an error.

HR and the relevant team should clarify the issue, identify a responsible contact and communicate any practical next step. A delay caused by company equipment or access should be considered fairly when assessing work progress.

## Completion of Onboarding

HR confirms onboarding completion **against the official Employee Onboarding Checklist in the SOP Library, as referenced in HRMS**. This includes checking that the employee has received the necessary orientation, understands the role and immediate expectations, has the access and resources reasonably needed to work, and knows the relevant people and policies. Any outstanding item must be recorded with a responsible owner and follow-up plan; it must not be marked complete without verification.

Completion of onboarding **does not** mean the employee has completed probation or received confirmation of employment.
