# Workplace Communication & Collaboration Policy

## Purpose

Help everyone at Blinto communicate clearly, respectfully and in time to support reliable work. This policy explains which channels to use, how to keep task information accurate, when to escalate concerns and how to collaborate without unnecessary interruptions.

Communication is more than sending a message. Important work decisions, assignments and changes must also be recorded where teammates can reliably find them.

## Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to confirmed and probationary employees, paid interns, Department Heads and team leaders, and freelancers or consultants collaborating on authorized Blinto work. It also applies when communicating with clients, vendors and other external partners in connection with company work.

Client-mandated communication channels or response commitments apply when they have been appropriately assigned and authorized.

## Our Communication Channels

Blinto uses the following channels for different purposes:

| Channel | Primary use |
| --- | --- |
| **Telegram** | Regular day-to-day team communication, quick questions, coordination and timely alerts |
| **ClickUp** | Assigned tasks, owners, priorities, progress, blockers, project decisions and relevant work records |
| **Google Workspace / Gmail** | Formal company and client email communications, where appropriate |
| **Google Meet or another approved meeting tool** | Scheduled online discussions, reviews and collaboration |
| **HRMS** | Attendance, leave and employee administration, within its supported functions |
| **Phone calls or an appropriate direct channel** | Genuinely urgent incidents requiring immediate attention |
| **Client-approved channels** | Client work when the channel is authorized and appropriate for the project |

**Telegram is Blinto's regular communication channel. ClickUp remains the main source of truth for tasks and project progress.** A message in Telegram does not replace an essential update in ClickUp.

The existence of a communication channel does not authorize transmitting secrets or restricted personal information there. The applicable security and privacy rules always apply.

## Availability and Response Expectations

During Blinto's normal office hours, employees should be reasonably reachable for work-related communication while balancing focused work, meetings and assigned duties.

- **Urgent operational messages:** Pay prompt attention when reasonably available. If the issue poses an immediate project, security, safety or client risk, the sender should use a direct approved channel or phone call rather than relying only on an unread chat message.
- **Routine work messages:** Normally respond within the same working day when reasonably practical.
- **When an answer will take time:** Acknowledge the message where useful and indicate when the sender can expect an update, rather than leaving a material request unexplained.
- **During approved breaks, leave or outside working hours:** Continuous message monitoring is not expected. Routine matters may wait until the next working day.
- **Client-specific commitments:** Follow an expressly assigned, legitimate client communication commitment, coordinating with the responsible lead if coverage is needed.

These are practical communication expectations, **not an invented minute-by-minute SLA or automated response-monitoring program**.

## Professional and Respectful Communication

Use clear, honest, respectful and constructive language in conversations, calls, comments, emails and meetings.

Team members should:

- communicate progress, mistakes, delays and concerns accurately;
- focus feedback on work, decisions and observable behavior rather than personal characteristics;
- avoid insulting, discriminatory, harassing, aggressive or threatening comments;
- respect colleagues' time and avoid unnecessary repeated messages, mentions and interruptions;
- assume good faith when a misunderstanding is possible, and seek clarification before escalating a disagreement; and
- use a more appropriate private or formal channel when the topic is sensitive.

Employees must not publicly embarrass a colleague over mistakes or performance. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation and serious misconduct are governed by the relevant conduct and grievance policies.

## Tasks, Ownership and Project Updates

**ClickUp is the default source of truth for assigned work** unless a project has an expressly approved alternative.

Employees should keep assigned task statuses and meaningful updates reasonably current. Important changes to deadlines, scope, owners, requirements, delivery status or project priorities should be captured in the relevant task or approved project record.

If work is discussed through Telegram, a call or a meeting, the responsible person must ensure any material decision or action is reflected in ClickUp or the appropriate approved project system.

A useful task record makes it clear **what needs to be done, who owns it, what changed and what happens next**. Avoid relying exclusively on verbal instructions or disappearing chat history when a decision affects the work of others.

Department Heads and project owners clarify priorities, responsibilities and cross-team dependencies. Team members should flag conflicts, missing information and project blockers early instead of waiting until a deadline is already missed.

## Cross-Department and Client Collaboration

When a task depends on another team, communicate the required input, owner and timing clearly. Involve the people needed to resolve the issue, rather than broadcasting unrelated details widely.

Employees must not make new client commitments outside their authority. Significant client requests, scope changes, deadlines and delivery risks should be brought to the responsible project owner and recorded in the appropriate work system.

Client communications should remain professional and accurate. Where a client requires a particular platform, approval workflow or response arrangement, follow that authorized requirement.

## Meetings and Daily Stand-Up

Blinto's daily stand-up is governed by the approved [Office Hours Policy](./office-hours-policy.md). Under that policy, the stand-up normally runs **9:00–9:15 AM**. This policy does not change its time or participation expectations.

For other required meetings, employees should join on time, come prepared and contribute appropriately. Organizers should keep meetings purposeful, include relevant participants and avoid unnecessary extensions.

Where a meeting results in a material decision, assignment, deadline or change, the responsible person should document it in ClickUp or another appropriate company record. Participants who cannot attend a required meeting should notify the organizer promptly when reasonably possible.

Detailed meeting and stand-up procedures, if separately adopted, belong in the relevant **Meeting & Standup Participation Policy**.

## Feedback, Disagreements and Grievances

Work-focused disagreement is normal. Employees should raise concerns respectfully, explain the issue with relevant examples and look for a practical resolution.

Where safe and appropriate, an employee may first discuss a routine work disagreement directly with the relevant colleague. Department Heads or project owners can help resolve task ownership, delivery and collaboration disputes.

Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, serious conduct concerns and sensitive employment issues should be raised with **HR through an appropriate private channel** and handled under the applicable workplace grievance process. Employees do not have to resolve serious concerns in a public Telegram group or through an informal team chat.

Good-faith concerns should be heard without retaliation.

## Escalating Urgent Issues

Do not wait for the next stand-up when a matter needs prompt action.

- **Project blockers, delivery risks or missed deadlines:** Tell the responsible Department Head or project owner as soon as the risk becomes clear.
- **Urgent operational incidents:** Contact the responsible person by phone or another approved direct channel when necessary.
- **Security events or possible data exposure:** Inform the responsible system owner promptly and follow the applicable security process.
- **Employee conduct, interpersonal or workplace concerns:** Contact HR through an appropriate private channel.
- **Serious client risks or commitments:** Inform the authorized project or client owner promptly.

After urgent containment or resolution, material decisions, work changes and follow-up actions should be documented in the appropriate approved company system.

## Confidentiality and Safe Communication

Use communication tools according to the type and sensitivity of the information.

**Do not send passwords, authentication codes, recovery secrets, API keys or login links through Telegram, email, ClickUp comments or ordinary chat.** The approved [Credential & Password Management Policy](./credential-and-password-management-policy.md) specifies the required secure methods.

Account invitations, access grants and removal must follow [Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning](./access-provisioning-and-deprovisioning.md), including the authoritative Access Register. A manager's chat message is not a substitute for the required access approval.

Do not place confidential employee records, sensitive performance-review information or restricted client data in broadly accessible chats or task comments. Use authorized, appropriately restricted systems for those records.

## Communication Outside Normal Working Hours

Blinto respects employees' time outside the normal working schedule.

Routine Telegram messages, emails and task comments may be handled on the next working day. Employees on approved leave are not normally required to monitor work channels.

After-hours contact should be reserved for genuine urgent business needs, including critical incidents or properly scheduled international client commitments. If additional work is required outside normal hours, applicable approval, recording and compensation requirements under the [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md) and employment law still apply.

The [Office Hours Policy](./office-hours-policy.md) governs the normal schedule; this policy does not create a general on-call obligation.

## Responsibilities

**All team members:** Use the appropriate channel, respond reasonably, communicate respectfully, keep assigned work current and report blockers or risks early.

**Department Heads and project owners:** Clarify ownership and priorities, promote constructive collaboration, coordinate cross-team work and escalate material risks.

**People Operations / HR:** Explain communication expectations during onboarding and handle workplace conduct or interpersonal concerns within its responsibilities.

**System and information owners:** Administer authorized communication access and respond to security issues under the existing access and credential SOPs.

## Current Practice and Improvements

**Confirmed current practice:** Blinto uses **Telegram for regular communication**, **ClickUp for project and task management**, and approved Google Workspace tools for relevant work. Existing SOPs already define office hours, daily stand-up practices, onboarding orientation and security/access management.

Blinto has identified several current challenges: delayed responses to important messages, late reporting of project blockers, work discussed in chat without corresponding ClickUp updates, undocumented decisions, inconsistent cross-department coordination, late or unprepared meetings, and occasionally unprofessional feedback.

**Improvements proposed by this draft:** More consistent same-day routine responses, timely escalation, documenting material work decisions in ClickUp, clearer cross-team ownership and constructive feedback expectations.

This policy does **not** claim Blinto has Slack as an official communication tool, a new automated monitoring platform, dedicated Telegram group structures, formal response SLAs or other systems not confirmed by management.

## Questions and Concerns

If an employee is unsure where to record a work decision, which channel to use, or who owns an issue, they should ask their Department Head or project owner. For concerns involving conduct, harassment or confidentiality, use the appropriate HR or security escalation route.
