# Resignation & Notice Period Policy

## Purpose

Explain how an employee can resign from Blinto, how their notice period and last working day are confirmed, what is expected during the notice period, and how final payments are handled.

## Scope

This policy applies to Blinto employees, including employees on probation and employees whose employment has been confirmed, subject to their actual legal employment classification, employment agreement, and applicable Bangladesh law. Internship and other special engagement terms are considered separately where appropriate.

## Core Principle

Employees may resign by giving the applicable notice. Blinto asks employees to communicate promptly, continue their responsibilities during the notice period, and follow the separate Employee Offboarding SOP. In return, employees should receive clear written information about their last working day, any legally supportable notice-pay obligations, and their final dues.

A resignation does not depend on a manager's discretionary acceptance where the employee has exercised a lawful right to resign. HR confirms the applicable dates and manages the exit process.

## Notice Period

Blinto's standard company resignation notice period is **30 calendar days for both probationary and confirmed employees**. This is the notice period Blinto ordinarily requires under its appointment letters or employment agreements.

Where a longer statutory resignation notice might otherwise apply, Blinto's intended policy is to **accept 30 calendar days as sufficient notice and waive the additional period**, to the extent this is legally permissible. This company concession does not restrict an employee's statutory rights and must be validated against the applicable law and agreement before implementation.

Notice is normally counted in calendar days. HR confirms the last working day in writing.


## How to Resign

1. Submit a resignation request through **Blinto HRMS**, including the intended resignation date and proposed last working day.
2. Inform your **Department Head** so that workload and client handover can be planned.
3. HR acknowledges the recorded resignation, checks the applicable notice requirement, and confirms the **last working day in writing**.
4. Follow the separate **Employee Offboarding SOP** for work handover and clearance; it does not alter your notice period.
5. Any change to the confirmed last working day requires a **written agreement** or another lawful basis, communicated by HR.

The notice period normally starts when HR receives the resignation through the designated written HRMS channel. If HRMS is unavailable, contact HR promptly so a verifiable written submission and receipt date can be recorded. An HR processing delay should not silently restart the notice period.


## Early Release, Unworked Notice & Notice Pay

### 1. Notice Requirement

Employees are required to give written resignation notice in accordance with their appointment letter or employment agreement and applicable company policy.

Blinto's standard notice period is **30 calendar days** for probationary and confirmed employees, subject to applicable statutory requirements. Blinto intends to accept **30 days as its required company notice period**, where such a waiver is legally effective.

### 2. Shortened Notice Period

An employee who wishes to leave before completing the required notice period must submit a written request to HR through HRMS.

Blinto may approve early release, waive all or part of the remaining notice period, or require the employee to fulfil the applicable notice obligation. HR will communicate any agreed early-release date and notice-pay waiver in writing. A request alone does not change the confirmed last working day.

### 3. Unworked Notice Period

Where an employee leaves before completing the required notice period:

- Salary is calculated up to the employee's effective separation date, subject to applicable wage-calculation rules and other earned or protected payments.
- Unworked days are not treated as paid working days unless paid leave or another applicable entitlement covers them.
- Blinto may separately assess **notice pay in lieu of the unserved notice**, only where legally enforceable and not waived.

Notice pay is distinct from salary not earned for time away. It is **not an additional disciplinary penalty**.

### 4. Notice-Pay Recovery

Any notice-pay claim must be calculated proportionately, using the unserved part of the **applicable 30-day company notice period** (or another lawful period where relevant), the legally applicable wage basis, and any documented agreement or waiver.

A notice-pay claim must appear as a **separate item** in the employee's final settlement statement. **It must not be deducted automatically from earned wages**: HR and Finance must first establish that the claim and any proposed payroll set-off are lawfully permitted.

### Legal Basis for Permitted Wage Deductions

For workers covered by the Bangladesh Labour Act, **section 125** limits deductions from wages to categories authorized by law. These include, subject to statutory conditions:

- **Lawfully imposed fines** under the applicable disciplinary provisions.
- **Unauthorized absence from duty**, within the limits set by section 126.
- **Directly attributable damage or loss** of goods entrusted to the employee, or money the employee is responsible for, resulting from negligence or default. A delayed or incomplete handover by itself does **not** establish this type of loss; section 127 requires appropriate inquiry and limits on recovery.
- **Recovery of advances or loans**, or adjustment of wage overpayments, subject to applicable rules.
- **Deductions ordered by a court or legally competent authority**.

These are examples from the statutory list, **not permission to impose an additional penalty for unserved notice**. A notice-pay obligation and permission to deduct it from earned wages are separate legal questions; neither this policy nor an employment agreement authorizes a deduction prohibited by law.


### Example — 20 days served out of a 30-day notice

Assume the applicable notice period is 30 calendar days, the employee serves 20 days, the remaining 10 days are neither worked nor otherwise payable, the monthly salary is ৳30,000, and the illustrative daily rate is ৳1,000.

| Particulars | Calculation | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Total 30-day salary | 30 × ৳1,000 | ৳30,000 |
| Salary for 20 payable days | 20 × ৳1,000 | ৳20,000 |
| Salary for 10 days not worked | 10 unpayable days | ৳0 payable |
| Separate potential notice-pay claim | 10 × ৳1,000 | ৳10,000 |
| **Illustrative final salary if lawful set-off is allowed** | ৳20,000 − ৳10,000 | **৳10,000** |

**Important:** The ৳10,000 notice-pay claim is *not* automatically a permitted wage deduction. If set-off is not legally permitted, the employee's ৳20,000 earned salary remains payable, while any independently enforceable notice-pay claim must be handled through a lawful separate process. Actual calculations depend on the wage basis, paid leave, statutory entitlements, approved waivers, and other final-settlement items.



## If an Employee Stops Attending

If an employee leaves before the agreed final working day or stops reporting to work, HR will first check the facts, attendance records, any medical or emergency circumstances, and messages received. The employee should have an appropriate opportunity to explain and correct inaccurate records.

A failure to attend may affect payment for time not worked where permitted by law. Any **separate notice-pay obligation** must be calculated and justified independently. Blinto will not automatically impose a second absence penalty, duplicate recovery, or an otherwise prohibited salary deduction.

Where prolonged absence triggers a specific statutory procedure, including the notice-and-response process under section 27(3A) for applicable workers, that procedure takes precedence over informal assumptions about abandonment or automatic resignation.

## Final Settlement

After employment ends, Blinto will calculate and pay all amounts legally and contractually due, including, where applicable:

- salary and other earned payments up to the final payable date;
- payment for eligible unused earned annual leave;
- statutory resignation compensation or gratuity, where applicable;
- approved but unpaid expense reimbursements; and
- other payable contractual or statutory benefits.

Lawfully supportable notice-pay obligations or other permissible adjustments will be shown separately rather than hidden in the salary figure. **HR** coordinates employment and attendance records and explains disputed items; **Finance** calculates and processes the settlement.

For covered workers, section 30 of the Bangladesh Labour Act generally requires payment of final dues within **30 working days after employment ceases**. Blinto will not use pending administrative clearance as a blanket reason to withhold legally due wages or benefits beyond the applicable deadline.

Resignation-compensation entitlements under section 27(4) were amended in 2026 and must be assessed according to completed service, actual coverage, and the current statutory text. A departing employee should ask HR for an itemized explanation if any amount is unclear.

## Roles and Decisions

**Employee:** Submits the resignation in HRMS, informs the Department Head, follows the applicable Attendance and Leave & Time-Off policies, and completes offboarding requirements under the separate SOP.

**Department Head:** Gives operational input on early release and coordinates the separate Employee Offboarding process; does not independently alter the last working day.

**People Operations / HR:** Acknowledges the resignation, confirms the applicable notice and last working date in writing, reviews early-release requests, coordinates offboarding, and handles employee questions or disputes. Leave follows the separate Leave & Time-Off Policy.

**Chief of Staff:** May review exceptional or disputed exit arrangements when escalation is needed.

**Finance:** Calculates and processes final dues and any legally authorized notice-pay recovery, supported by an itemized record.

The separate approval sequence for **involuntary termination** is governed by the Employee Offboarding and Corrective Action policies. It does not make ordinary employee resignation conditional on CEO approval.

## Questions, Exceptions & Employee Rights

Contact HR promptly if:

- your confirmed notice period differs from the contract or your understanding of the law;
- you need early release or have a genuine emergency;
- you disagree with an attendance record, proposed deduction, notice-pay calculation, or final-settlement amount; or
- a delay in handover or clearance is affecting your departure.

Employees may request a written calculation or explanation. HR will review the concern, involve Finance where necessary, and escalate unresolved material disputes to the Chief of Staff or appropriate authority. This policy does not limit any statutory complaint or remedy.
