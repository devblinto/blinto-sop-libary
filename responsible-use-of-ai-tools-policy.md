# Responsible Use of AI Tools Policy

## Purpose

Help Blinto team members use artificial intelligence (AI) tools productively and responsibly without exposing client or company information, introducing unreviewed errors, or misrepresenting completed work.

AI is a support tool. **The employee remains responsible for the accuracy, quality, safety, originality, and confidentiality of anything submitted or delivered.** This policy complements the [Acceptable Use of Company Technology Policy](./acceptable-use-of-company-technology-policy.md).

## Scope

This policy applies to Blinto employees, including probationary and confirmed employees, paid interns, and authorized freelancers or consultants using AI for Blinto or client work.

It covers generative AI assistants, coding editors, search and research tools, AI features within work applications, design tools, automation services, and similar services, whether accessed through a company-managed account or an explicitly permitted personal account.

A client's stricter requirements or applicable law take precedence.

## Which AI Tools Can Be Used?

Blinto may permit tools in the following categories:

- **ChatGPT, Claude, and Google Gemini** for suitable assistance;
- **GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and other AI-assisted coding tools**;
- **AI functions in Google Workspace**;
- **AI-assisted design tools**, including relevant Figma and Adobe features; and
- other tools assessed and approved for the intended use.

**Being listed here does not by itself make a vendor, account, plan, or feature approved.** Tools must be approved for the intended activity, information sensitivity, client, and account arrangement. A tool suitable for public brainstorming may not be suitable for confidential client source code or employee data.

If an employee is unsure whether a tool is permitted, they should ask their Department Head or the responsible system owner **before using it with work information**.

Blinto has identified uncertainty about approved tools as a current problem. A maintained approved-tool list is a **proposed control**, not an existing complete catalog.

## Appropriate Uses

Subject to the tool and data permissions above, AI may support:

- brainstorming, planning, drafting and improving work documents or emails;
- research, translation, language improvement and preliminary information gathering;
- coding, debugging, technical explanation and test suggestions;
- design ideas, visual concepts and creative exploration;
- task organization, project planning and approved analysis using permitted data; and
- automation of repetitive tasks **only after the necessary approval**.

Employees must still apply professional judgment and role-specific quality standards. Permission to use AI does not authorize sending client work or confidential data to an external service.

## What Information Can Be Entered?

### Generally permissible, with an approved tool

Publicly available material, general nonconfidential instructions, and appropriately approved nonsensitive internal content may be used when it does not breach a client contract or another restriction.

### Requires additional authorization or safeguards

**Client information, nonpublic project documents, private source code, client repositories, internal business data, and other confidential material** may be entered or connected only when the particular tool, account, data handling and client use are expressly authorized. Client restrictions apply even when a tool is otherwise approved by Blinto.

Sensitive information should be removed or anonymized where appropriate. **Anonymization does not automatically make a restricted client dataset safe or authorized for an AI service.**

### Prohibited without an applicable separately authorized secure arrangement

Never paste or upload company or client **passwords, recovery codes, API keys, tokens, authentication secrets, or other credentials** into an AI prompt or tool.

Do not enter employee medical information, payroll records, identity documents, private contact information, or other sensitive employee data into **unapproved AI tools**. Any proposed use of personal data in an approved system must still have a lawful purpose, necessary safeguards and authorization under the [Employee Data Privacy & Records Management Policy](./employee-data-privacy-and-records-management-policy.md).

Employees must not connect an AI application to unrestricted Google Drive, GitHub, email, HRMS, or client data merely because the software offers an integration. Access must follow the existing access-approval process.

## Reviewing AI-Generated Work

**Human review is required before AI-assisted work is relied on, shared or delivered.**

Depending on the task, employees must:

- check factual statements, dates, citations, calculations and claims against trustworthy sources;
- review and test AI-generated code for correctness, security, dependencies, licensing and compatibility before committing or deploying it;
- review AI-drafted client communication for accuracy, tone, confidentiality and approved commitments;
- check designs and other creative material for originality, rights, licenses and suitability for the client;
- verify that the output actually meets the client brief and Blinto's normal quality standards; and
- obtain any additional manager or specialist review required for high-impact work.

AI can invent facts, misinterpret instructions, generate insecure code, reproduce protected material or miss relevant context. An AI answer is **not itself evidence** that a claim is correct.

Employees must not submit unreviewed AI-generated code to production, send fabricated or misleading deliverables, or claim they personally verified work they have not checked.

## Client Projects and Disclosure

For every client project, employees must follow the client's applicable AI-use rules. Some clients may limit specific tools, prohibit uploading files or repositories, or require disclosure of AI assistance.

- Do not upload client files, private repositories, source code or confidential materials to an unauthorized service.
- Obtain any authorization required by the client agreement or the designated client/project owner.
- Review all AI-assisted client deliverables before release.
- Apply ordinary quality, security and acceptance standards to AI-generated code or other work.
- Disclose AI assistance where the contract or client requirements call for it.
- Do not present unverified AI output as completed professional analysis, tested code, or original commissioned content.

When instructions are unclear, the employee should ask the Department Head or authorized client/project owner rather than assume permission.

## Approval of New Tools and Spending

A Department Head may recommend an AI tool when it meets a real work need. The **authorized system owner** reviews relevant access and security considerations; **HR** reviews proposed handling of employee personal data where relevant.

For **company-wide AI subscriptions**, **the Chief of Staff provides final business approval**, with **Finance approval of the budget or payment**. This does not replace required security or client approvals. Other individual tool requests follow the applicable delegated purchasing and access-approval arrangements; this policy does not invent an additional company-wide purchase process.

Employees must not buy an AI subscription expecting reimbursement without prior approval. An approved tool or subscription must be granted and recorded under [Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning](./access-provisioning-and-deprovisioning.md).

## AI Accounts, Licenses and Work Outputs

Where Blinto provides an approved company-managed AI account, employees should use that account for authorized work.

An **approved personal AI account** may be used only for **nonsensitive work** within the express permissions given. Personal accounts are not a shortcut around client confidentiality, software licensing, data controls or account approval. Employees should not assume that consumer and organizational accounts offer the same privacy or training settings.

Do not share paid AI login credentials, seats, or subscriptions in violation of licensing or the credential policy. Company-funded subscriptions and related access remain company-controlled.

Important work outputs must be transferred to approved company storage, not left solely in a personal AI chat history. When employment, role or project access ends, AI-tool access is removed under the existing Offboarding and Access Provisioning processes.

## Actions That Are Not Allowed

The following are prohibited:

- exposing passwords, keys or other authentication secrets to AI services;
- disclosing confidential employee information without proper approval and legal safeguards;
- uploading private client repositories or restricted materials to unauthorized tools;
- using AI to bypass access controls, security rules or client contractual conditions;
- submitting unreviewed code to production or fabricated research, citations or deliverables;
- using AI to impersonate employees, clients or managers deceptively;
- infringing copyright or breaching software and content licenses;
- making final hiring, disciplinary, termination or other significant HR decisions **solely through AI**, without appropriate human review and applicable legal process; and
- using an AI agent or integration to take consequential external actions without the approvals required for the underlying action.

AI assistance cannot replace a required human sign-off, client approval, legal safeguard or ordinary Blinto authorization.

## Learning, Questions and Incident Reporting

HR introduces these principles during onboarding. Department Heads explain role-specific uses, restrictions and quality expectations. Employees are encouraged to learn how AI can make mistakes and to verify important output.

If an employee suspects that confidential material has been pasted into an unauthorized tool, an AI integration has exposed files, or credentials have been shared, they should **report it promptly to HR and/or the responsible system owner** through an appropriate private work channel. Do not conceal the incident or circulate the exposed content further.

Technical containment and permissions follow the existing security SOPs. A possible policy violation is reviewed fairly under the [Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy](./corrective-action-and-performance-improvement-policy.md). An employee should have an appropriate opportunity to explain the circumstances; serious misconduct allegations follow the applicable legal process.

## Responsibilities

**Employees, interns and authorized contractors:** Use approved tools for approved purposes, protect information, validate outputs, and report possible misuse or exposure.

**Department Heads:** Recommend appropriate tools, explain client and role limitations, and ensure quality review.

**Authorized system owners:** Assess AI-tool security and integrations and administer access under existing processes.

**People Operations / HR:** Provide policy orientation, review employee-data concerns, and handle employment-policy questions.

**Chief of Staff:** Give final business approval for company-wide AI subscriptions.

**Finance:** Approve relevant budgets or payments and apply ordinary financial controls.

## Legal and Policy Safeguards

This policy is intended to operate consistently with applicable Bangladesh law, including:

- the **Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 (Act No. 63 of 2026)**, concerning lawful handling of personal information and applicable data-subject protections;
- the **Cyber Protection Act, 2026 (Act No. 81 of 2026)**, as amended, concerning relevant cyber offences and unauthorized system activity; and
- the **Copyright Act, 2023 (Act No. 34 of 2023)** and applicable contractual or licensing rights, including use of protected text, images, designs, software and code.

Copyright ownership and protection of entirely AI-generated works can be legally uncertain. Do not promise a client exclusive rights or originality based solely on a tool's output without the appropriate review.

The applicable law, employment rights, and client or contractual obligations prevail over this policy. The policy does not assert that Blinto has adopted any particular monitoring system, AI vendor, automatic content scanner or employee surveillance capability.

## Implementation Notes

The following improvements are **proposed, not yet confirmed as implemented**:

- publish and maintain the approved AI tools and permitted-use arrangements;
- clarify whether approved personal accounts may be used for specific nonsensitive tasks;
- make authorization and reimbursement of AI subscriptions more consistent; and
- reinforce human review and training so employees do not rely uncritically on AI output.
