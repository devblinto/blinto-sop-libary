# Promotion, Career Progression & Salary Review Policy

## Purpose

Explain how Blinto considers career advancement, promotions, changes in responsibility, and salary reviews. We want employees to understand what evidence matters, how decisions are made, and when a change becomes official.

**Promotion, a discretionary salary adjustment, and a statutory wage increment are different matters.** An employee can advance professionally without changing job title, and a salary change may occur without promotion. Any statutory or contractual wage entitlement must be honored separately from discretionary decisions.

## Scope and Eligibility

This policy applies to **confirmed employees across all departments**, subject to the requirements of the proposed role and demonstrated capability. A confirmed employee may be considered for promotion or other career progression when there is an appropriate business case; being confirmed does not itself guarantee advancement.

Employees who are still on probation receive evaluation and confirmation treatment under the separate **Probation, Evaluation & Confirmation Policy**. A review at confirmation may identify future progression needs, but is not an automatic promotion or discretionary increment.

**Paid interns** may be considered for regular employment through the approved **Hiring** and **Internship Program** SOPs. That is an employment selection and conversion decision, not an automatic promotion.

## Forms of Career Progression

Depending on business need, employees may be considered for:

- a promotion to a higher position or level;
- movement into a team-lead or management role;
- a salary adjustment without a change in title;
- a lateral transfer to a different role or department;
- expanded responsibilities within the same role; or
- a newly established role justified by the company's organizational needs.

An expanded duty assignment or lateral transfer does not automatically mean that a formal promotion or salary increase has been approved. Material changes to employment terms will be communicated in writing before becoming effective.

## What Blinto Considers

Promotion recommendations should be assessed against the **career levels, salary ranges, performance measures and evaluation factors defined in Blinto’s Performance Tracker software and its governing SOP**, using the applicable version for the review period. These are the primary company references for annual review and career progression. Supporting considerations include:

- documented performance-review results and consistent work quality;
- demonstrated technical and professional capability;
- readiness to handle greater responsibility and complexity;
- leadership, teamwork, communication, and dependable conduct;
- appropriate client and project contributions;
- attendance and professional reliability, assessed fairly and with regard to lawful leave or accommodations;
- availability of a suitable role and Blinto's organizational structure; and
- business needs, sustainability, and budget.

The Performance Tracker’s applicable factors and measurement method guide the assessment; no single informal criterion automatically decides the result. Employees should be assessed against the duties and standards of the proposed career level, using fair, job-related evidence. Protected leave, lawful complaints, or other protected activity must not be used as improper reasons to deny advancement.

## When Reviews May Happen

Blinto may consider promotions or discretionary salary reviews:

- during the **December annual performance and salary-review cycle**;
- after a probation confirmation, as a separate decision rather than an automatic entitlement;
- when an employee has taken on materially greater responsibilities;
- after exceptional, sustained performance; or
- when restructuring or a business need creates a suitable role.

These are **opportunities to consider a decision**, not promises of a scheduled promotion or discretionary increase. **December is Blinto’s standard annual review period**, using the January–December calendar year. During **annual reviews**, Blinto will follow the performance-measurement process, career levels, salary ranges and evaluation factors already specified in its **Performance Tracker software and associated SOP**. This policy does not reproduce or change those specifications, and it does not establish promotion quotas or automatic promotion intervals.

Where employment law, valid service rules, an applicable wage order, or a binding contract requires an increment or other payment, Blinto must assess and pay that entitlement on its own legal basis rather than make it conditional on this discretionary review process.

## How a Promotion or Discretionary Salary Change Is Approved

The proposed approval sequence is:

**Department Head recommendation → HR review → Finance verification → Chief of Staff approval → CEO final authorization.**

This sequence includes both senior approvers selected for this draft. **It is a proposed approval arrangement requiring management confirmation before adoption.** No individual manager may promise or announce an unapproved title or salary change as final.

### Performance Tracker reference during review

Blinto uses **Performance Tracker software** to support its performance assessments. The **Performance Tracker and its governing SOP define the career levels, salary ranges applicable to those levels, and the factors used to measure performance and readiness for progression**.

During the annual performance-review cycle, the Department Head and HR must use the applicable Performance Tracker assessment and career-level criteria as the basis for recommendations. They should consider the employee’s documented results, current level, responsibilities, the requirements of any proposed level, and the corresponding salary range. The approved [Performance Review](./performance-review.md) SOP continues to govern the formal review process.

A Performance Tracker result informs a recommendation; it does **not** independently approve a promotion, alter salary, or override the required HR, Finance, Chief of Staff and CEO approvals or applicable legal wage obligations. Where a tool record, approved SOP, or salary-range version appears inconsistent, HR should resolve the discrepancy with the responsible owner before finalizing a decision.

### Department Head

The Department Head identifies the business need and recommends the proposed change, supported by performance evidence, the expected role responsibilities, and a suggested effective date.

### People Operations / HR

HR reviews eligibility, performance history, existing designation and terms, fairness and internal consistency, and whether a separate hiring, probation, conflict-of-interest or contractual process applies.

### Finance

Finance checks the proposed gross salary, affordability, payroll implications, and applicable statutory or contractual obligations. Finance verification is required before the senior business approvals and payroll implementation.

### Chief of Staff and CEO

The Chief of Staff evaluates the business rationale and provides an approval recommendation/authorization at the penultimate stage. **The CEO gives the final authorization** under this draft. If the company decides that approval authority should instead be delegated to one of these offices, the policy must be revised explicitly before that rule is used.

### Employee communication

HR confirms approved changes **in writing**, including the designation or reporting relationship where relevant, approved **gross salary** where revised, and effective date. Until that communication is issued, an informal discussion or recommendation is not a confirmed employment-term change.

## Salary Reviews and Payroll Implementation

A promotion **may or may not** involve a salary increase. A salary review may also produce an adjustment without a new title. The company considers the **applicable Performance Tracker career level and salary range**, measured performance, role scope, internal consistency, business needs and the available budget while honoring applicable minimum wages, required increments and contractual rights.

An approved change takes effect on the **written effective date**. Finance implements the change through payroll after receiving the required authorized record. HR updates the controlled employee record and, where the current HRMS supports it, the designation, reporting relationship and salary fields.

A proposed change is not retrospective unless its written authorization expressly specifies a lawful retroactive effective date. The employee's existing agreed pay remains payable until any lawful variation takes effect. Changes must not be used to avoid accrued wages or other statutory rights.

The **career-level salary ranges are defined in the Performance Tracker and its governing SOP**, not in this policy. This policy does not introduce new ranges, an across-the-board annual discretionary increment, or an automatic increase upon probation confirmation.

## Annual Salary Review and Confirmation-Related Salary Adjustments

### December annual review schedule

Blinto uses a **January–December calendar year**. Its **regular annual performance and salary review takes place in December each year**. December is the standard compensation-review period for eligible employees across departments.

The annual review uses the **Performance Tracker software and governing SOP**, including the documented performance measures, evaluation factors, career levels and corresponding salary ranges. Every employee eligible for the annual performance-review cycle should receive the applicable performance assessment even when a discretionary salary increase is not available to them that year.

A December review is an opportunity to assess performance, development, progression and compensation; it does **not** automatically approve a discretionary increment or promotion. Legally required increments and other wage entitlements remain separate.

### Probation completion and confirmation salary adjustment

When an employee successfully completes probation, Blinto conducts a **performance and compensation assessment** under the applicable probation/confirmation process. Management **may approve a salary adjustment** upon confirmation, based on that assessment, the relevant Performance Tracker criteria and salary range, and the normal written approval and payroll process.

A confirmation-related salary adjustment is a **separate compensation decision** from the regular December annual review. Successful confirmation **does not automatically guarantee** a discretionary salary increase.

### December increment eligibility after a confirmation adjustment

An employee who receives a **discretionary confirmation-related salary increase** in a calendar year still participates in the normal **December performance review**. Their performance, achievements, competencies, career-level readiness and development progress must still be evaluated using the Performance Tracker and its SOP.

However, the employee **will not ordinarily receive a second discretionary salary increment in that same calendar year's December cycle**, because their salary was adjusted upon confirmation. This normal rule does **not** prohibit an expressly approved exception or affect any statutory or contractual wage adjustment.

Employees who receive a confirmation-related discretionary salary increase during a calendar year will **ordinarily next be considered for a discretionary annual salary increment in December of the following calendar year**, subject to their assessment, applicable career-level salary range, business considerations and required approvals. Consideration is not a guarantee of an increase.

The timing rule above applies when a **confirmation-related salary increase was actually granted**; it does not automatically exclude an employee whose probation was confirmed **without** a discretionary salary adjustment from the December discretionary review.

### Exceptions and special adjustments

Management may approve an additional or out-of-cycle **discretionary** salary change in exceptional circumstances, including:

- materially increased responsibilities;
- promotion or an approved change of designation;
- exceptional performance or business contribution;
- a justified market salary correction; or
- correction of internal salary inequity.

These exceptions follow the established promotion and salary-change approval route, including the required written effective date and Finance verification. **Legal or contractual wage adjustments are obligations, not discretionary exceptions**, and must be processed when legally due even if they fall outside the December review cycle.

| Next ordinary discretionary increment consideration | December 2027 |

These examples assume an approved confirmation-related **discretionary** salary increase and the illustrated three-month probation timeline. Actual probation completion, employment classification, legal increment eligibility and contractual rights govern each case.

## Job Titles, Reporting Lines and New Positions

HR maintains the authoritative approved designation and reporting information in the appropriate controlled employment record. Department Heads may propose a new job title or role where the work and business structure justify it.

A formal promotion should reflect the applicable **Performance Tracker career-level requirements** and a meaningful difference in scope, capability or accountability rather than a title change without substance. Employees should receive an understandable explanation of their updated role and who they report to.

## Requesting a Career or Salary Discussion

Employees may discuss growth goals, readiness for new responsibilities, or a salary-review request with their Department Head. They may present examples of contributions, new skills, responsibilities, relevant feedback or changed role requirements.

The Department Head considers the evidence and, where justified, recommends a formal review to HR. HR then coordinates the applicable approval route. Employees should receive feedback on the decision and, where useful, development guidance about the capabilities or opportunities relevant to future progression.

An employee's request for a discussion does not itself create a right to a discretionary salary increase. It does not affect any independent right to an increment or benefit that is required by law or contract.

## Responsibilities

**Employees:** Discuss career goals constructively, provide accurate examples of work, and follow agreed development priorities.

**Department Heads:** Apply the Performance Tracker assessment and relevant career-level criteria, provide fair evidence-based recommendations, explain role requirements, and avoid unapproved promises.

**People Operations / HR:** Coordinate the December annual performance and salary review using the Performance Tracker and its SOP; review confirmation-adjustment history, career-level and salary-range consistency; assess mandatory wage entitlements separately; maintain confidential records; coordinate written notification; and address record errors or concerns.

**Finance:** Verify affordability and salary treatment, check legally required wage entitlements independently of discretionary review timing, and implement approved payroll changes.

**Chief of Staff:** Review and approve the proposed business case in the designated stage.

**CEO:** Provide final authorization under the proposed approval flow.
