# Office Meals & Refreshments Policy

## Purpose

Blinto provides an optional office meal program to make breakfast and lunch convenient and affordable for team members. This policy explains participation, contributions, meal timings, food hygiene and responsibilities, while respecting employees' freedom to choose their own meals.

## Scope and Eligibility

Confirmed employees, probationary employees, paid interns, Department Heads, management and office support staff may participate in the office meal program. Participation is voluntary and must be authorized by the individual before charges begin.

Employees who choose not to participate may make their own meal arrangements. Nonparticipation does not affect employment status, performance evaluation, attendance rights or access to ordinary workplace facilities.

## Monthly Meal Program

The program covers office breakfast and lunch. It does not establish an additional cash allowance, snack entitlement or entitlement to special event meals.

### Monthly Cost Sharing

The following rates are consistent with the latest Compensation & Salary Administration Policy, which establishes the program cost as of 9 November 2026.

| Monthly cost per participating employee | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Total estimated meal-program cost | BDT 3,000 |
| Employee contribution (one-third) | BDT 1,000 |
| Blinto contribution (two-thirds) | BDT 2,000 |

The employee contribution is collected through an appropriately authorized and lawful payroll deduction where permitted. The contribution does not reduce contractual gross salary; a lawful deduction affects net pay.

Employees who do not participate are not charged. Participation is a monthly meal-plan commitment, not a pay-per-meal arrangement or a free occasional-meal benefit. Nonparticipants must not use meals reserved for enrolled participants without a separately authorized payment arrangement. Any occasional-meal arrangement must be confirmed with HR in advance; this policy does not promise one.

## Joining or Leaving the Program

Employees should contact HR or office administration to join, change or leave the program. HR records the employee's election, effective date and applicable contribution authorization before meal arrangements and charges begin.

Employees should notify HR before monthly meal arrangements are finalized. Changes in participation normally take effect from the next calendar month. HR explains the applicable timing and confirms the effective date to the employee and office administration. New joiners may enroll during a month, subject to practical meal arrangements.

No employee is automatically enrolled. An employee may opt out for a future meal period. Participation changes must be reflected in the participant list and communicated to Finance before processing the relevant contribution. A disputed or unauthorized charge must be reviewed and corrected promptly.

## Partial Months, Absence and Unused Meals

For an approved mid-month enrollment or departure, the contribution is proportionate to the applicable participation period. HR confirms the period and the proration basis with the employee, and Finance verifies the amount before collection or reconciliation. Apply the same cost-sharing ratio to the applicable prorated program cost. Do not invent charges for periods before enrollment or after participation ends.

The precise proration method should be agreed by HR and Finance and applied consistently; it is not automatically the 30-day salary divisor used for applicable salary calculations. HR should explain the calculation before charging a partial-month contribution.

For a full month of enrollment, the agreed monthly contribution normally remains payable even when an employee misses individual meals because of occasional absence, approved leave, remote work or personal choice. Approved leave does not automatically create a refund. Any exceptional arrangement must be confirmed in advance where practical, rather than assumed from attendance records.

Employees do not receive cash for unused meals, unused company contributions or opting out. These rules do not prevent correction or refund of an erroneous or unauthorized charge.

When employment ends, HR confirms the participation end date and Finance reconciles the applicable contribution under the Final Settlement & Employee Dues Policy. No unverified meal charge may delay or unlawfully reduce earned wages or statutory final dues.

## Payroll Contributions and Changes to Costs

HR maintains the current authorized participant list, including effective dates, changes and agreed partial-month amounts. Finance checks the contribution against that record before processing payroll and identifies it separately in the employee's salary breakdown.

For covered workers, deductions must meet the requirements of sections 125 and 128 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended. Employee acceptance is required but is not sufficient by itself: HR and Finance must confirm the applicable legal basis, any required government authorization or conditions for employer-provided services, and the permissible amount. The deduction must not exceed the legally permitted value of the service or the employee's agreed contribution. Where payroll deduction is not lawful, arrange a lawful alternative payment method instead of deducting wages without authority.

No informal meal-related fine or unauthorized salary deduction is permitted. Employees may ask HR to explain or correct a contribution. Finance and HR resolve discrepancies under the Payroll Processing SOP.

The monthly cost may change with food ingredients, raw materials or other relevant meal expenses. Chief of Staff approval is required for changes to program costs and subsidies, subject to any applicable statutory canteen-management requirements. HR communicates the revised total cost, employee share, company share and effective date before implementation. Maintain the one-third employee and two-thirds company cost-sharing ratio unless a revised arrangement is expressly approved and communicated. Employees may choose not to continue under revised terms; obtain the appropriate updated authorization before collecting a revised contribution.

## Breakfast, Lunch and Working Hours

Meal arrangements follow the approved Office Hours and Attendance policies.

| Activity | Scheduled time |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | 8:30–8:45 AM |
| Work preparation | 8:45–9:00 AM |
| Daily standup | 9:00–9:15 AM |
| Lunch and prayer break | 1:00–2:15 PM |

Employees should normally finish breakfast during the scheduled breakfast period and prepare for standup before 9:00 AM. A required participant who misses breakfast must attend the 9:00 AM standup first. After standup ends at approximately 9:15 AM, the participant may eat the missed breakfast, coordinating with the Team Lead or Department Head so the delayed meal does not create an uncommunicated work absence.

Employees are responsible for prioritizing required meetings; leads reinforce this expectation, and HR supports consistent application. Genuine emergencies, medical needs and approved accommodations should be considered fairly.

Meal arrangements must not unnecessarily disrupt work or meetings. Participation in the program does not change the approved attendance threshold, two-day monthly automatic late allowance or existing HRMS attendance escalation. Employees who bring their own meals or do not enroll retain their applicable meal/rest breaks, including statutory breaks under section 101 of the Labour Act where applicable.

## Food Quality, Hygiene and Dietary Needs

Office administration coordinates hygienic food preparation, storage and service, and monitors food quality and cleanliness. Food should be handled safely, protected from contamination, and stored at suitable temperatures. Unsafe, spoiled or suspected contaminated food must not be served. Anyone identifying a food-safety concern should inform office administration promptly; administration should stop service of the affected food where necessary and arrange appropriate action.

Employees should wash their hands as appropriate, use designated dining areas, dispose of waste properly, return utensils to the designated location and leave shared spaces reasonably clean. Respect the personnel responsible for preparing and serving food. Follow applicable office cleanliness rules; professional cleaning and food preparation remain the responsibility of designated personnel.

Employees may inform office administration of relevant food allergies, religious dietary requirements or other dietary needs. Reasonable arrangements should be considered where practical, without promising a particular menu or allergen-free preparation environment. Administration should communicate any relevant limitations before service. Handle dietary or medical information discreetly and share only what is necessary for safe arrangements.

If someone becomes ill or experiences a suspected serious allergic reaction, follow the Workplace Health, Safety & Emergency Policy and obtain appropriate medical assistance. Report suspected food-related illness promptly so administration can assess the food-service risk.

Office administration must ensure that meal arrangements comply with applicable food-safety and hygiene obligations, including the Food Safety Act, 2013 and relevant regulations. Where external food services are used, ensure applicable food-safety and supplier requirements are met.

## Drinking Water and Dining Facilities

Adequate safe drinking water is available to all employees, regardless of meal-program enrollment. Drinking water must be accessible, hygienically maintained and protected from contamination, consistent with section 58 of the Labour Act where applicable.

Employees who bring their own food may use appropriate designated dining facilities. Dining spaces should be clean, adequately lit, ventilated and maintained at a comfortable temperature.

Office administration and HR must verify applicable establishment and workforce thresholds and ensure required dining rooms and canteens are provided and maintained under sections 92–93 of the Labour Act and applicable Labour Rules. Where the statutory canteen provisions apply, comply with the prescribed facilities, management arrangements, worker representation and food-price requirements. Management approval under this policy does not replace any legally required canteen committee process. These obligations are independent of voluntary meal-plan enrollment.

## Refreshments, Visitors and Special Meals

Tea and coffee are available according to existing office arrangements. This policy does not establish a new quantity, schedule or entitlement to refreshments.

Visitor refreshments and client or meeting refreshments may be arranged for legitimate business needs with appropriate authorization. Special meals or refreshments for company events require approval. These arrangements do not create a general entitlement to free occasional meals outside the monthly program.

Personal food purchases are not reimbursed unless separately authorized under the Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy. No fixed additional refreshment allowance is introduced.

## Administration and Responsibilities

**Employees and participants:** Confirm participation and changes promptly, authorize lawful contributions, follow meal timings, respect shared facilities and food-service personnel, and report relevant hygiene or dietary concerns.

**HR / People Operations:** Explain enrollment, confirm participation and contribution authorization, maintain the participant list and effective dates, coordinate changes with administration and Finance, and assist employees with disputed charges or participation questions.

**Office administration:** Coordinate meal quantities with verified participation, plan breakfast and lunch arrangements, consider dietary needs where practical, monitor hygiene and food quality, and respond to food-service or facility concerns. Coordinate expected quantities and known changes with HR to reduce waste or shortages without inventing an automated ordering system.

**Department Heads / Team Leads:** Reinforce meal and standup timings, coordinate team requirements and approved exceptions, and communicate known operational changes where relevant to meal planning.

**Finance:** Verify applicable program expenses, cost sharing, authorized employee contributions and reconciliations; process lawful deductions or alternative collections; maintain appropriate financial records and correct verified errors.

**Chief of Staff:** Approve changes to program costs and subsidies, oversee material exceptions requiring management decision, and ensure any applicable statutory consultation or approval requirements are respected.

HR, office administration and Finance should coordinate participation and meal quantities before the relevant monthly arrangements are finalized. Maintain only the necessary records in approved restricted locations; no new software, vendor or staffing structure is assumed.

## Questions and Concerns

Employees may raise enrollment, contribution or refund questions with HR and food quality, hygiene or service concerns with office administration. Department Heads may help coordinate scheduling concerns. Unresolved material issues should be referred to the Chief of Staff as appropriate.

Concerns must be handled respectfully. Good-faith reports of unsafe food, incorrect charges or other problems should not lead to retaliation. Repeated intentional misconduct, if any, is addressed under the applicable corrective-action procedure following fair review, not through informal fines.
