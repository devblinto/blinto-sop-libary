# Visitor & Workplace Access Policy

## Purpose

Welcome legitimate visitors to Blinto while protecting employees, visitors, company and client information, equipment, and the workplace. The aim is to make visits straightforward, respectful, safe, and appropriately authorized.

This policy governs **physical entry to and movement within the workplace**. Digital accounts, passwords, client-system permissions, and related technical access remain governed by the approved [Credential Policy](./credential-and-password-management-policy.md) and [Access Provisioning SOP](./access-provisioning-and-deprovisioning.md).

## Scope

This policy applies to everyone entering or using Blinto's workplace: confirmed and probationary employees, interns, Department Heads, management, clients, business partners, interview candidates, vendors, delivery personnel, maintenance providers, government or regulatory visitors, and employees' family members or personal guests. Where an outside party has separate legal access rights, those rights must be respected.

## Employee and Visitor Guidance

### 1. Visits must have a legitimate purpose

- Business clients and approved partners may visit for authorized meetings or work.
- HR coordinates job applicants and employment-related visitors.
- Vendors, delivery personnel, and maintenance providers may enter for an authorized service or delivery; access should be limited to what the task requires.
- Official government or regulatory visitors are referred promptly to HR, office administration, or authorized management. Do not obstruct lawful inspection or access; verify identity and scope where appropriate.
- Personal guests, including family members, require appropriate prior approval and must not interfere with normal work.
- Unscheduled visitors are assessed case by case. A visitor does not gain unrestricted access simply by arriving at the office.

### 2. Arrange and authorize a visit

The employee hosting a visit confirms its purpose and coordinates with the appropriate person before the visitor arrives where practical.

- **Client or business visit:** the responsible Department Head authorizes the relevant visit.
- **Job interview or employee-related visit:** HR coordinates the arrangement.
- **Vendor, delivery or maintenance visit:** the responsible office contact confirms the visit and any needed work-area access.
- **Personal visit:** the employee obtains appropriate prior approval through the responsible Department Head or office contact.
- **Exceptional, sensitive, or higher-risk visit:** refer to the Chief of Staff for approval or direction.

Where advance approval was not possible, contact the appropriate authority before allowing access to nonpublic areas. A host cannot approve access to an area or record outside their own authority.

### 3. Welcome visitors and confirm access

When a visitor arrives, the responsible employee or office contact should:

1. Confirm the visit with the host or authorizing person.
2. Direct the visitor to an appropriate waiting or meeting area.
3. Explain relevant safety, privacy, conduct, and access requirements in a friendly and understandable manner.
4. Tell the host that the visitor has arrived and coordinate the meeting or service.
5. Record arrival and departure **where an approved practical visitor-record process is in place or the circumstances make a record appropriate**.

Identification may be checked when reasonably necessary to establish identity or authorization. Do not routinely copy identity documents or collect personal details that are unnecessary for the visit. This policy does not claim Blinto already maintains a visitor register, reception desk, badge system, or security staff.

### 4. Protect work areas and confidential information

Visitors may use meeting rooms and approved common areas for their authorized purpose. They must not enter other nonpublic work areas without permission; accompany visitors when entering such areas.

Additional permission is required for areas containing restricted equipment, servers or network equipment, sensitive records, or work requiring special safeguards. In particular:

- Protect client work, computer screens, source code, files, and conversations from unintended viewing or hearing.
- Keep HR, payroll, employment, and personal information restricted.
- Do not allow a visitor to use company equipment, accounts, credentials, or confidential records without authorization.
- Do not share office keys or other physical entry items with unauthorized people.
- Do not allow unauthorized photographs, recordings, or disclosure of client or company work.

If a visit requires **digital** system access, follow the approved access and credential procedures; a physical visit does not imply digital authorization.

### 5. Employee office access

Employees must use only the entry arrangements they are authorized to use and must not let another person enter without appropriate authorization. Protect shared entrances and report lost keys or physical access items promptly to the responsible office contact.

After-hours workplace entry requires authorization through the responsible Department Head or other designated management authority. Office opening and closing duties belong to specifically authorized personnel; this policy does not assign those duties to every employee or establish new fixed visiting hours.

HR and the responsible office contact review physical access when an employee changes roles or leaves Blinto. Coordinate the timing of access removal with [Employee Offboarding](./employee-offboarding.md); do not independently change digital-account arrangements outside the relevant SOP.

Emergency exits, escape paths, and emergency-access arrangements must remain unobstructed and usable. No security practice should prevent safe evacuation.

### 6. Conduct and respectful visits

Visitors and hosts should treat people respectfully, keep noise and disruption reasonable, follow workplace hygiene and safety instructions, and respect meetings and working areas. Smoking and any other prohibited activities follow applicable workplace rules.

Visitors may not take company property or use company facilities beyond their authorization. Hosts help visitors understand the rules and promptly address concerns, while HR or office administration coordinates any wider response. Visitors' accessibility or reasonable assistance needs should be handled respectfully where practicable.

### 7. Report unauthorized access or safety concerns

Report an unfamiliar or unauthorized person, lost entry item, attempted unauthorized entry, security concern, or unsafe visitor behavior promptly to the office contact or Department Head. Contact HR when employee information or employee safety is implicated, and escalate significant access or security incidents to the Chief of Staff.

If there is an immediate threat, prioritize personal safety and contact emergency services as appropriate. Do not physically confront a threatening person unnecessarily. Keep evacuation routes clear and follow the [Workplace Health, Safety & Emergency Policy](./workplace-health-safety-and-emergency-policy.md).

Preserve relevant information when safe, and promptly inform the authorized person if company/client information may have been viewed, photographed, removed, or disclosed. Security incidents involving information should also follow the [Information Security & Confidentiality Policy](./information-security-and-confidentiality-policy.md).

### 8. Privacy of visitor information

Collect only the minimum visitor details needed for a legitimate purpose, such as coordinating the visit, identifying an authorized person, or investigating a genuine safety issue. Explain the purpose when information is requested. Restrict access and use approved protected records rather than open Telegram groups, public ClickUp tasks, or unrestricted documents.

Accurate records may be corrected on reasonable request. Retain and dispose of visitor information according to the applicable [Employee Data Privacy & Records Management Policy](./employee-data-privacy-and-records-management-policy.md), lawful retention obligations, and any approved recordkeeping arrangement. Do not introduce biometric collection, CCTV, or employee/visitor surveillance through this policy.

There is **no automatic requirement in this policy to establish a new visitor register**. HR and office administration may propose a simple, privacy-conscious record if management approves a practical process.

## Internal HR & Operations Procedure

### A. Before the visit

The host or responsible Department Head verifies the purpose, anticipated participants, timing, and any area restrictions. HR manages interview and employment-related arrangements; office administration or the responsible office contact coordinates deliveries, maintenance and visitor arrival as relevant. Refer exceptional or sensitive requests to the Chief of Staff.

For visits involving confidential client work, HR or the responsible Department Head confirms necessary protection and any client-specific visitor conditions. Do not assume a separate nondisclosure form is mandatory for every visitor; use applicable existing agreements and confidentiality rules.

### B. At arrival and during the visit

The available responsible employee or office contact confirms authorization, identifies the host, directs the visitor to an appropriate area, and provides relevant safety guidance. The host accompanies the visitor when entering nonpublic work areas and ensures confidential information is not exposed.

When an approved visitor-record process exists, record only the authorized necessary details and departure. If the company has not established such a process, do not create an improvised public log or claim registration is automated.

### C. At departure

The host or responsible contact confirms the visitor has departed the work area, that borrowed items or temporary permissions have been returned or withdrawn where applicable, and that the workspace and information remain protected. Escalate any missing items, unresolved access concerns, or suspected information exposure.

### D. Access issues, exceptions, and escalation

Department Heads resolve routine visit-authorization questions. HR handles employee-related, candidate, and personal-guest concerns. Office administration coordinates routine premises and maintenance concerns. The Chief of Staff directs exceptional or serious incidents and ensures the CEO is informed where the circumstances constitute a material safety or business risk.

For urgent threats, authorized personnel may immediately protect people and restrict physical access while formal reviews follow the applicable workplace and company procedures. An incident report alone does not establish employee misconduct; any corrective action must follow a fair review under the applicable policy.

### E. Records and periodic review

HR and office administration keep any necessary access or visit records restricted, correct known inaccuracies, and review whether the agreed process remains practical. Physical key or access permissions should be reviewed during relevant role changes and offboarding. Do not assume an electronic access-control system, centralized visitor database, security guard, or automated audit history exists.

## Operational Safeguards

- **Safety:** Keep occupied-workplace exit and escape routes unobstructed, consistent with the fire-safety provisions in section 62 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended, to the extent applicable to the premises.
- **Visitor privacy:** Handle visitor personal details for a lawful and appropriate purpose, use proportionate security, and follow the applicable provisions of the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026. No universal statutory requirement for every Blinto visitor to sign a register is asserted here.
- **Lawful visits:** Government or regulatory visitors exercising lawful powers must be handled respectfully and in accordance with applicable legal requirements; ordinary company approval rules cannot override lawful authority.
- **No new monitoring:** This document authorizes no CCTV, biometric screening, new tracking system, or surveillance arrangement.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Main responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Employees and hosts | Arrange appropriate authorization, guide visitors, protect information, and report concerns |
| Department Heads | Approve relevant business visits and resolve team-area access questions |
| HR / People Operations | Coordinate candidates, employee-related visits, privacy concerns, and applicable access reviews |
| Office administration / responsible office contact | Coordinate arrival, approved physical access arrangements, maintenance visits, and facility concerns |
| Authorized system administrators | Handle digital access only under the approved Access Provisioning and Credential procedures |
| Chief of Staff | Approve exceptional visits and oversee significant access/security concerns |
| Visitors and contractors | Respect approved access limits, privacy, conduct, and safety instructions |
