# Workplace Health, Safety & Emergency Policy

## Purpose

Help everyone at Blinto work in a safe, healthy office and know what to do if an emergency occurs. Safety comes before ordinary work, meetings, attendance procedures, or deadlines.

This document sets out employee-facing expectations. It does not replace building emergency instructions, professional medical advice, or requirements under Bangladesh law.

## Scope

This policy applies to all Blinto employees, including probationary employees; paid interns; freelancers and consultants while working on site; and others present at Blinto's office or shared facilities. It also applies, as relevant, during company-organized off-site activities and business travel.

People working elsewhere should follow the safety requirements of the place where they are working and alert their Blinto contact to serious work-related risks.

## Our Shared Safety Responsibilities

**Everyone:** Take reasonable care, use facilities safely, follow lawful safety instructions, report hazards promptly, and do not put yourself or others at unnecessary risk.

**HR/Admin:** Coordinate office safety concerns, help arrange assistance in an emergency, receive incident reports, retain necessary records, and work with building management on corrective measures.

**Department Heads:** Support safe working practices, notify HR/Admin about concerns, and help coordinate people and work during an emergency.

**Building management:** Is the point of coordination for building-wide fire, evacuation, structural, and shared-facility systems. This division of responsibilities does not remove any legal obligation Blinto itself may have.

Any employee may raise a good-faith concern about an unsafe condition without retaliation. No one should be required to remain in a situation posing an immediate and serious danger merely to follow ordinary work procedures.

## Everyday Workplace Safety

- Keep corridors, walkways, doors, stairways and exits clear.
- Report damaged plugs, exposed wires, faulty equipment, electrical smells, sparks, or overheating. Do not use unsafe equipment.
- Do not overload power outlets or tamper with electrical, fire or other safety equipment.
- Keep desks and shared workspaces clean and free of avoidable trip or spill hazards.
- Report water leaks, spills, loose fittings or other unsafe conditions to HR/Admin promptly.
- Follow relevant instructions from HR/Admin and building management.
- Do not smoke in restricted indoor areas.
- Use sensible desk and screen arrangements. Report discomfort or an unsuitable workstation so reasonable adjustments can be considered.

## Fire, Smoke and Building Evacuation

If you discover smoke or a possible fire, **raise the alarm immediately** and inform HR/Admin or building management if doing so is safe. Call **999** for emergency fire assistance when needed. Do not delay calling emergency services to obtain internal approval.

If evacuation is required:

1. Stop work and follow the safest available marked exit or instructions from emergency responders/building management.
2. **Use stairs, not lifts, during a fire evacuation.**
3. Do not return to collect personal belongings or equipment.
4. Move away from the danger and follow instructions at the safe location identified by building management or emergency responders.
5. Tell HR/Admin or the responsible lead if someone is missing, injured or may need assistance. Do not re-enter to search for them.
6. Do not return inside until the competent authority or building management confirms that it is safe.

Use a fire extinguisher only if you are trained, the fire is small, a safe exit is behind you, and doing so would not place you at risk. Otherwise, evacuate.

**Important:** A specific assembly point and the status of fire exits, alarms and equipment have not yet been confirmed for this policy. HR/Admin must verify these with building management and explain the actual arrangements to employees. This policy does not assert that a particular assembly area, drill program or appointed fire coordinator already exists.

## Earthquake Safety

During shaking, **Drop, Cover and Hold On** if a safe place of cover is available. Stay away from windows, glass and objects that could fall. Do not rush into stairways or towards exits while shaking continues.

After the shaking stops, check for immediate hazards. Evacuate by stairs when it is safe and when building management or emergency responders direct you to do so. Stay clear of visibly damaged areas and report to the designated safe location once confirmed. **Do not re-enter** until the building is declared safe by the appropriate authority.

If you are outside, move away from buildings, glass, walls, power lines and other falling-object hazards where possible.

## Medical Emergencies, Injury and First Aid

If someone is seriously ill or injured, seek urgent assistance immediately. Call **999** for emergency services when needed; employees may seek urgent medical treatment without waiting for routine internal approval.

HR/Admin coordinates practical assistance, access to available first-aid supplies, and communication with a designated emergency contact when appropriate. Do not move a seriously injured person unless necessary to avoid immediate danger or instructed by trained responders. Only give first aid that you are competent to provide.

Employees who become unwell at work may tell HR/Admin or their Department Head and seek appropriate care, rest or safe transport as circumstances require. Applicable leave and attendance matters are handled under their own policies after the urgent health concern is addressed.

**First-aid responsibility is currently unclear.** HR/Admin must identify the appropriate trained first-aid person or persons, confirm access to first-aid supplies, and communicate how to obtain help. The existence of a first-aid box must not be taken to mean a trained responder is already assigned.

Workplace injuries and serious health incidents should be reported to HR/Admin as soon as practicable, without delaying urgent care.

## Electrical Problems, Severe Weather and Security Threats

**Electrical hazards:** Stop using affected equipment, keep others away and report the problem. Do not attempt repairs unless specifically qualified and authorized. If smoke or fire develops, follow the fire procedure.

**Flooding, storms or severe weather:** Follow official warnings and building-management instructions. Avoid floodwater and unsafe electrical areas. HR/Admin and Department Heads coordinate necessary workplace or travel changes. Do not travel into an immediate danger solely to meet normal attendance expectations.

**Security threats or violence:** Move to safety, seek emergency help through **999** when needed, and notify HR/Admin or an uninvolved senior leader. Do not confront a dangerous individual or interfere with emergency responders. Any complaint of threatening, discriminatory or harassing conduct may also be handled through the applicable workplace conduct and grievance process.

## Reporting Hazards, Accidents and Near Misses

Tell **HR/Admin immediately** about an urgent hazard, accident or near miss. Notify the Department Head where relevant. If HR/Admin is involved in the issue or unavailable, contact an uninvolved Department Head or senior leader. In an emergency, call **999 first** when appropriate.

HR/Admin will record significant incidents and coordinate reasonable follow-up with relevant people and building management. Serious incidents must be escalated to senior management, and any required legal notifications must be made by the responsible authorized person.

The person who reports an issue should receive an appropriate update when a significant danger is addressed, taking account of confidentiality and the needs of other people involved. Good-faith reports must not lead to retaliation.

A formal incident-reporting channel and routine are **not yet established**. Until confirmed, direct reporting to HR/Admin is the interim method. Employees should not wait for a form or an HRMS feature to report a safety concern.

## Healthy Office Facilities and Meals

Blinto aims to provide safe drinking water, clean washrooms and handwashing facilities, appropriate lighting and ventilation, safe workstations, proper waste disposal, and reasonable support when a person becomes unwell. Shared areas and accessible routes should be kept usable and safe as far as practicable.

Breakfast and lunch arrangements should follow appropriate food hygiene, clean storage, handling and serving practices. Meal **enrollment, cost sharing, and salary deductions** belong in the separately drafted [Compensation & Salary Administration Policy](./compensation-and-salary-administration-policy.md), not this safety policy.

Employees should promptly report any issue with water, washrooms, ventilation, food quality or another health-related facility to HR/Admin.

## Orientation and Emergency Readiness

During [Employee Onboarding](./employee-onboarding.md), HR/Admin should explain how to raise safety concerns, how to obtain first aid, emergency number **999**, and the actual building exits and evacuation instructions once verified. The existing Employee Onboarding Checklist remains the separate completion record; no duplicate checklist is included here.

Blinto currently has **no regular fire-drill program**, and the first-aid ownership and incident-reporting arrangements require clarification. HR/Admin should work with building management and leadership to establish and communicate appropriate arrangements, confirm applicable statutory obligations (including fire-drill frequency where the legal headcount threshold is met), and arrange suitable employee orientation.

No assembly point, emergency coordinator, internal emergency phone number or completed safety inspection is represented as already established in this document.

## Law and Exceptions

Blinto will apply the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended through 2026, applicable Labour Rules, fire/building requirements and directions from competent authorities wherever applicable. These requirements take priority if any part of this policy is less protective.

Applicable provisions address cleanliness, ventilation, lighting, drinking water, sanitation, fire precautions and access to first aid. The specific legal requirements depend on the nature and size of the establishment and premises. HR/Admin and management must verify their applicability rather than assume that all thresholds have been met or are inapplicable.
