# Compensation & Salary Administration Policy

## Purpose

Explain how Blinto pays employees, calculates regular and partial-month salary, handles lawful deductions, communicates salary changes, and resolves payment questions. Employees should be able to understand what they are paid and whom to contact when a payment appears incorrect.

Rules for overtime, leave entitlements, resignation notice, and final settlement are covered by their separate policies.

## Scope

This policy applies to Blinto employees receiving a regular salary, including employees on probation, subject to their employment terms and applicable Bangladesh law. Intern compensation and independent-contractor fees follow the relevant agreement and program rules; statutory rights still depend on the actual working relationship rather than its label.

## Your Salary and Gross Pay

Your appointment letter states your agreed **gross monthly salary**. Blinto currently communicates the regular salary as a single gross amount, rather than a standard company-wide breakdown into basic pay and separate allowances.

- **Gross monthly salary** is your regular agreed monthly salary before applicable deductions.
- **Net salary** is the amount paid to your bank account after lawful and properly calculated deductions.
- **Basic salary** is a separate wage component where defined by an applicable contract, approved compensation structure, or law. Employees should not assume that gross salary and basic salary always mean the same thing.
- Bonuses, incentives, reimbursements, and overtime are separate from ordinary gross monthly salary unless the applicable agreement or law expressly requires otherwise.

If a statutory benefit or payment requires a specific wage-component calculation, Finance will apply the legally required basis. The absence of a component breakdown in an appointment letter does not remove statutory entitlements.

## Salary Payment

Blinto's regular salary payment target is **by the end of the current calendar month**. Salaries are paid by **bank transfer** to the employee's registered account. Employees should notify HR promptly of changes to their bank details.

If a payment delay is anticipated, Blinto should inform affected employees as early as reasonably possible, explain the situation, and arrange payment promptly. In all cases, payment must meet the applicable legal deadline. For workers covered by section 123 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, ordinary wages must generally be paid within seven working days after the relevant wage period ends.

## How Monthly Salary Is Calculated

### Full-Month Salary

An employee entitled to a full month's regular pay receives the **contracted gross monthly salary**, subject to authorized deductions. A 28-, 29-, or 31-day month does not change the agreed full-month amount.

Approved paid leave is treated as paid time according to the [Leave & Time-Off Policy](./leave-and-time-off-policy.md) and does not, by itself, reduce ordinary salary.

### Partial-Month Salary

Blinto's proposed standard daily calculation for an employee who joins or leaves partway through a month, or another qualifying partial-month situation, is:

**Daily salary = gross monthly salary ÷ 30**

**Partial-month salary = daily salary × applicable payable days**

For example, if an employee's gross monthly salary is **৳30,000**, the standard daily rate is **৳1,000**. If the applicable calculation recognizes **20 payable days**, the illustrative gross partial-month salary is **৳20,000** before any lawful deductions or other payable items.

This is a **company calculation convention**, not permission to pay less than a legally due amount. HR and Finance must verify how payable days are counted, avoid paying more than the contracted full-month amount solely because of month length, and apply any overriding statutory wage method. An employee may ask HR to explain the calculation used.

### Attendance, Leave and Payable Days

HR verifies attendance, approved paid or unpaid leave, and any relevant record corrections. Finance uses the verified records to calculate payroll.

- Approved paid leave must be handled according to its entitlement.
- Verified unpaid leave or legally recognized unpaid absence may affect payable salary.
- A missing punch, disputed attendance record, or pending leave correction is not automatically proof of unauthorized absence; employees may request a review.
- Exceptional salary adjustments must be checked by Finance and supported by the applicable approval.

For leave balances, request procedures, and absence rules, see the [Leave & Time-Off Policy](./leave-and-time-off-policy.md) and [Attendance Policy](./attendance-policy.md).

## Lawful Salary Deductions

Blinto will not treat salary deductions as an informal disciplinary tool. Deductions must have a valid legal basis, be calculated correctly, and be explained to the employee.

Depending on the employee's circumstances and applicable law, permitted items may include:

- recovery of an authorized employee loan or salary advance, subject to lawful terms;
- deductions for verified unpaid leave or qualifying unauthorized absence;
- lawfully imposed fines, where the required process and restrictions are satisfied;
- recovery of qualifying actual damage or loss, only with the evidence, investigation, and legal conditions required;
- legally required taxes or other statutory deductions, if applicable; and
- other amounts specifically permitted by applicable law or a valid order of a competent authority.

**Notice pay:** A potential claim for unserved resignation notice must be distinguished from an ordinary absence deduction. Any claim must be legally enforceable, individually verified, and shown separately. Blinto must not automatically set it off against earned salary without a lawful deduction basis. See the separate **Resignation & Notice Period Policy** and **Final Settlement & Employee Dues Policy**.

Employees may ask HR for a written explanation of any deduction and may dispute an inaccurate record. The optional meal-program contribution, where lawfully payable through payroll, is explained under **Optional Employee Meal Program** below. For workers covered by the Bangladesh Labour Act, sections 125–127 establish restrictions and safeguards concerning wage deductions.

## Optional Employee Meal Program

Blinto offers an **optional employee meal program covering breakfast and lunch**. Employees may choose whether to participate. The program is not a mandatory employment benefit or a mandatory salary deduction.

### Cost Sharing — As of 9 November 2026

| Monthly meal-program cost per participating employee | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Total estimated program cost | **৳3,000** |
| Employee contribution — one-third (1/3) | **৳1,000** |
| Blinto contribution — two-thirds (2/3) | **৳2,000** |

The employee contribution is intended to be paid from the employee's monthly gross salary **only through a lawful, appropriately authorized payroll deduction**. Where a payroll deduction is not legally available, HR and Finance must arrange a lawful alternative payment method rather than deducting salary without authority. Participation does not reduce the employee's contractual gross salary; an authorized meal contribution affects the net amount received.

### Choosing to Participate

- **Participation is voluntary.** Employees who do not join the program are not charged the meal-plan contribution and should not take meals provided exclusively under the enrolled plan without an agreed payment arrangement.
- **Enrollment is a monthly meal-plan commitment, not a free occasional-meal option.** An employee who joins for a month is expected to pay the applicable agreed contribution even if they choose to eat on only two or three days, unless HR approves a different arrangement in advance.
- Employees **cannot eat meals under the program without paying the applicable contribution**, whether regularly or occasionally. Someone who does not want monthly enrollment should not use the program unless HR confirms a separately priced occasional-meal arrangement in advance; no such occasional-meal arrangement is promised by this policy.
- Employees should ask HR how to enroll or stop participating. Changes to enrollment or charges should be confirmed **before the relevant monthly meal period** wherever possible. HR should explain any applicable cutoff or special circumstances, such as joining or leaving mid-month, before collecting payment. No automatic partial-month refund or prorated rate is promised unless expressly agreed.

### Changes to Meal Costs

The **৳3,000 monthly total is the rate as of 9 November 2026**, not a permanently fixed amount. Blinto may revise the meal-program cost to reflect changes in food ingredients, raw materials, or other relevant meal expenses. Blinto will inform participating employees of the revised total cost, their one-third contribution, Blinto's two-thirds contribution, and the effective date **before implementing a change**. Employees may choose not to continue for a future period under the revised terms.

### Salary Deductions and Questions

HR and Finance must verify the employee's documented election and the legally applicable basis before deducting meal contributions from wages. **Consent alone does not necessarily make a deduction lawful**: sections 125 and 128 of the Bangladesh Labour Act regulate deductions for employer-provided services and facilities. A qualifying deduction must also comply with any applicable government-approval conditions and cannot exceed the permissible value of the benefit. Meal charges must not be presented as a fine for declining to participate.

An authorized employee meal contribution should appear separately on the payslip or salary statement. Employees may contact HR to ask about enrollment, the cost, disputed charges, or discontinuation.

## Payslips and Salary Information

Blinto aims to provide every employee with a **clear salary statement or payslip for each wage period**, showing, as applicable:

- the regular gross salary and any separately payable items;
- overtime and bonuses, if payable;
- lawful deductions and adjustments; and
- the net amount transferred.

This is an improvement to the current employee experience, as payslips have not always been sufficiently clear. The payslip commitment is **proposed for formal adoption**; applicable statutory wage-slip requirements must be met in any event.

Employees may view and request clarification of their own compensation details. HR, Finance, and managers must protect other employees' confidential payroll information and may disclose it only where authorized and appropriate. This does not prohibit employees from discussing their own compensation voluntarily or exercising lawful rights.

## Salary Reviews and Revisions

Salary reviews may take place:

- after probation confirmation;
- during an annual performance-based review; or
- following an approved promotion or material role change.

Decisions depend on the applicable performance assessment, employment terms, company circumstances, and authorized compensation decision. HR communicates an approved change **in writing**, including its effective date; Finance implements the approved payroll change.

The separate [Performance Review](./performance-review.md) process governs how performance is assessed. This policy does not create an automatic probation increment, annual raise, or new approval authority.

## Bonuses, Festival Benefits and Overtime

Festival bonuses are governed by a **separate applicable policy, agreement, and statutory eligibility rules**. Eligible bonuses will be identified separately from ordinary monthly salary.

Overtime compensation, where due, follows the [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md), applicable employment terms, and the law. A payroll delay or a missing internal overtime approval must not be used to disregard a payment that is legally owed.

## If Your Salary Appears Incorrect

1. **Contact HR** with the month, issue, and any relevant records.
2. **HR checks** attendance, leave, employment terms, and corrections where relevant.
3. **Finance checks** the payroll calculation and applicable adjustments.
4. Blinto **communicates the outcome**, explains any correction, and arranges payment of any underpaid amount through an appropriate payroll correction within applicable legal deadlines.

Employees should raise salary concerns in good faith without fear of retaliation. If a concern remains unresolved, ask HR for a further review through an appropriate leadership channel. Employees retain any applicable statutory wage-claim rights.

## Responsibilities

**Employees** should maintain accurate personal and bank information, use the proper attendance and leave processes, and report payment concerns when discovered.

**People Operations / HR** explains employment terms, verifies relevant attendance and leave records, communicates approved salary changes, and helps employees understand payroll outcomes.

**Finance** calculates and disburses salary, checks legally applicable deductions and wage calculations, and corrects verified payroll errors.

**Department Heads** confirm relevant role or attendance facts where requested; they do not independently promise salary changes or disclose another employee's pay without authorization.

## Exceptions and Legal Protections

Applicable labour law, statutory minimums, and stronger enforceable employment terms take precedence where they provide greater protection. Blinto will not use this policy to remove a legally required wage component, minimum wage, payslip, timely payment, overtime payment, or other entitlement.

Unclear classifications, wage-component definitions, deductions, or disputed calculations should be referred to HR and Finance for verification before payroll is finalized.
