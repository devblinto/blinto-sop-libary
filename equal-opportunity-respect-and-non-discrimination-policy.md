# Equal Opportunity, Respect & Non-Discrimination Policy

## Purpose

Blinto wants people to have a fair opportunity to apply for work, contribute, develop and progress. This policy explains the standards for non-discrimination, equal treatment, respectful conduct, fair employment decisions and raising concerns.

It focuses on **prevention and fair decision-making**. Reporting, inquiry and grievance resolution remain governed by the separate Workplace Harassment, Discrimination & Grievance Policy and any legally required complaint procedure.

## Who Is Covered

This policy applies to confirmed and probationary employees, paid interns, applicants and job candidates, Department Heads, managers, freelancers and consultants working with Blinto. Clients, visitors and external collaborators must treat Blinto people respectfully when interacting with them for work.

The policy is a company-wide standard. Specific statutory employment rights depend on the applicable law, the person's legal worker status and the relevant employment or engagement arrangements. Extending a Blinto standard to a consultant or applicant does not itself convert the person into an employee.

## Non-Discrimination Standard

Blinto does not accept unfair treatment in work-related decisions because of a person's:

- gender or sex;
- religion or belief;
- race, ethnicity, nationality or national origin;
- disability;
- age, where protected or relevant under applicable law;
- pregnancy, maternity or related protected needs;
- marital or family status;
- political opinion or affiliation to the extent protected by law; or
- any other characteristic protected under applicable Bangladesh law.

These are both **legal protections where applicable** and **Blinto's proposed broader standards of respectful, fair treatment**. In particular, Blinto should avoid direct discrimination (less favorable treatment because of a protected characteristic) and indirect discrimination (an apparently neutral requirement that unjustifiably disadvantages a protected group).

Job requirements may differ where the distinction is lawful, genuinely relevant to the work, objectively supported and not a pretext for discrimination.

## Equal Opportunity in Recruitment

Hiring must follow Blinto's approved [Hiring SOP](./hiring.md). Candidate assessments should be based on job requirements, capability, relevant experience, demonstrated skills and consistent, role-related evidence.

Interviewers and decision-makers should:

- use relevant assessment criteria and avoid relying solely on personal impressions;
- avoid discriminatory or unnecessarily intrusive questions;
- allow reasonable interview-accessibility arrangements when needed and feasible, without reducing statutory rights;
- consider qualified candidates fairly and document relevant decision evidence in the authorized recruiting or HR record; and
- follow the existing hiring recommendation, HR review and final-offer approval chain.

Meeting minimum requirements does not guarantee an offer. Any selection decision must be made fairly on legitimate, job-related grounds.

## Fair Treatment During Employment

Blinto expects fair, job-relevant decisions about work assignments, performance, training, promotion, compensation, benefits and corrective action.

**Performance:** Assess employees against known role expectations and the approved [Performance Review SOP](./performance-review.md), not stereotypes or assumptions. Blinto's **Performance Tracker software and its governing SOP** specify performance measures, career levels, related salary ranges and assessment factors for annual review and progression decisions. Those are the authoritative criteria, subject to applicable law.

**Career progression and compensation:** Follow the applicable Promotion, Career Progression & Salary Review Policy and approved salary ranges, alongside lawful role, capability, performance, responsibility and business considerations. A performance result does not bypass required approval or mandatory wage protections.

**Development:** Make role-relevant training opportunities available according to legitimate skills needs, departmental requirements and approved budget, under the separate Employee Training & Development draft. Do not exclude an otherwise eligible person for a discriminatory reason.

**Assignments and discipline:** Assign work and apply corrective processes consistently, considering relevant role needs, facts, reasonable exceptions and applicable employment protections. Protected leave or reasonable accommodation must not be mislabeled as misconduct.

**Leave and benefits:** Administer approved leave and all applicable statutory benefits without discrimination. Pregnancy and maternity rights, and other legally protected benefits, must be respected.

## Equal Pay and Salary Decisions

Blinto must comply with equal-pay and minimum-wage requirements applicable under Bangladesh law. Under **section 345 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended in 2026**, covered workers performing work of the same nature, standard or value must receive equal-pay treatment as provided by that section, including its express references to male, female and disabled workers.

Blinto may account for documented lawful factors such as different responsibilities, verified skills, relevant experience, performance and applicable approved career levels and salary ranges. Those factors must be genuinely relevant and consistently applied, not a disguise for unequal treatment.

Salary records are confidential employment information. An employee who believes their designation, salary record or pay decision is inaccurate or discriminatory may contact HR through an appropriate private channel. No discretionary policy may override mandatory wage or benefit rights.

## Respectful Workplace Behavior

Everyone must communicate and work respectfully, regardless of background. Derogatory jokes, degrading stereotypes, discriminatory remarks, deliberate exclusion, bullying, harassment, intimidation and retaliation are unacceptable.

Respectful disagreement about ideas, professional feedback and lawful, evidence-based performance management are permitted. The issue is **unfair or disrespectful treatment**, not the mere existence of disagreement.

Managers and team leaders must respond appropriately when they witness or receive a concern. They must not dismiss discriminatory comments as harmless joking or require an employee to resolve a serious concern in a public group chat.

## Disability, Pregnancy and Other Individual Needs

Employees and candidates may discuss reasonable arrangements relevant to disability, pregnancy or maternity, or a temporary medical limitation, with HR. HR and the Department Head should consider the person's circumstances, the actual duties of the role, safety, operational feasibility and applicable legal duties.

Appropriate arrangements might include an accessible interview method, an adjusted workspace arrangement, a reasonable temporary task modification or another practicable measure. These are **examples to consider**, not a claim that Blinto already operates an accessibility program or has all possible accommodations in place.

Statutory maternity protections and disability rights cannot be replaced by a discretionary feasibility assessment. Requests should be reviewed individually, reasonably and without retaliation. HR should keep any medical or sensitive personal details restricted to those who genuinely need them.

## Raising a Concern

An employee, intern, applicant or authorized collaborator who experiences or witnesses potentially discriminatory or unfair work-related treatment may:

- report the concern privately to **HR**;
- inform the **Department Head**, if appropriate and not involved; or
- contact the **Chief of Staff** if HR or the Department Head is involved or there is a reasonable conflict of interest.

The **Workplace Harassment, Discrimination & Grievance Policy** governs formal reporting, investigation and resolution, together with any mandatory statutory complaint-handling framework. Do not use ordinary public task comments or team chats for sensitive allegations.

Blinto should handle reports discreetly, with access limited to the people who need the information. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when a fair investigation, required disclosure or lawful action is necessary. The affected person should receive appropriate information about the process and outcome, consistent with privacy and legal requirements.

No person should suffer retaliation for a good-faith complaint, participation in a lawful inquiry or request for an appropriate accommodation. Knowingly false allegations and proven misconduct are dealt with fairly under applicable procedures, not assumed merely because a complaint is unsubstantiated.

## Accountability and Corrective Action

All team members are responsible for following this standard. Department Heads must apply job-relevant criteria and address concerns in their teams. HR coordinates policy orientation, fair employment practices, record privacy and the appropriate complaint pathway.

Where misconduct is substantiated, corrective action follows the approved [Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy](./corrective-action-and-performance-improvement-policy.md) and the applicable investigation and legal procedures. Serious matters may require formal inquiry rather than informal coaching.

This policy creates **no automatic fine, salary deduction or financial penalty** for an allegation or violation.

## Statutory Protections and Bangladesh Legal Context

This policy must be applied consistently with the **Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended through 2026**, and relevant Labour Rules and other applicable laws. Important distinctions include:

- **Sections 345, 345A and 345B:** Equal-pay protection, prohibition of discrimination against covered workers, and protection against direct and indirect discrimination were expressly addressed or updated by the **Bangladesh Labour (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act No. 43 of 2026)**. Section 345A includes enumerated grounds and certain lawful job-qualification distinctions. Section 345B also governs how the employer must justify relevant treatment or requirements in covered matters.
- **Maternity protections:** The Labour Act's maternity chapter, as amended, governs relevant statutory entitlements and protections. This policy does not restate eligibility or benefit calculations that belong to the Leave & Time-Off Policy and current law.
- **Disability:** The **Rights and Protection of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2013**, including section 36, prohibits disability-related discrimination within its scope.
- **Constitutional equality:** Articles 27 and 28 of Bangladesh's Constitution concern equality and discrimination by the State. Article 29 concerns public employment. These should not be mistaken for a complete standalone private-sector recruitment rule; private-sector duties also arise under applicable labour, disability and other legislation.

Before formal approval, HR should confirm coverage, currently operative requirements, and any mandatory complaints-committee obligations under the 2026 amendment with Bangladesh employment counsel. Nothing in this policy reduces statutory, contractual or valid service-rule rights.

## Current Practice and Proposed Improvements

Blinto already has approved **Hiring, Onboarding, Performance Review and Corrective Action SOPs**. Blinto also uses **Performance Tracker software and its SOP** for evaluation criteria, career levels and salary ranges.

This is Blinto's **first standalone Equal Opportunity, Respect & Non-Discrimination Policy draft**. Employees' understanding of discrimination protections and reporting routes needs improvement. Blinto has identified inconsistent documentation of hiring criteria, unprofessional jokes or stereotypes, concern about reporting unfair treatment, and a need for clearer manager guidance.

The standards in this draft propose stronger employee communication, fair and documented selection practices, a clearer HR escalation route and more consistent management decisions. This document **does not claim** that Blinto already operates an accessibility scheme, equal-pay audit program, new protected-characteristics database or automated HRMS compliance workflow.
