# Leave Carry-Forward & Encashment Policy

## Purpose

Explain how Blinto carries unused Annual/Earned Leave (AL/EL) into the next year, allows eligible employees to receive payment for unused leave, and settles earned leave when employment ends. Blinto encourages employees to take meaningful rest rather than treating leave primarily as an additional source of income.

The Leave & Time-Off Policy governs leave entitlement, applications and ordinary leave approval. This policy explains the treatment of unused balances and payments without creating a second leave allocation.

## Eligibility and Annual Leave Balance

Only confirmed employees who have completed **at least one year of continuous employment** and have an eligible accrued and unused AL/EL balance may request voluntary annual encashment under this policy. **No employee may voluntarily encash company-granted or accrued annual leave before completing one year, including an employee who resigns before completing one year.** Employees leaving Blinto remain entitled to payment of any unused annual leave that is independently payable under applicable law or a binding employment term. Confirmation status does not remove rights that apply under law or binding employment terms, including for employees on probation.

Blinto's Leave & Time-Off Policy v1.7 uses **14 working days** as the ordinary annual planning baseline, prorated where relevant for new joiners. For eligible adult workers in a commercial establishment, section 117 of the Bangladesh Labour Act provides earned leave at **one day for every 18 qualifying days worked** after the applicable one-year continuous-service requirement. The higher applicable statutory, contractual or approved entitlement must be preserved. HR must distinguish the company's working-day allocation from the statutory leave calculation, including the treatment of intervening holidays under section 117(3).

Employees who complete probation before one year of service may use an available company-granted annual leave balance as **paid time off only** under the Leave & Time-Off Policy. **Any remaining first-year company-granted or company-recorded accrued days are not cashable under Blinto's discretionary scheme before the one-year service anniversary, including at resignation or other separation.** This rule does not cancel a legal or already-binding contractual payment entitlement. There is no duplicate allocation during probation. The separate three-day probationary casual leave allowance is not an annual leave balance and cannot be voluntarily encashed.

## Carrying Leave Forward

Eligible unused annual leave carries into the next applicable leave period. Blinto uses December to reconcile calendar-year balances, without changing statutory service-based accrual periods.

- **60 days** is the normal accumulation threshold for covered adult workers in a commercial establishment. HR must confirm the applicable classification and any higher contractual entitlement.
- Leave that an employee requested but the employer refused must be preserved above the statutory threshold where section 117(7) applies. A statutory accumulation threshold is not permission to delete leave already earned.
- Oldest eligible leave should ordinarily be used first.
- HR must reconcile existing balances before applying a ceiling or making a correction. Employees must be informed of any material adjustment.

Unused Casual Leave (CL) and Sick Leave (SL) do not carry forward under Blinto's ordinary leave rules and are not voluntarily encashable, unless a binding entitlement provides otherwise.

## Annual Encashment During Employment

After completing **one year of continuous employment**, eligible employees may submit **one voluntary encashment request per calendar year during the December annual closing process**. No company voluntary encashment is available before one year, even where a leave balance is displayed in HRMS. The request must be submitted to HR through HRMS where supported, or directly through the approved HR channel where necessary.

Blinto permits a request for up to **50% of eligible accrued and unused AL/EL**, subject to the applicable statutory ceiling. Rule 107(2) of the Bangladesh Labour Rules limits year-end encashment to half of earned leave and once a year. HR must check the applicable statutory annual entitlement as well as the unused balance; the company percentage must not be used to exceed a legal ceiling on encashing accumulated balances. Only actual eligible unused leave can be encashed. Unearned projected leave and advance leave cannot be cashed out.

For example, an employee with 14 eligible annual leave days, all unused, may request up to seven days, subject to verification. An employee with ten unused days from a 14-day annual entitlement may request up to five days under Blinto's 50% unused-balance rule. Where the legally applicable calculation requires a different limit or a greater enforceable entitlement, it prevails.

The annual company approval process must not be used to deny or delay any mandatory encashment right. Employees should plan and take leave throughout the year; encashment is not a substitute for legally required rest.

## Request and Approval

The employee submits the requested number of days to HR. The Department Head confirms any work-planning implications. HR verifies the available accrued balance, eligibility, previous encashments and applicable limits. The **Chief of Staff approves voluntary company encashment** after HR review. Finance verifies the amount and pays it with the next approved payroll, subject to any earlier mandatory payment deadline.

HR communicates the decision and updates the employee's balance. If a request is not approved, the employee should receive the relevant reason and may ask HR to review an incorrect calculation or record. Required statutory payments and final-settlement leave dues are not subject to discretionary approval.

## How Encashment Is Calculated

Blinto's company calculation uses the employee's **gross monthly salary for the immediately preceding completed payroll month, excluding overtime and bonuses**, divided by 30.

**Daily encashment rate = eligible preceding month's gross salary ÷ 30**

**Encashment payment = daily encashment rate × approved unused leave days**

Ordinary gross salary in the Compensation & Salary Administration Policy excludes separately paid overtime and bonuses. If a payroll total includes those items, subtract them once to arrive at the eligible salary basis; do not deduct them twice.

Example: An employee's preceding completed month shows total earnings of BDT 50,000, including BDT 4,000 overtime and BDT 6,000 bonus. The eligible amount is BDT 40,000. For five approved days, the company calculation is BDT 40,000 ÷ 30 × 5 = **BDT 6,666.67** before legally applicable deductions.

The 30-day basis is consistent with Blinto's internal salary calculation approach and the year-end encashment calculation described in Rule 107(2), as amended in 2022. Finance must verify the applicable last-drawn salary basis and statutory wage components. Section 119 prescribes the daily average of relevant full-time wages, dearness allowance and applicable ad hoc/interim wages for days worked in the preceding month, excluding overtime and bonuses, for statutory leave wages. Any applicable cash allowance in lieu of food grains must also be included. Where a legally required or binding contractual calculation produces a higher payable amount, pay that entitlement. A partial month, salary change or unusual payroll component requires individual review.

Approved encashed days are removed from the eligible balance once payment is processed and reconciled. A pending request should not result in duplicate use or duplicate payment of those days.

## Advance Annual Leave

Under the Leave & Time-Off Policy, employees may request advance annual leave after completing **one year of continuous employment**, subject to HR approval and Department Head coverage confirmation. Advance leave is recorded separately as a negative or projected balance and reconciled against future accrual. It is not earned leave available for cash-out.

If employment ends before the advance has been earned, HR and Finance reconcile the balance separately. No automatic salary deduction or offset is authorized. Any recovery must have a valid legal basis and comply with applicable wage-deduction rules and binding terms; the employee must receive an explanation and an opportunity to dispute it.

## Unused Leave When Employment Ends

**If employment ends before one year:** **No cash encashment is payable under Blinto's discretionary policy for unused first-year company-granted or company-recorded accrued annual leave**, even when an employee resigns before the one-year anniversary. First-year leave may be taken as paid time off where approved but does not thereby become a company cash benefit. This exclusion cannot defeat any payment required by applicable law or an already-binding employment term.

**If employment ends after one year:** On resignation, termination, retirement or other separation, Blinto pays **100% of legally or contractually payable unused earned annual leave**, including eligible carried-forward leave and current-period earned leave. HR verifies the balance at the effective separation date, taking into account leave taken, prior encashments, protected carry-forward and any corrections.

The 50% annual in-service limit does not apply to final settlement. The employee does not have to wait for December or seek discretionary Chief of Staff approval to receive a legally owed payment.

Finance calculates the payable amount using the applicable statutory leave-wage basis and any more favorable binding terms. The company in-service formula must not automatically replace the statutory separation calculation. Payment is included in the Final Settlement & Employee Dues process within applicable deadlines, including the Labour Act section 123(2) deadline where applicable. In the event of death, payable unused leave is handled with the lawful nominee or legal successor in accordance with applicable law.

## Leave Records and Questions

HRMS is the primary record where supported. HR maintains appropriately restricted records for any function not supported by HRMS, including statutory accrual reconciliation, carried-forward leave, current-period entitlement, approved leave, advance balances, encashment and payment history. Statutory leave-book requirements must be met where applicable.

At year-end, HR reconciles balances and works with Finance to confirm paid encashments. Employees may ask HR to verify a balance or correct an error; relevant supporting information should be reviewed fairly. Changes should be traceable, and sensitive payroll information must be kept confidential.

## Responsibilities

**Employees:** Plan leave, submit accurate requests, review balances and raise discrepancies promptly.

**Department Heads:** Confirm coverage and work-planning implications without overriding statutory leave rights.

**People Operations / HR:** Verify entitlement and balance, reconcile carry-forward, administer requests and communicate decisions and corrections.

**Chief of Staff:** Approve voluntary company encashment within applicable rules.

**Finance:** Verify lawful wage calculations, process approved or mandatory payments and reconcile payroll and settlement records.

## Current Practice and Implementation

This draft preserves the leave and compensation rules previously developed for Blinto and adds the selected December closing and approval process. Blinto has identified unclear carry-forward and encashment rules, inconsistent balances, unclear approval responsibility and the absence of a standalone policy. The proposed process clarifies these matters; it does not assume that HRMS already supports automated carry-forward, statutory accrual ledgers or encashment approval and payment integration.

## Legal Basis

This policy is subject to the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, as amended through November 2026, particularly sections 11 (unused leave at separation), 117 (earned leave, accumulation and employer-refused leave), 119 (leave wages), 123 (payment deadlines) and 125 (lawful deductions), and the Bangladesh Labour Rules, 2015 as amended, particularly Rules 107 and 108. The 2026 amendment to section 117 also recognizes up to 120 days of maternity leave for continuous-service purposes. Continuous-service treatment and qualifying workday accrual must be assessed separately under the applicable law.
