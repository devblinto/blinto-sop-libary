# Code of Conduct & Professional Ethics Policy

## Purpose

Blinto expects everyone to be treated with respect and to be able to trust the people they work with. This policy explains how employees should act toward colleagues, clients, visitors, and business partners, and what to do when something does not feel right.

It is meant to be practical: act honestly, communicate respectfully, keep commitments, protect information, and speak up about concerns.

## Scope

This policy applies to all Blinto employees during company work, office activities, client engagements, work-related meetings and events, and company communication channels, including remote and online work. Relevant expectations also apply when an employee represents Blinto outside the office.

Contractors, interns, and other collaborators are expected to follow applicable conduct and confidentiality requirements under their engagement terms. Nothing in this policy removes rights protected by law or an employment agreement.

## Core Principles

At Blinto, employees are expected to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat colleagues, clients, visitors, and partners with dignity.
- **Be honest.** Do not falsify records, misrepresent work, conceal material mistakes, or misuse trust.
- **Take ownership.** Meet reasonable commitments, keep colleagues informed, and raise blockers early.
- **Work fairly.** Do not discriminate, bully, harass, intimidate, threaten, or retaliate against anyone.
- **Follow lawful instructions.** Follow reasonable directions and approved company policies, and ask for clarification when needed.
- **Protect trust.** Handle company property, client information, and confidential materials responsibly.

## Respectful Workplace & Professional Behavior

Employees must not use abusive, threatening, humiliating, offensive, or discriminatory words or conduct. Bullying and harassment are unacceptable in person, in messages, during video calls, at client sites, and at company events.

Disagreements about work are normal. Discuss the issue, not the person's identity or character. Listen to others, avoid public shaming, and involve the Department Head or HR when an issue cannot be resolved respectfully.

Employees should respect colleagues' cultural and religious practices, personal dignity, and reasonable boundaries. Blinto does not impose a strict formal dress code under this policy; employees should still behave appropriately for their work and client-facing responsibilities.

## Accountability & Work Commitments

Employees should:

- understand assigned responsibilities, priorities, and deadlines;
- give accurate updates on progress and any risk of delay;
- ask for help early when they cannot meet a commitment;
- use working time and company resources for authorized work; and
- follow approved operating procedures and reasonable, lawful instructions.

A missed target or honest mistake is not automatically misconduct. Employees should explain the circumstances promptly and cooperate in finding a solution. Performance gaps are addressed under the separate [Performance Review](./performance-review.md) and [Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy](./corrective-action-and-performance-improvement-policy.md).

## Communication With Colleagues

Employees should use professional, clear, and respectful language in email, ClickUp, chats, calls, and meetings. Avoid personal attacks, hostile group-channel arguments, and embarrassing colleagues in public communications.

Work-related messages should normally be handled within reasonable working hours and according to genuine client or operational commitments. This policy does **not** create an expectation of constant availability after working hours. Approved after-hours work is governed by the [Office Hours Policy](./office-hours-policy.md) and [Overtime Policy](./overtime-policy.md).

For unresolved work disagreements, contact the Department Head. If the concern involves that person or employees feel unable to approach them, contact HR or appropriate senior leadership instead.

## Professional Conduct With Clients & Partners

When communicating or working with clients, employees must:

- provide accurate and professional information;
- protect client data, internal discussions, and confidential work;
- avoid promising prices, scope changes, deadlines, refunds, or commercial arrangements without authority;
- alert the Department Head promptly about complaints, significant delivery risks, or commitments that may not be met;
- avoid personal financial arrangements with clients connected to Blinto work; and
- use approved communication, file-sharing, and project-management tools.

Employees must not publish client work, testimonials, logos, internal messages, or project details without the required permission.

## Conflicts of Interest & Outside Work

A conflict of interest can arise when an employee's personal, financial, family, or outside-business interests could affect—or reasonably appear to affect—their decisions at Blinto.

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts to HR promptly. This includes relevant family or personal relationships affecting hiring, procurement, supervision, or commercial decisions.

Employees must **disclose outside employment, freelance work, or business activities before undertaking them** where they may conflict with Blinto responsibilities. Working for a direct competitor requires **prior written approval**. Employees must not divert Blinto clients or business opportunities, use company time or equipment for unauthorized personal commercial work, or misuse confidential information to benefit another business.

Disclosure does not automatically mean an activity is prohibited. HR and appropriate leadership will review the circumstances fairly, including contractual terms and lawful employee rights, and communicate any necessary restrictions or approval conditions.

## Gifts, Benefits & Anti-Bribery

Employees must not request, offer, give, or accept bribes, kickbacks, secret commissions, or personal benefits intended to influence a work decision.

All gifts or hospitality offered by clients, vendors, or business partners must be disclosed to HR. A small customary gift may be accepted only when it is modest, lawful, transparent, and does not create an obligation or conflict. Employees should ask HR before accepting anything that could reasonably be perceived as influencing a decision.

Employees must not promise or provide gifts or other benefits on Blinto's behalf without authorization.

## Company Property, Information & Security

Employees must use company equipment and systems primarily for approved work and take reasonable care of them. Report missing, damaged, or compromised property promptly.

Do not:

- share personal account passwords or grant unauthorized access;
- copy or transfer confidential company or client files to personal accounts or devices without authorization;
- use unapproved software or insecure methods to handle sensitive information; or
- delete, alter, conceal, or misuse company records or client materials.

Follow the [Credential & Password Management Policy](./credential-and-password-management-policy.md) and [Access Provisioning & Deprovisioning](./access-provisioning-and-deprovisioning.md) for the detailed security rules. Company property and information must be returned or handed over under [Employee Offboarding](./employee-offboarding.md).

## Workplace Environment

Everyone should help keep shared spaces clean and usable, respect other people's work, and avoid unnecessarily disruptive behavior. Employees should act with consideration during meetings, shared breaks, and company events.

Blinto supports respectful cultural and religious expression and does not allow employees to be targeted or disadvantaged because of protected personal characteristics.

## Social Media & Public Statements

Employees may share approved Blinto announcements and content responsibly. They must not disclose confidential company or client information, internal discussions, private employee details, or unpublished client work.

Only authorized representatives may make official statements on Blinto's behalf. Employees should make clear when a personal opinion is their own, not an official Blinto position. This policy must not be used to restrict lawful reporting, protected disclosures, or other legally protected expression.

## Raising a Concern

Employees can raise a conduct or ethics concern with:

1. **People Operations / HR** directly;
2. their **Department Head**, when appropriate; or
3. **senior leadership** if the concern involves the Department Head, HR, or another person who would otherwise handle it.

Employees may request a confidential conversation. Blinto should share information only with people who need it for a fair and lawful review; absolute secrecy cannot be guaranteed where investigation or legal duties require disclosure.

Where a concern involves discrimination, workplace violence, or harassment, HR must direct the employee to the **applicable legally required complaints mechanism or committee**. Employees must not be forced to raise a complaint only with the person they are complaining about.

Employees are encouraged to report in good faith, even if they do not have every detail. Knowingly fabricated allegations are different from a sincere report that cannot ultimately be substantiated.

**Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in a legitimate review is prohibited.** Employees should report suspected retaliation to HR or senior leadership.

## What Happens After a Report

Blinto will assess reported concerns fairly and as promptly as practicable, including relevant facts, employee explanations, confidentiality, and any immediate safety needs.

Employees accused of a policy breach should receive a reasonable opportunity to understand and respond to the allegation. No one is presumed responsible merely because a complaint was made.

Ordinary coaching and performance matters follow the [Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy](./corrective-action-and-performance-improvement-policy.md). Serious misconduct, harassment, violence, fraud, or security concerns may require a distinct investigation and legally prescribed safeguards. Any formal disciplinary punishment must comply with applicable law, including the required response, hearing, and inquiry process for covered workers.

Immediate, proportionate protective measures may be taken to safeguard people, clients, or systems while a matter is reviewed. A protective access restriction is not by itself a final disciplinary finding.

## Responsibilities

**Employees** follow these standards, raise concerns honestly, protect information, and cooperate with fair reviews.

**Department Heads** model appropriate behavior, make expectations clear, address concerns respectfully, and refer serious or sensitive matters to HR.

**People Operations / HR** provides a safe reporting route, guides employees on applicable processes, and coordinates fair consideration of concerns and relevant employee rights.

**Leadership** ensures serious or conflicted cases receive appropriate independent review and any employment decisions follow Blinto's authorized approval structure and the law.

## Exceptions & Employee Rights

Employees may ask HR for clarification when expectations conflict, a legitimate instruction appears unlawful, or a requested activity creates an ethical concern. No employee should be punished for declining an unlawful instruction or exercising a legally protected right.

This policy does not replace statutory protections, employment agreements, or Blinto's more specific policies. Where those instruments provide a stronger legal right or impose a more specific lawful requirement, they apply.
