# Final Settlement & Employee Dues Policy

## Purpose

Explain what an employee or eligible beneficiary can expect when employment ends: how final dues are identified, who handles the calculation, when payment is made, and how to question an amount. Blinto aims to make final settlement accurate, timely, and understandable.

## Scope

This policy applies to employment ending through resignation, employer-initiated termination, expiry of a fixed-term contract, or death during employment. Entitlements depend on the person's legal employment classification, contract, and applicable law. This policy does not replace any stronger statutory or contractual right.

## Core Principle

**Employees receive all amounts lawfully owed to them when employment ends.** Payment is not a discretionary reward for completing clearance. Blinto will explain the calculation and any lawful adjustment in an itemized statement. A disputed item or incomplete administrative handover must not automatically stop the payment of undisputed, legally due amounts.

## What May Be Included in Final Settlement

Depending on eligibility and the reason for separation, the settlement may include:

- **Earned but unpaid salary** through the effective payable separation date, including any earlier unpaid wages.
- **Unused earned Annual Leave (AL/EL)** payable on separation, including eligible carried-forward leave. All legally payable unused earned leave is considered; the annual 50% in-service encashment limit does **not** cap final settlement.
- **Approved business-expense reimbursements** not yet paid.
- **Unpaid overtime compensation** that is legally or contractually owed, including verified eligible work not yet included in payroll. A missing prior approval record does not by itself extinguish a statutory wage entitlement.
- **Bonuses or incentives** already earned and payable under the applicable contract, approved scheme, or law.
- **Statutory resignation, termination, retrenchment, discharge, death-related, or other separation compensation**, where the relevant legal conditions apply.
- **Gratuity, provident-fund benefits, or other contractual dues** where applicable, calculated without double-counting or reducing an additional entitlement required by law.

Not every item applies to every employee. HR will explain which categories apply to the particular separation.

## Salary for a Partial Month

For a partial final payroll month, Finance confirms the employee's **payable dates**, approved paid leave, unpaid absences, effective separation date, and applicable salary components.

**Blinto's standard company calculation uses a fixed 30-day salary divisor**, regardless of whether the relevant calendar month has 28, 29, 30, or 31 days:

**Daily salary rate = applicable gross monthly salary ÷ 30**

**Payable partial-month salary = daily salary rate × applicable payable days**, subject to the applicable payroll treatment and legally required protections. A full month's ordinary salary remains the contracted monthly amount; the 30-day divisor must not cause an employee to receive more or less than their contracted monthly salary simply because the month has a different number of calendar days.

**Example (illustrative only):** With a monthly gross salary of ৳30,000 and 20 payable days, the salary component is `(৳30,000 ÷ 30) × 20 = ৳20,000`. This example excludes other final-settlement components and lawful adjustments.

Where applicable law or a more favorable binding employment term requires a different wage calculation, that requirement takes precedence. HR and Finance must verify legal compliance before this proposed company rule is approved.

## Unused Annual Leave at Separation

Blinto will pay **100% of legally or contractually payable unused earned annual leave** on separation, including eligible carried-forward leave and current-year accrued leave. The employee's HRMS balance will be reviewed against accrual, prior leave, carry-forward, and any corrections before Finance calculates the amount.

**Unearned advance annual leave is not encashable.** Any negative advance-leave balance must be reviewed separately; it does not automatically authorize a deduction from earned wages.

The final leave-payment rate must follow the applicable statutory wage calculation and any more favorable binding terms. Blinto's proposed in-service annual-leave encashment reference—previous completed month's gross salary excluding overtime and bonuses, divided by 30—is **not automatically the statutory final-settlement formula**. Where the legally prescribed calculation gives a higher entitlement, the legal amount will be paid.

## Notice Period and Potential Notice-Pay Claims

The **Resignation & Notice Period Policy** explains Blinto's proposed 30-calendar-day company notice standard and requests for early release. This policy addresses only how a potential notice-pay claim appears in final settlement.

- HR records the agreed notice requirement, days served, unserved portion, and any approved early release or waiver.
- Salary for days not worked is assessed separately from a **potential notice-pay claim** for unserved notice.
- Finance and HR verify the legal basis and wage rate before any claim is included.
- A potential notice-pay claim appears as its **own line** in the settlement statement; it is not a second description of unpaid absence.
- **No automatic deduction or set-off from earned salary is permitted merely because notice was unserved.** Wage deductions are limited by applicable law. An independently enforceable claim must be pursued only through a lawful method.
- Employees may question the dates, rate, alleged unserved period, and any requested recovery.

If Blinto has waived notice pay or approved early release in writing, the settlement must reflect that waiver.

## Missing or Damaged Company Property

If property is missing or damaged, HR/Admin will explain the concern to the employee and allow a response. Any proposed recovery must be based on a legally supportable, demonstrable loss and the required fair investigation. **No automatic laptop, equipment, or incomplete-handover fine** applies under this policy. Any lawful recovery must be identified separately.

Work transfer, return of assets, and clearance are governed by the separate **Employee Offboarding SOP** and its checklist. These procedures do not replace the rules for timely payment of final dues.

## When Final Settlement Is Paid

Blinto will normally include final settlement in the **next regular payroll if that date meets all applicable legal deadlines**. If it does not, Finance must arrange an earlier or separate payment.

For workers covered by **section 30 of the Bangladesh Labour Act**, all final amounts due must be paid **within 30 working days after employment ends**. Other applicable legal or contractual deadlines that give stronger protection must also be followed.

An unresolved internal clearance item, optional exit interview, or dispute over a separate recovery claim is not by itself a reason to withhold undisputed legally due wages beyond the applicable deadline.

## How Employees Receive Their Settlement Information

HR will provide or arrange an **itemized final-settlement statement** showing, as applicable:

- the final employment date and salary period used;
- unpaid and partial-month salary;
- eligible annual-leave payout;
- applicable overtime, reimbursements, bonuses, compensation, or gratuity;
- legally permitted tax or other deductions;
- any **separately identified** notice-pay or asset-related claim and its legal treatment; and
- the net amount paid, payment date, or outstanding amount with an explanation.

Finance calculates and processes the payment and obtains approval through the authorized Finance payroll process. HR verifies employment dates, attendance, and leave data and explains the statement. The Department Head confirms handover status, and HR/Admin confirms asset status; these confirmations do not independently authorize deductions.

## Questions, Corrections and Disputes

Employees may contact People Operations/HR if they believe an attendance date, leave balance, overtime record, notice calculation, recovery, or other settlement amount is incorrect.

HR will review the explanation and supporting records with Finance, communicate any correction or reasoned decision, and provide an updated statement where needed. An employee is not required to accept an incorrect calculation to ask for undisputed amounts or a legally required service certificate. Employees retain their rights under applicable law.

## Death During Employment

If an employee dies during employment, HR will coordinate with Finance and the appropriate nominee, legal heir, or authorized representative to verify and release legally payable wages and other death-related benefits in accordance with applicable law. The company will communicate sensitively, request only necessary documents, and avoid treating ordinary employee handover requirements as conditions for payment to beneficiaries.

## Responsibilities

**Employees or eligible representatives:** Provide accurate information, raise calculation concerns promptly, and cooperate with the applicable offboarding or beneficiary-verification process.

**People Operations / HR:** Confirm relevant employment details, verify leave and attendance, communicate settlement information, coordinate questions and corrections, and protect employee records.

**Finance:** Calculate the settlement, verify lawful payments and deductions, obtain authorized payroll approval, and make payment by the applicable deadline.

**Department Head and HR/Admin:** Verify handover and company-property status under the separate Employee Offboarding SOP without independently controlling final wages.

## Exceptions & Legal Safeguards

Where the Bangladesh Labour Act, Labour Rules, an employment agreement, or another binding right provides a higher benefit or stricter payment protection, that requirement applies. Separation compensation and wage calculations differ by legal classification and reason for departure and must not be presumed from job title alone.

No rule here authorizes a fine, forfeiture, or salary deduction merely because an employee resigns, has an HRMS dispute, or has not completed administrative clearance. All proposed recoveries require lawful justification and a transparent explanation.
