# Probation, Evaluation & Confirmation Policy

## Purpose

Explain what probation means at Blinto, how new employees are evaluated, when a confirmation decision is made, and what employees can expect if improvement is needed.

Blinto aims to give every new employee clear expectations, useful feedback, and a fair, timely decision.

This policy works with [Employee Onboarding](./employee-onboarding.md) and [Performance Review](./performance-review.md). Those SOPs remain the main sources for onboarding steps and formal review methods.

## Scope

This policy applies to new employees appointed on probation under their applicable employment terms. Interns, apprentices, temporary workers, and employees in other legally distinct arrangements follow their own applicable terms and statutory classification; they must not be automatically assigned the same probation rules.

The employee's actual work and applicable Bangladesh law, not only a job title, determine any statutory worker classification. Where the law or a valid contract provides a more favorable right, that right applies.

## Standard Probation Period

Blinto's standard probation period for regular new employees is **3 months**, starting from the joining date stated in the appointment letter or employment contract, where that period is lawful for the role.

For workers covered by the Bangladesh Labour Act, the applicable statutory category may require a different period. In particular, clerical workers have a statutory probation period of 6 months, while other covered workers generally have 3 months, subject to lawful exceptions.

People Operations must confirm the appropriate classification and employment terms before recording the probation end date in HRMS. A company-wide three-month target must not override a statutory rule.

## Expectations During Probation

At onboarding, the employee should receive or be shown:

- the job description, reporting line, and main responsibilities;
- the approved probation period and expected review date;
- clear, role-relevant goals and performance measures;
- attendance, leave, conduct, and confidentiality expectations;
- the tools and support needed for the role; and
- the name of the responsible Team/Department Lead.

Employees should be given reasonable opportunities to ask questions and understand what good performance looks like.

## Probation Evaluation Criteria

The Department Head evaluates the employee against the approved role expectations and available evidence. Relevant criteria may include:

- job-specific KPI achievement;
- quality and accuracy of work;
- completion of assigned tasks and deadlines;
- technical or role-specific skills;
- problem-solving, initiative, and ownership;
- attendance and punctuality, including valid exceptions;
- teamwork and communication;
- client service and professionalism, where relevant; and
- learning ability and demonstrated improvement.

Not every criterion has the same importance for every job. Do not invent a score or penalize an employee for targets that were never communicated. Approved leave, protected rights, and authorized accommodations must be considered fairly.

Use the existing company KPI/performance framework and the [Performance Review](./performance-review.md) process; do not create a competing scoring framework in this policy.

## Feedback During Probation

Blinto currently requires a **formal final evaluation before probation ends**. The Department Head should also provide timely feedback during normal work so concerns are not saved until the last day.

Structured 30-day and 60-day check-ins are **recommended improvements**, not mandatory current company review milestones unless separately approved and introduced. People Operations may facilitate short check-ins to prevent delayed decisions.

If performance is below expectations, the Department Head should explain the issue, give concrete examples, identify available support, and document a reasonable improvement expectation.

## Final Probation Review

### Step 1 — Schedule the review

People Operations tracks the probation end date and requests the final review sufficiently early to allow the applicable decision and any required notice before that date. For an ordinary three-month probation, aim to complete the review before Day 90; use the employee's actual joining date and legal/contractual term rather than assuming every three months always contains exactly 90 days.

A missed internal review deadline does not, by itself, extend probation or suspend any statutory employment status.

### Step 2 — Gather evidence

The Department Head prepares a written evaluation using relevant work, KPI, quality, deadline, communication, and feedback records. Where appropriate, ClickUp and the company KPI tracker may support the evaluation.

### Step 3 — Discuss the evaluation

The Department Head gives the employee a chance to discuss the assessment, explain relevant circumstances, and understand any concerns. The discussion should be specific, respectful, and documented.

### Step 4 — Complete HR and leadership review

The approval workflow is:

**Department Head evaluation and recommendation → People Operations/HR verification → Chief of Staff review → CEO final decision**

HR checks the appointment terms, probation dates, actual worker classification, relevant leave and attendance exceptions, review records, and the lawful options available. The Chief of Staff reviews the recommendation. The **CEO is the final internal confirmation authority**, subject to applicable law.

### Step 5 — Communicate and record the outcome

People Operations communicates the decision in writing, issues the appropriate confirmation or other lawful notice, updates HRMS, and retains the evaluation and approval trail.

The employee may acknowledge receipt through HRMS. Acknowledgement records receipt; it does not remove legal rights or prevent the employee from raising a factual concern.

A delayed confirmation letter must not be used to postpone permanent status or other rights that arise automatically under applicable law.

## Possible Probation Outcomes

### Confirmation

If the employee meets the required standard and the employment terms support confirmation, the CEO approves the confirmation decision and HR issues a written confirmation letter and updates HRMS.

For covered workers, completion of the applicable probationary period may establish permanent status under the Labour Act even if a letter has not been issued. HR must recognize and record any such status rather than waiting for paperwork.

### Structured Improvement

If an employee needs support, the Department Head may recommend a documented improvement plan with clear goals, support, and a review date.

An improvement plan does **not automatically extend the legal probation period**. If the applicable probation period has ended, the employee's legal status and rights must be handled accordingly.

### Probation Extension

An extension may be considered only where permitted by the applicable Bangladesh law and employment terms. For covered skilled workers under section 4(8), the law permits a limited additional period where the quality of work could not be determined during the initial probation period; this must not be treated as a general right to extend probation for poor performance or administrative delay.

Before a lawful extension:

1. the Department Head and HR must record the actual reason and supporting evidence;
2. HR must verify the legal classification, permitted period, and contract;
3. the Chief of Staff reviews the recommendation;
4. the CEO gives final internal approval; and
5. HR issues written notice with the lawful new end date, expectations, support, and review arrangements before the existing probation expires where practicable.

If extension is not legally available, HR must use another lawful option and must not mislabel the employee as still on probation.

### Change of Responsibilities

A change in duties or proposed transfer requires a separate, properly authorized review. It must not be used to reset or avoid statutory probation rights. A permanent worker serving probation in a new post may have special statutory protections.

### Separation

If employment may end, HR must check the legal basis, actual worker classification, appointment letter, applicable notice and benefits, and any required process. The Department Head recommends and HR reviews; the CEO gives final internal authorization. Follow [Employee Offboarding](./employee-offboarding.md).

Poor performance, misconduct, resignation, and ordinary termination are not interchangeable legal grounds. Do not issue a termination or impose a salary deduction solely on the basis of a probation score.

## Resignation and Notice During Probation

Blinto's currently reported employment contract requires **30 days' notice**, including during probation. HR must check the exact signed contract and the applicable statutory notice requirements before using this period in any individual case.

The notice rule may differ by legal worker classification and by whether an employee is still probationary or has become permanent. The contract's 30-day wording must not be assumed to displace a more protective mandatory legal provision.

Employees should submit resignation through the approved channel. Any early release or payment in lieu of notice must be handled under the applicable contract, law, and authorized approval process.

## Leave and Benefits During Probation

**Blinto's proposed probationary Casual Leave allowance:** An employee may use **up to 3 working days of paid Casual Leave (CL) in total during probation** for an **emergency or sickness**. The allowance does not reset each month. Where approved under the Leave & Time-Off Policy, a half-day counts as **0.5 day**. The employee should inform HR and the Department Head as soon as reasonably possible and record the absence through HRMS.

**After those 3 CL days are exhausted**, additional absence will ordinarily be considered **Leave Without Pay (LWP)** **if no other paid entitlement applies**. HR will review the circumstances and any available evidence. Management may consider a discretionary paid exception or another lawful arrangement depending on the reason for leave, particularly an emergency or medical situation. The employee will be informed of the decision.

**The three-day company allowance cannot reduce statutory leave rights.** Employees on probation remain entitled to any casual leave, sick leave, or other paid benefit applicable under Bangladesh law or a stronger employment contract. For covered workers, sections 115 and 116 of the Labour Act govern statutory casual and sick leave. HR must check these rights, and any applicable medical-certification requirement, **before** classifying further leave as unpaid.

The [Leave & Time-Off Policy](./leave-and-time-off-policy.md) governs leave applications, balances, approvals and medical evidence.

- HRMS must show the actual probationary CL days taken, other available paid leave, and approved LWP.
- An employee does not receive a duplicate annual-leave allocation during probation.
- Blinto's proposed advance annual-leave benefit is not available before one year of service, subject to applicable law and stronger contractual rights.
- A lack of the company CL balance does not automatically justify denying protected leave or treating a genuine medical absence as misconduct.

HR and Finance must verify the lawful basis before any unpaid-leave payroll adjustment. Management review is not a promise of paid exception, and no blanket deduction is authorized by this policy.

## Salary Review After Confirmation

Blinto may review an employee's salary after confirmation, based on performance, the role, internal compensation arrangements, and management approval.

**Confirmation does not automatically guarantee a salary increase.** Any salary change must follow the applicable compensation approval process and be communicated in writing. A salary review must not reduce an employee's existing contractual or statutory rights.

## Records and HRMS

People Operations keeps a confidential record of:

- employee ID, role, appointment classification, and joining date;
- signed employment terms and applicable probation period;
- communicated expectations and KPI framework;
- coaching, feedback, and review evidence;
- the completed final evaluation and employee comments;
- Department Head recommendation, HR verification, Chief of Staff review, and CEO decision;
- any lawful extension notice or improvement plan;
- confirmation, separation, or other outcome notice;
- HRMS effective status, dates, and employee acknowledgement.

HRMS should generate reminders before probation ends, distinguish **probation review pending** from actual **legal employment status**, and maintain an audit trail of changes.

## Responsibilities

**Employee**

- Understand role expectations and ask for clarification where needed.
- Perform assigned work responsibly and follow applicable policies.
- Participate in evaluation discussions and provide relevant information.

**Team/Department Lead**

- Set clear initial expectations and provide timely feedback.
- Evaluate actual role performance and gather evidence.
- Recommend a fair, supported outcome before the probation period ends.

**People Operations/HR**

- Verify the lawful probation period, employment category, and contract.
- Track deadlines, coordinate evaluation, and check fairness and documentation.
- Verify leave/attendance records and lawful employment options.
- Communicate outcomes, issue approved letters, and update HRMS.

**Chief of Staff**

- Review probation recommendations and highlight operational or consistency concerns before the CEO decision.

**CEO**

- Make the final internal decision on confirmation, lawful extension, and other major probation outcomes requiring executive approval.

## Exceptions & Escalation

Contact People Operations when:

- an employee's classification or probation period is unclear;
- there is no clear initial KPI or role expectation;
- a review is delayed or probation is about to expire;
- an extension or employment separation is proposed;
- the employee disputes a material evaluation fact;
- a leave, disability, medical, discrimination, or other protected-rights issue affects the review; or
- notice or benefit calculations are uncertain.

People Operations must escalate potential legal conflicts for qualified legal review and ensure that no new rule reduces a mandatory employee right.

## Policy Administration

This document is a **draft for management and legal review**. It does not replace any approved employment term or automatically change an employee's legal status. Only the approved policy, with a confirmed effective date and employee communication, may be implemented.

Review this policy every six months or earlier if applicable law, employment terms, or company procedures change.
