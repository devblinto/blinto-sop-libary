# Office Cleanliness & Shared Facilities Policy

## Purpose

Keep Blinto's workplace clean, hygienic, organized, comfortable, and safe for everyone. Everyday courtesy and prompt reporting help preserve a productive office, while routine and specialist cleaning remain the responsibility of the designated personnel.

This policy sets expectations for individuals who use the office; it does **not** create a professional cleaning assignment for employees outside their roles, introduce new penalties, or replace the [Workplace Health, Safety & Emergency Policy](./workplace-health-safety-and-emergency-policy.md).

## Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to everyone using Blinto's workplace, including confirmed and probationary employees, interns, Department Heads and management, office support and cleaning personnel, and visitors and contractors. Hosts and responsible Blinto contacts should communicate relevant shared-facility rules to visitors.

## Everyday Workplace Expectations

Everyone should:

- keep the areas they personally use reasonably tidy;
- dispose of their waste in the appropriate places;
- treat shared furniture, equipment, supplies, and facilities with care;
- leave shared areas ready for the next person;
- avoid obstructing corridors, doors, stairs, and emergency exits; and
- report hygiene, damage, maintenance, or safety concerns promptly.

A minor issue is normally resolved through a respectful reminder or practical assistance. No one should publicly shame another person over cleanliness concerns.

## Individual Workstations

Employees should keep their desks reasonably organized and remove food packaging, personal rubbish, and used containers. Protect computers, cables, documents, and other company property from liquid spills and avoid creating tripping or electrical hazards.

Clean minor personal spills promptly **only when it is safe to do so**. Do not use unsuitable liquid cleaners or chemicals on company electronics, and do not attempt to repair electrical equipment. Where an item needs specialist handling, report it to the responsible office contact.

Before leaving for the day, leave the workstation in a reasonably tidy condition. These everyday expectations are distinct from the professional cleaning services coordinated by office administration.

## Meeting Rooms & Shared Work Areas

After using a meeting room or shared work area:

- remove personal belongings and personal waste;
- return chairs and shared equipment to their appropriate places;
- leave tables and surrounding areas reasonably clean; and
- report damaged, missing, or malfunctioning equipment.

The last user of a shared room should make a reasonable check that it is ready for the next user. Everyone must keep access routes and emergency exits clear at all times. Visitors must follow the relevant rules in the [Visitor & Workplace Access Policy](./visitor-and-workplace-access-policy.md).

## Dining Areas & Shared Kitchen Facilities

Use designated dining spaces where practical. After eating or using shared kitchen facilities:

- place used dishes and utensils in their designated location;
- dispose of food waste appropriately;
- clean up ordinary personal spills or food residue when safe;
- store personal food hygienically and avoid leaving spoiled or expired food in shared areas; and
- respect shared dining equipment and the people providing meal services.

Employees should not leave food waste or used dishes for the next person. Concerns about recurring food waste, unreturned utensils, and dining-area hygiene should be raised with office administration and addressed without blame or public criticism.

Meal eligibility, contributions, breakfast and lunch arrangements are governed by the separate [Office Meals & Refreshments Policy](./office-meals-and-refreshments-policy.md). Normal work and break times remain governed by the approved [Office Hours Policy](./office-hours-policy.md).

## Washrooms, Hygiene & Drinking Water

Everyone should leave washrooms clean and usable, dispose of waste in the proper bins, and respect other users' privacy and dignity.

Report leaking taps, blocked drains, broken fixtures, inadequate water supply, and shortages of essential hygiene supplies to HR or office administration. Do not attempt unauthorized plumbing or maintenance work.

Blinto is responsible for maintaining suitable sanitary facilities, sufficient safe drinking water, and appropriate workplace cleanliness through its authorized arrangements. Washroom cleaning and disinfection are duties for designated cleaning or suitably responsible personnel—not informal tasks imposed on unrelated employees. Workplace sanitation, ventilation, drinking water, and cleanliness must meet applicable Bangladesh Labour Act and Labour Rules requirements wherever they apply.

## Ventilation, Utilities & Shared Supplies

Use air-conditioning, fans, ventilation, lighting, water, and consumable office supplies responsibly. Follow the authorized arrangements for shared room settings, and avoid changing them in ways that unnecessarily disrupt others.

Turn off unnecessary lights or equipment when appropriate and safe, without interfering with equipment that must remain operational. Return borrowed shared equipment, do not remove office supplies or company property without permission, and report essential shortages or misuse.

Office administration should coordinate the maintenance of suitable ventilation, lighting, drinking-water facilities, waste disposal, and shared resources in accordance with actual office needs and legal requirements. This policy does not prescribe new equipment inventories or a cleaning timetable.

## Cleaning & Maintenance Responsibilities

**Designated cleaning or office support personnel** carry out routine cleaning and sanitation within their assigned duties. Specialist, hazardous, or professional cleaning must be handled by appropriately competent or trained personnel.

**Office administration** coordinates housekeeping, cleaning arrangements, sanitation supplies, and facilities maintenance; checks reported concerns; and arranges appropriate follow-up. This does not assume Blinto has a dedicated maintenance team or particular cleaning staffing model.

**Employees and other users** are responsible for their own ordinary waste, reasonable tidiness, and safe minor spill cleanup—not professional cleaning outside their assigned roles.

**Department Heads and Team Leads** reinforce shared-space expectations, help resolve recurring team issues, and escalate unresolved operational or safety concerns.

**HR / People Operations** receives employee concerns, supports fair handling and accommodation needs, and coordinates with office administration when concerns affect workplace welfare or employee conduct.

## Reporting Maintenance, Hygiene & Safety Concerns

For ordinary issues—such as a damaged chair, shortage of supplies, untidy shared area, water leak, or broken washroom fixture—inform HR or office administration with enough detail to identify the problem. The receiving person should route it to the appropriate responsible contact and follow up as needed.

For electrical hazards, a fire risk, significant water leakage, an unsafe fixture, or another immediate danger:

1. Keep yourself and others away from the hazard when safe to do so.
2. Notify the responsible office contact or Department Head **immediately**.
3. Do not attempt unauthorized electrical, technical, or hazardous repairs.
4. Restrict access to the unsafe area when reasonably necessary and safe.
5. Follow the [Workplace Health, Safety & Emergency Policy](./workplace-health-safety-and-emergency-policy.md) and emergency instructions.

Good-faith hygiene or safety reporting is encouraged. Employees should not be penalized simply for raising a genuine concern.

## Respectful Handling of Repeated Problems

Where someone repeatedly leaves food waste, fails to return utensils, misuses shared resources, or ignores reasonable facility rules, the usual response should begin with a private, respectful explanation and a practical opportunity to improve.

Department Heads may address repeated team concerns with HR. Deliberate or persistent misconduct may be considered under the approved [Corrective Action & Performance Improvement Policy](./corrective-action-and-performance-improvement-policy.md), following fair review and an opportunity for the person to respond. A hygiene complaint is not, by itself, proof of misconduct.

Blinto does **not** introduce informal fines, automatic salary deductions, public shaming, or a new attendance penalty through this policy. Relevant disability, health-related needs, pregnancy-related needs, and reasonable accommodations should be considered respectfully and privately under applicable company policy and law.

## Responsibilities at a Glance

| Role | Main responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| All workplace users | Keep personally used spaces tidy, dispose of waste properly, respect others, and report concerns |
| Designated cleaning/support personnel | Perform assigned routine and professional cleaning and sanitation |
| Office administration | Coordinate housekeeping, supplies, maintenance, and suitable facility conditions |
| Department Heads / Team Leads | Reinforce expectations and escalate persistent or operational issues |
| HR / People Operations | Handle employee welfare concerns, accommodations, and fair employee-relations review |
| Authorized specialist / repair provider | Carry out repairs or hazardous work requiring appropriate competence |
